# The NFC Least Is Back

The NFC East went 0-3-1 in Week 5. That has happened just four times since the 2002 divisional realignment. The Giants are in first place at 3-1-1. Let that sit.

Washington had a chance to walk off the Giants in overtime. Dan Quinn's staff decided to run the clock out instead. Not kick it. Run it out. In overtime. A tie.

Jayden Daniels took the blame postgame, which is noble but also not his job — the coaching staff forgot that overtime ends when the clock hits zero whether you've scored or not. The Commanders are 1-3-1.

Over in London, the Eagles were up 10 late on a Jaguars team that hadn't beaten them in decades. Then they weren't. Jacksonville 19, Philadelphia 17. Nick Sirianni took full responsibility, which — same deal as Daniels — is admirable and also beside the point when your offense can't close out a two-score lead in the fourth quarter.

The reigning NFC champs from two years ago are 2-3 and sitting second in the division on tiebreaker. Their window isn't shut, but you can hear it rattling.

And the Cowboys lost too, because of course they did.

Now look at the leaderboard. The Giants are 3-1-1 and in sole possession of first place in the NFC East. A team with a tie on its record. Leading the division. Through five weeks.

The 2020 NFC East sent a 7-9 Washington team to the playoffs and that team hosted a wild card game. This year's winner is on pace to do worse.

David Bailey Finally Arrives

David Bailey — the No. 2 overall pick in the 2025 draft — had three sacks against the Browns in a 17-12 Jets win. Best game of his young career. The critics who've been circling since draft night can take the week off.

Jets are 2-3. Not good. But a No. 2 pick looking like a No. 2 pick is the kind of thing that keeps a rebuild honest.

The Brewers Stun the Dodgers

Milwaukee 2, Los Angeles 1. NLCS Game 1 goes to the Brewers — their first playoff win over the Dodgers in years. Milwaukee was swept by LA in last year's NLCS. They remembered.

Brice Turang took Tarik Skubal — the reigning Cy Young, the Dodgers' deadline prize — deep to tie the game. That's the pitcher who was supposed to lock this series down. Instead he gave up the swing that cracked it open.

Jacob Misiorowski got called for a balk that genuinely was a balk — his front knee buckled before his back foot disengaged. The Brewers won anyway. Milwaukee has home-field against the defending champs. They just took Game 1.

Tua Strikes Again in Atlanta

On January 8, 2018, Tua Tagovailoa entered the national championship game at Mercedes-Benz Stadium as a backup quarterback and crushed the hearts of Georgia fans with one throw.

On October 11, 2026, Tua Tagovailoa entered an NFL game at Mercedes-Benz Stadium as a backup quarterback and crushed the hearts of Georgia fans with one throw.

Michael Penix Jr. hurt his ribs late in regulation. Tua came in for overtime. Threw a pick. Ravens won 30-23.

Penix says he'll be back Week 6. He had better be.
