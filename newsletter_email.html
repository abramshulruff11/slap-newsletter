Rays One Win From the World Series

Tampa Bay 3, Cleveland 1. The Rays lead the ALCS 3-2. Monday night at Tropicana Field, they can clinch.

Drew Rasmussen keeps dragging this team to the brink of history. Seven innings, four hits, one earned run against a Cleveland lineup that had to score to stay alive. His postseason has turned into a Rays origin story — pitching staff carries bats, bats scrape together just enough, win the game anyway.

The offense did what the offense does. Liam Hicks dropped a two-RBI single in the 5th to put the Rays up 2-0. Chandler Simpson came around on a Ben Williamson single to make it 3-0. Three runs. That was the game plan. That was the whole game plan.

- Hicks: 2-RBI single, 5th inning
- Simpson: scored on Williamson single, 5th
- Final offensive line: three runs, one quietly perfect night of situational hitting

Bottom of the 8th. Jose Ramirez — the one guy on Cleveland's roster who can tie a game with one swing — walking to the plate as the tying run. Tyler Wells in. Two outs.

Packers 23, Cowboys 13 — Dallas Hits the Panic Button

Green Bay beat Dallas 23-13 on Sunday Night Football. The Cowboys are now 2-4. Last time they started this slow? 2020.

Jordan Love: 23-of-41, 288 total yards, two total touchdowns, one pick. Matthew Golden caught six balls for 104 yards and a score — his second straight game over 100 receiving yards. Love is quietly figuring it out and the Packers are 4-2.

Three roughing-the-passer penalties on Dak Prescott, including one that wiped out a Carrington Valentine interception. Packers Twitter and Cowboys Twitter actually agreed on something for one night: this was the loudest whistle of the season.

Dallas has now lost to Green Bay for the first time since November 2022.

Ben Simmons Tears His Achilles Before the Season Starts

Ben Simmons tore his right Achilles at Kings practice on Sunday. Season over before it started.

He signed a one-year, $3.5 million deal with Sacramento last month after going unsigned last year. He was expected to start Wednesday against the Clippers. He averaged 10.5 points, 4.5 rebounds and 4.0 assists across two preseason games. He was moving. He was attacking. The buzz coming out of Sacramento was overwhelmingly positive.

Then a live drill on a Sunday in October took it all back.

Yahoo noted this came after ESPN had announced a documentary on his NBA comeback — just hours before the practice. Former No. 1 overall pick. Three-time All-Star. Rookie of the Year. Sat out an entire season rehabbing his back. Finally gets back on an NBA floor. Gets a documentary green-lit. Tears his Achilles two days before opening night.

Thunder Lock Up Cason Wallace for $139M

Oklahoma City signed Cason Wallace to a five-year, $139 million rookie-scale extension. The Thunder just added another long-term contract to a roster that already has SGA, Jalen Williams and Chet Holmgren on the books.

The Thunder spent the entire offseason trying to shed payroll and duck under the second apron. Then they handed Wallace $139 million. The math is going to be a problem next summer. That's a next-summer problem.
