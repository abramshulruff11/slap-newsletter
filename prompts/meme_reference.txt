## MEME REFERENCE — When to Use Memes vs. GIFs

GIFs are REACTIONS — short animations that convey an emotion. They say "this is how I feel."
Memes are ARGUMENTS — static images with text overlays that make a specific point. They say "this is what happened, and here's why it's funny."

Use a GIF when you want to show HOW someone feels.
Use a meme when you want to make a COMPARISON, a TAKE, or a VISUAL JOKE with custom text.

A section can have both. A meme makes the argument, a GIF delivers the emotional punchline.

================================================================================
HOW TO SPECIFY MEMES IN THE NEWSLETTER
================================================================================

There are TWO types of meme placements:

### TYPE 1: GENERATED MEME (evergreen template + custom text)
Use when a classic meme format fits and you want to write custom captions.

Output this exact HTML — the pipeline parses data attributes to call the Imgflip API:

<div class="meme-placeholder" data-template="TEMPLATE_SLUG" data-boxes="PANEL 1||PANEL 2"></div>

Separate panels with `||`. Write one caption per panel in order (left→right, top→bottom).

PANEL COUNTS — how many captions to write per template:

Write EXACTLY the number of captions listed. Imgflip renders any box you do
not supply as BLANK — it does not fail, it just ships a meme with an empty
panel. Corrected 2026-08-25 against Imgflip's live template data; the previous
table under-counted 14 templates and was the cause of blank-panel memes.

| Captions | Templates |
|----------|-----------|
| 2 captions | drake, this-is-fine, change-my-mind, hide-the-pain-harold, bernie-i-am-once-again-asking, always-has-been, monkey-puppet, mocking-spongebob, first-time, waiting-skeleton, one-does-not-simply, spider-man-pointing-at-spider-man, woman-yelling-at-cat, ight-imma-head-out |
| 3 captions | distracted-boyfriend, left-exit-12-off-ramp, two-buttons, corporates-want-you-to-find-the-difference, epic-handshake, surprised-pikachu, trade-offer, eric-andre-shooting, is-this-a-pigeon, panik-kalm-panik |
| 4 captions | expanding-brain, vince-mcmahon-reaction, clown-applying-makeup, buff-doge-vs-cheems |
| 3 captions → 4 physical panels | gru-plan (code repeats the flaw as panel 4) |
| 2 captions → 3 physical panels | anakin-padme (code repeats Padme's question as the final panel) |

- **two-buttons takes THREE captions, not two.** Panel 1 and 2 are the two conflicting
  buttons; panel 3 is the SUBJECT — the sweating figure who has to choose. Leaving panel 3
  off means nobody is shown agonizing and the dilemma reads as an abstract pair of options.
- **distracted-boyfriend takes THREE captions, not two.** Panel 1 = the established thing being
  left behind, panel 2 = the new thing catching their eye, panel 3 = the SUBJECT (the figure in
  the middle — the person or team making the decision). Leaving panel 3 off strands the joke:
  without a named subject it reads as an abstract comparison instead of someone caught in the act.
- data-template must be one of the slugs listed in the template library below
- Keep text short — 5-10 words max per panel
- ALL CAPS is standard for meme text (applied automatically by the pipeline)
- No quotation marks inside attribute values — use apostrophes if needed
- For mocking-spongebob: write bottom text normally, the pipeline auto-applies alternating caps

Example (2-panel):
<div class="meme-placeholder" data-template="drake" data-boxes="Watching the game on ESPN||Reading about it in SLAP the next day"></div>

Example (3-caption gru-plan):
<div class="meme-placeholder" data-template="gru-plan" data-boxes="SIGN THE STAR PLAYER||WIN THE CHAMPIONSHIP||WAIT HE WANTS A MAX EXTENSION"></div>

### TYPE 2: FOUND MEME (trending meme from a tweet or web search)
Use when a specific meme is circulating about a current event (Tiger Woods crash memes, draft day memes, etc.) and you found it during research.

```html
<div class="meme-placeholder">
MEME: [Description of the meme] | Source: [URL to the image]
</div>
```

Example:
<div class="meme-placeholder">
MEME: Tiger Woods car with "me trying to drive home after watching my team lose" caption | Source: https://pbs.twimg.com/media/xxxxx.jpg
</div>

### SETUP LINES FOR MEMES

Same rule as GIFs — every meme needs a setup line. But meme setup lines are often shorter because the meme itself contains text that does more work.

GOOD:
<p>Every NBA fan watching Cooper Flagg right now:</p>
MEME: Galaxy Brain | panels about increasingly wild Flagg comparisons

BAD:
MEME: Galaxy Brain | panels about Flagg comparisons
(No setup line — the meme just floats.)


================================================================================
MEME TEMPLATES BY JOKE TYPE
================================================================================

### COMPARISON / PREFERENCE — "This, not that"
Use when someone chose one thing over another, or when two things should be compared.

- **Drake Hotline Bling** (slug: `drake`, imgflip: 181913649) — Top: thing being rejected. Bottom: thing being chosen. Classic, works for anything.
- **Distracted Boyfriend** (slug: `distracted-boyfriend`, imgflip: 112126428) — Three-way dynamic. Write 3 captions: `data-boxes="CURRENT COMMITMENT||THE ONE LOOKING||NEW TEMPTATION"`. Box 2 (the guy) is often omitted by mistake — leave it out and Imgflip renders the RIGHT-side woman (the actual new temptation, the joke's punchline) with no caption at all.
- **Left Exit 12 Off Ramp** (slug: `left-exit-12-off-ramp`, imgflip: 124822590) — Car swerving off highway. Write 3 captions: `data-boxes="WHO'S DRIVING||THE SMART CHOICE (highway)||THE DUMB CHOICE (exit)"`.
- **Two Buttons** (slug: `two-buttons`, imgflip: 87743020) — Sweating guy agonizing over two buttons. Write 3 captions, NOT 2: `data-boxes="BUTTON 1||BUTTON 2||WHO IS SWEATING"`. The third box labels the person facing the dilemma — leave it out and the meme never says who is agonizing, which is the joke. Use when a team/player faces an impossible dilemma.
- **Corporate Needs You to Find the Difference** (slug: `corporates-want-you-to-find-the-difference`, imgflip: 180190441) — "They're the same picture." Write 3 captions: `data-boxes="THE SETUP LINE||THING ONE||THING TWO"` — box 0 is the corporate-speak intro line, boxes 1-2 are the two 'different' things.
- **Buff Doge vs. Cheems** (slug: `buff-doge-vs-cheems`, imgflip: 247375501) — Strong/intimidating version vs. weak/pathetic version. Write 4 captions: `data-boxes="STRONG LABEL||STRONG CAPTION||WEAK LABEL||WEAK CAPTION"` — this template has a title line AND a caption line per side (4 boxes total, not 2). Use for then vs. now, or two teams at opposite ends.
- **Epic Handshake** (slug: `epic-handshake`, imgflip: 135256802) — Two things unexpectedly agreeing. Write 3 captions: `data-boxes="PARTY ONE||PARTY TWO||SHARED TRAIT"` — the third box labels what they secretly share.

### ESCALATION / LEVELS — "It keeps getting more extreme"
Use when a stat line, situation, or take escalates through multiple levels.

- **Galaxy Brain / Expanding Brain** (slug: `expanding-brain`, imgflip: 93895088) — 4 panels of increasingly "enlightened" takes. Write 4 captions: `data-boxes="TAKE 1||TAKE 2||TAKE 3||TAKE 4"` — panel 4 is the galaxy-brain payoff and is NOT auto-filled. Perfect for devolving takes or increasingly wild stat comparisons.
- **Vince McMahon Reaction** (slug: `vince-mcmahon-reaction`, imgflip: 193966043) — 4 panels of increasing excitement. Write 4 captions: `data-boxes="PANEL 1||PANEL 2||PANEL 3||PANEL 4"`. Use when you want custom text escalating across panels.
- **Gru's Plan** (slug: `gru-plan`, imgflip: 131940431) — 4 panels: step 1 → step 2 → the flaw → stare at the flaw. Write 3 captions: `data-boxes="STEP 1||STEP 2||THE FLAW"` (code repeats the flaw as panel 4). Use when a team's plan has an obvious problem they didn't see coming.
- **Clown Applying Makeup** (slug: `clown-applying-makeup`, imgflip: 195515965) — Progressive clown makeup. Write 4 captions: `data-boxes="STEP 1||STEP 2||STEP 3||STEP 4"`. Each panel = a step deeper into delusion. Perfect for fans who keep defending a bad team/player.

### DENIAL / COPIUM — "Refusing to accept reality"

- **This Is Fine Dog** (slug: `this-is-fine`, imgflip: 55311130) — The meme version lets you add custom text to label WHAT is on fire. Use when a franchise is clearly in crisis but projecting calm.
- **Clown Applying Makeup** (slug: `clown-applying-makeup`, imgflip: 178591752) — (see Escalation above, also works here)
- **Change My Mind** (slug: `change-my-mind`, imgflip: 129242436) — Crowder sitting at table with a sign. Use for bold/provocative takes.
- **Hide the Pain Harold** (slug: `hide-the-pain-harold`, imgflip: 27865) — Smiling through obvious pain. Use for fans/players pretending everything is fine when it clearly isn't.
- **Anakin Padme** (slug: `anakin-padme`, imgflip: 322841258) — 4-panel. Anakin says something ominous, Padme hopefully asks if he means the good version, Anakin stares blankly. Write 2 captions: `data-boxes="ANAKIN'S OMINOUS LINE||PADME'S HOPEFUL QUESTION"` (code fills the blank/repeat panels). Use for teams in denial about a bad decision.
- **Bernie I Am Once Again Asking** (slug: `bernie-i-am-once-again-asking`, imgflip: 382370190) — Use for recurring requests/situations. "[Team] is once again asking [player] to carry them."

### REACTION / SURPRISE — "Wait, what?"

- **Surprised Pikachu** (slug: `surprised-pikachu`, imgflip: 155067746) — When the obvious outcome happens and everyone acts shocked. Write 3 captions: `data-boxes="WHO||WHAT THEY DID||THE PREDICTABLE RESULT"`.
- **Wait, It's All X? Always Has Been** (slug: `always-has-been`, imgflip: 252600902) — Astronaut meme. Use when something that seems new was actually always the case.
- **Monkey Puppet Side-Eye** (slug: `monkey-puppet`, imgflip: 148909805) — Awkward glance. When someone is caught or called out.
- **Mocking Spongebob** (slug: `mocking-spongebob`, imgflip: 102156234) — When a bad excuse or dumb take deserves direct mockery. `data-boxes="THE STRAIGHT VERSION||THE PHRASE REPEATED"`. The pipeline auto-applies alternating caps to the bottom panel — write it normally.
- **First Time?** (slug: `first-time`, imgflip: 277489984) — Parachute instructor meme. Use for recurring disasters where a fan/player acts surprised at something that always happens. `data-boxes="THE SETUP QUOTE (person/thing freaking out)||FIRST TIME?"` — the bottom panel is always the instructor's line.

### DOMINATION / SUPERIORITY — "One thing destroying another"

- **Trade Offer** (slug: `trade-offer`, imgflip: 309868304) — "I receive: [good thing]. You receive: [bad thing]." Write 3 captions: `data-boxes="WHO'S OFFERING||WHAT THEY RECEIVE||WHAT YOU RECEIVE"`. Perfect for lopsided trades.
- **Panik / Kalm / Panik** (slug: `panik-kalm-panik`, imgflip: 226297822) — THREE panels of emotional whiplash. `data-boxes="PANIK TEXT||KALM TEXT||PANIK TEXT"` — first shock, false relief, final panic. Good for game recaps with momentum swings.
- **Waiting Skeleton** (slug: `waiting-skeleton`, imgflip: 4087833) — When a franchise or fan base has been waiting for something so long it's become a joke.
- **One Does Not Simply** (slug: `one-does-not-simply`, imgflip: 61579) — Boromir from LOTR. "One does not simply [impossible or absurd thing]." Use for situations that seem easy but always end in disaster for a specific team or player.

### BETRAYAL / SELF-DESTRUCTION — "You left me" / "They did this to themselves"

- **Spider-Man Pointing at Spider-Man** (slug: `spider-man-pointing-at-spider-man`, imgflip: 119215120) — When two things/people are identical but acting like they're different.
- **Eric Andre Shooting Hannibal** (slug: `eric-andre-shooting`, imgflip: 97984) — "Why would [someone else] do this?" Write 3 captions: `data-boxes="WHO PULLED THE TRIGGER||THE ACT OF SELF-SABOTAGE||THE FEIGNED CONFUSION LINE"`. Shoots own franchise then blames others.
- **Is This a Pigeon?** (slug: `is-this-a-pigeon`, imgflip: 100777631) — Write 3 captions: `data-boxes="WHO IS MISIDENTIFYING||THE THING THEY SEE||IS THIS [WRONG LABEL]?"`. Box 3 is the bottom question line — omit it and the punchline is blank.
- **Woman Yelling at Cat** (slug: `woman-yelling-at-cat`, imgflip: 188390779) — Two-panel argument. Left panel: someone making an emotional accusation. Right panel: the calm/unbothered response. Perfect for fan vs. reality, media vs. player, or team vs. opponent dynamics.

### RESIGNATION / WALKING AWAY

- **Ight Imma Head Out** (slug: `ight-imma-head-out`, imgflip: 378389) — Spongebob getting up to leave. Use when a player, coach, or franchise is clearly done and walking away from a situation.


================================================================================
TRENDING MEMES — HOW TO FIND AND USE THEM
================================================================================

Trending memes are reactive to current events. They appear on Twitter as image tweets, usually within 24-48 hours of a big moment. You CANNOT generate these from templates — they're custom images the internet creates.

### WHERE THEY COME FROM:
- Image attachments on tweets from comedy accounts (HaterReport, NBAMemes, BallsackSports, NOTSportsCenter)
- Reddit sports subreddits (r/nba, r/nfl, r/cfb)
- Instagram meme accounts

### HOW TO USE THEM IN THE PIPELINE:
During Pass 1 research, if a major story has spawned memes (you'll see them in the tweet feed as image tweets with high engagement), note the best ones in research_notes with descriptions. The writer can then either:
1. Embed the tweet that CONTAINS the meme (easiest — the meme comes along as part of the tweet embed)
2. Reference the meme image directly if available as a standalone URL

### WHEN TRENDING MEMES MATTER MOST:
- Major injuries with visual moments (Tiger Woods crash, player collapsing)
- Draft day (prospect memes are always gold)
- Trade deadline (photoshopped jerseys, "goodbye" edits)
- Playoff eliminations (crying Jordan, faded flair)
- Historic stat lines (custom comparison graphics)
- Coaching hires/fires (Welcome/goodbye edits)

### RULE: Embed the tweet when possible.
If a great meme is attached to a tweet from one of your source accounts, just embed the tweet. The meme image renders as part of the embed. Don't try to extract and re-host the image — embed the tweet and let the platform do the work.


================================================================================
MEME vs. GIF DECISION TREE
================================================================================

Ask yourself:

1. Am I making a COMPARISON between two things? → MEME (Drake, Distracted Boyfriend, Trade Offer)
2. Am I showing ESCALATION with custom text at each level? → MEME (Galaxy Brain, Clown Makeup)
3. Am I reacting to something with pure EMOTION? → GIF (Vince McMahon, blinking guy, MJ crying)
4. Am I showing a player/team's CONTRADICTION? → MEME (Spider-Man pointing, Eric Andre)
5. Am I making a TAKE that needs a visual? → MEME (Change My Mind, Is This a Pigeon)
6. Did the internet already make a SPECIFIC meme about this story? → FOUND MEME (embed the tweet)
7. Am I just punctuating a moment with a reaction? → GIF

When in doubt: if you need custom text on the image to make the joke work, it's a meme. If the image/animation alone delivers the punchline, it's a GIF.


================================================================================
ROTATION & QUALITY RULES
================================================================================

1. Max 2-3 generated memes per issue. Don't overdo it — memes are heavier than GIFs. Too many and the newsletter feels like a meme page, not a newsletter.
2. Drake Hotline Bling: max 1x per week. It's the most overused meme format on the internet.
3. Surprised Pikachu: max 1x per week. Same reason.
4. If a trending meme is circulating about today's story, USE IT over a generated template. Trending > evergreen. The reader has probably already seen the trending meme and will appreciate the recognition.
5. Every meme must pass the same punchline test as GIFs: can you explain the joke in one sentence? If not, pick a different format.
6. Generated meme captions should be SHORT. Top text: 5-10 words max. Bottom text: 5-10 words max. If you need a paragraph to set up the meme, the meme isn't working.
