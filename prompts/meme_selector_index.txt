## subject_abandons_sensible_for_funky
   A named subject faces an obviously correct option and visibly goes with the worse, pettier, or stranger one.
   TEST: The choice is ALREADY MADE and public. We are reacting to it, not predicting it.
   - drake (2 boxes, subject: copy) -- Subject already chose the petty option over the obvious one. Needs a named subject in the copy.
   - distracted-boyfriend (3 boxes, subject: box:2) -- Subject publicly moves on from an incumbent toward a new option. Subject goes IN box 2.
   - left-exit-12-off-ramp (2 boxes, subject: copy) -- Abrupt last-second swerve off an established plan. Use over drake when suddenness is the story.

## forced_choice_dilemma
   A subject frozen between two options of the SAME valence -- both appealing, or both painful -- that cannot coexist.
   TEST: The choice is NOT yet made -- the subject is still sweating. If it is made, use subject_abandons_sensible_for_funky.
   - two-buttons (3 boxes, subject: box:2) -- Subject still sweating between two SAME-valence options, choice not yet made. Subject goes IN box 2.
