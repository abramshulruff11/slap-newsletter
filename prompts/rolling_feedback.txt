## ROLLING FEEDBACK — Hard rules extracted from real output failures.
## Max 3 rules added per issue review session. Apply every rule, every time.
## These override general guidance in base_prompt.txt when in conflict.

================================================================================
RULE 1 — ACCOUNT HARD CAP
================================================================================
No single Twitter account may appear more than 2 times in a complete issue.
Count every tweet blockquote by @handle before finalizing. If any account
exceeds 2 appearances, replace the excess with tweets from accounts that
haven't appeared yet. This is a structural requirement, not a suggestion.

================================================================================
RULE 2 — NO REPEAT MEDIA FROM PREVIOUS ISSUE
================================================================================
No GIF or Meme that appeared in the previous issue may be reused in the
current issue. Before selecting any GIF or Meme, check recent_output.json
for the previous issue's media. If a GIF URL or Meme concept matches anything
in that file, choose a different one. This rule applies to memes even if the
full meme pipeline is not yet active — if the meme concept matches the
previous issue, pick a different meme.

================================================================================
RULE 3 — VERIFY ALL FACTS BEFORE PUBLISHING
================================================================================
Every factual claim must be verified as accurate for today's date before it
appears in the newsletter. Do not rely on training knowledge alone. This
includes — but is not limited to — player team affiliations, roster moves,
conference and division placement, championship and record claims, career and
season statistics, game outcomes, and coaching staff. Players get traded.
Rosters change. Records fall. If a claim feels uncertain, run a web search to
confirm it. One wrong fact destroys reader trust faster than any voice failure.
