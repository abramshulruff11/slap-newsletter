Brewers One Win From the World Series

Milwaukee beat Los Angeles 6-2 in Game 3. The Brewers lead the NLCS 3-0 and can sweep the defending World Series champions Thursday night in Milwaukee.

First pitch from Blake Snell to Jackson Chourio in the top of the first. Chourio's sixth postseason home run. Most postseason home runs by a player before turning 23. Past Kyle Schwarber. Past Juan Soto. Twenty-two years old.

Los Angeles clawed back. Will Smith doubled off the wall in the 6th to give the Dodgers a 2-1 lead. For about 20 minutes, the sweep talk quieted down and this looked like a real baseball game.

Then Snell came out for the 7th.

Jake Bauers worked the at-bat of the series. Eleven pitches. Foul after foul after foul. On pitch 11, he launched the go-ahead two-run shot. Garrett Mitchell walked up next and went back-to-back.

Snell before the inning: ten straight starts without allowing a homer. Snell during the inning: three homers in four batters.

Jake Bauers has been public enemy number one at Dodger Stadium all series. This was the dagger.

Robert Gasser also went five scoreless. Joey Ortiz tacked on. Brice Turang scored on a wild pitch. The Dodgers never caught their breath after the 7th.
