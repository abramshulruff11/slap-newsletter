Manny Fernandez Dies at 79

Manny Fernandez, a two-time Super Bowl-winning defensive lineman with the Miami Dolphins, has died. He was 79.

@AdamSchefter
Manny Fernandez, an anchor of the Miami Dolphins' legendary No-Name Defense, has died, the team announced Tuesday. He was 79. https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/48881180/manny-fernandez-star-1972-undefeated-dolphins-dies-79
View tweet

Fernandez played his entire eight-year NFL career in Miami and was an anchor for the famed "No-Name Defense" that led the Dolphins to three consecutive Super Bowl appearances, including back-to-back victories in 1972 and 1973. He played a crucial role in Super Bowl VII, when Miami beat Washington to finish 17-0 and complete the only undefeated season in NFL history.

Astros Throw Combined No-Hitter

Tatsuya Imai (six innings), Steven Okert (one), Alimber Santa (two) combined to throw the first no-hitter since Sept. 4, 2024, in a 9-0 win over the Rangers.
