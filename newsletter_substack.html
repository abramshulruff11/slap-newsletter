Wemby Puts Spurs Up 2-1 With Fourth-Quarter Takeover in Minnesota

The Spurs beat the Timberwolves 115-108 in Game 3 to take a 2-1 series lead. Victor Wembanyama scored a playoff career-high 39 points, along with 15 rebounds and five blocks.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2052969146669838348

Only Hakeem Olajuwon, Shaquille O'Neal and Kareem Abdul-Jabbar had hit those thresholds in a postseason game before Wembanyama. Wembanyama got there quicker than any of them. This is his seventh playoff game. Olajuwon needed nine to post a similar line.

The fourth quarter is when Wembanyama completely took over.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2052969467265655286

16 of his 39 in the fourth. Seven of his team's nine in a late clutch stretch. He picked up his fifth foul with 6:18 left, stayed in the game, and hit the three that answered Naz Reid and pushed the lead to six with 3:06 to go.

Guardians Acquire Gold Glove Catcher Patrick Bailey

https://twitter.com/JeffPassan/status/2053129093122506914

Two-time Gold Glove winner Patrick Bailey to Cleveland for lefty pitching prospect Matt Wilkinson and their Competitive Balance Round A pick (No. 29).

https://twitter.com/JeffPassan/status/2053131596241236051

Bailey is perhaps the best defensive catcher in baseball. He's hitting .146/.213/.183 this year with 12 hits and one extra-base hit in 89 plate appearances. Career .224/.282/.329 hitter.

The Giants needed offense. Bailey wasn't providing it. Cleveland has a pitching staff that can benefit from elite framing and game-calling. The Guardians are betting that Bailey's glove is worth more than his bat.
