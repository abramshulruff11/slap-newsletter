NBA Is Back — Opening Night Delivered Two Overtimes, Three Blowouts and a West Coast Upset

Twelve games. Two overtimes. Three blowouts. The 2026-27 season tipped off.

Celtics 125, Sixers 120 in overtime. Bucks 128, Pacers 127 in overtime. Both Eastern Conference matchups bled into extra time on night one.

Then came the blowouts. Chicago dropped 133 on Minnesota and won by 17. Orlando dropped 136 on Miami and won by 21. San Antonio put up 126 on Sacramento and won by 29.

The Bulls haven't had a winning season since 2021-22. One game is one game, but telling the Timberwolves — a team that has made real noise the last couple of years — to go home down 17 is not nothing.

Out West, Utah walked into Phoenix and left with a 119-116 win. The Suns haven't been back to the Finals since 2020-21. They just lost at home to a Jazz team that hasn't had a winning season since 2021-22.

Denver 121, Lakers 106. The Nuggets rolled into Crypto.com Arena and reminded everyone what the Lakers look like after losing their franchise player.

Across the full slate: the Pistons edged Toronto 129-125, Golden State handled Portland 128-118, New Orleans squeaked past Washington 107-106, and Memphis beat Charlotte 117-98. Twelve games, zero duds.

Bruins Finally Beat the Ducks in Anaheim Again

Boston 4, Anaheim 2. First Bruins win over the Ducks since October 2023. First Bruins win AT Anaheim since October 2023. Same date, three years apart, finally a different outcome.

Boston trailed 2-0 after one and scored three times in the third to flip it. Elias Lindholm set up the tying goal and scored the go-ahead. David Pastrnak had the equalizer on the power play. Fourth straight win for the Bruins, now 6-2-0.

Also on the NHL slate: Colorado 5, Pittsburgh 2. Avs rolled, Crosby's Penguins did not.

CFB Friday — Washington's Backup QB Walks Into Purdue and Walks Out With a W

Washington 38, Purdue 26. On the road. Without starting quarterback Demond Williams Jr., who sat with a rib injury. Backup Elijah Brown got the start and the Huskies covered 12 points anyway.

Tulane handled Memphis 31-17 at home. App State escaped Coastal Carolina 35-32 on the road in a game that stayed within one score the whole way.

ALCS Game 4 Washed Out — Brewers Just Sitting There

Rain postponed ALCS Game 4 between the Rays and Guardians to Saturday. The AL series stretches on. Meanwhile, in Milwaukee, the Brewers — who swept the Dodgers in the NLCS — are sitting at home watching the forecast.

Milwaukee hasn't been to a World Series since 1982. They finally punched the ticket and now the weather is making them wait on the AL to figure itself out.
