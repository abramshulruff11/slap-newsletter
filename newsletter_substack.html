Rays Even the ALCS Behind Freddy Peralta — and One Very Bad Hop

Game 2 of the ALCS. Cleveland needed a split out of Tampa. They got Freddy Peralta instead.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2110207714680799633

Six innings, zero earned, and Cleveland's bats never figured out what pitch was coming next.

And then came the fourth inning. José Ramírez left third base uncovered on Chandler Simpson's infield chopper, then misplayed Ben Williamson's grounder two batters later. The ball clipped the turf and skidded between his legs.

Three runs scored in the inning. None of them earned. Cleveland's elite pitching held Tampa to three runs on the night — and Cleveland's defense handed Tampa all three.

Liam Hicks delivered the dagger — a two-run single that turned a scoreless ballgame into 3-0 Tampa. The Guardians' entire pitching plan erased in the time it takes a ball to hop twice on turf.

The Ramírez plan for the night:

Chase DeLauter finally got Cleveland on the board with an RBI single in the 7th, but by then it was accounting. Three unearned runs for Tampa, one earned run for Cleveland, series tied 1-1, flight to Cleveland for Game 3 Wednesday.

Ramírez, through the translator, after the game:

"It's a little faster here," Ramírez said of the Tropicana Field infield, through a club translator. "I tried to knock it down, but I couldn't."

Flyers Hit 0-8 — Worst Start in Franchise History

Philadelphia lost to Seattle 2-3 in overtime. Eight games. Zero wins. Zero overtime losses with a point to show for them. Just eight straight Ls to open a season that is already a smoking crater.

Worst start in franchise history. The Flyers have existed since 1967. They have never been this bad through eight games — not during the expansion years, not during the Eric Lindros fallout, not during any of the five head-coaching changes in the last decade. Never.

Seattle is the exact inverse. The Kraken are 5-2, their best start in franchise history, and they got there by beating Philly in overtime on the NHL's showcase Frozen Frenzy night with every team playing and the whole league watching.

John Tortorella, somewhere in a hallway, chewing through a water bottle.
