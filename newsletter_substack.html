Jalen Brunson hunts James Harden in the largest Knicks playoff comeback since 1970

The Knicks were down 93-71 with 7:52 left. They closed on a 44-11 run.

https://twitter.com/SharpFootball/status/2056936705244938530

The largest playoff comeback in Knicks history. The largest playoff comeback in the NBA since 1970. Teams trailing by 22 in the fourth quarter of a playoff game were 1-594 entering Tuesday night.

The Cavaliers held a 99.9% win probability with 7:49 left.

Brunson: 38 points, six assists, five rebounds, three steals. He scored 15 in the fourth quarter on 7-of-9 shooting. He outscored the Cavaliers by himself down the stretch. Brunson shot 7-of-11 when Harden defended him.

https://twitter.com/BallsackSports/status/2056937486664773690

Harden: 15 points, 5-16 FG, 1-8 from three, 6 turnovers. For the 48th playoff game of his career, Harden had more turnovers than made field goals. That's 48 out of 188 career playoff games.

https://twitter.com/StatMuse/status/2056933558204215323

Mike Brown postgame:

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2056941915698786750

They ran the same pick-and-roll to get Brunson on Harden for five straight possessions. All buckets. Kenny Atkinson sat on his hands. No timeout. No adjustment. No benching.

Atkinson postgame: "He's been one of our best defenders in these playoffs. I trust him."

https://twitter.com/TheNBACentel/status/2056939349762003139

Donovan Mitchell and James Harden combined for three points on 1-of-10 shooting during the decisive stretch. Mitchell went 1-6 in the fourth quarter and overtime with three points, one rebound, zero assists, and one turnover.

Kenny Atkinson postgame: "We got a little unlucky, quite honestly."

https://twitter.com/OldTakesExposed/status/2056933125238804825

Brunson relentlessly attacked Harden to spark an 18-1 run that made it a ballgame, then tied it at 101 with 19 seconds left in regulation. The Knicks opened overtime with a 9-0 run. Landry Shamet — zero points through three quarters — hit a clutch corner three in the fourth and another in OT.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2057042233065422947

MSG was a morgue at 93-71. By overtime it was bedlam. Mike Breen on the call. Knicks fans chanting outside the arena after the game. Not chanting "Let's go Knicks." Chanting two things:

- "F*CK TRAE YOUNG"
- "WE WANT WEMBY"

Teams that win Game 1 of the Conference Finals go on to win the series 78.2% of the time.

This is a legacy game. For Brunson. For Harden. For the Knicks. For the Cavaliers' collapse file.
