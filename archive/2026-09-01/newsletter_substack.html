Messi Walks Away From Argentina

Lionel Messi announced his retirement from the Argentina national team on Monday. 21 years. 125 goals. 207 appearances. Six World Cups. Done at 39.

https://twitter.com/JomboyMedia/status/2094448072981901667

Six weeks after Argentina lost the 2026 World Cup final to Spain in extra time. Three weeks after his father and longtime agent Jorge passed away. He'll keep playing for Inter Miami. The international career is over.

Before 2022, this looked like the cruelest story in sports. Lost the 2014 World Cup final to Germany. Lost Copa America finals in 2007, 2015, and 2016. After the 2016 loss to Chile on penalties, he announced his retirement in tears in the tunnel in East Rutherford, New Jersey — the same metro area where, a decade later, he'd play his final World Cup match.

He came back. Won Copa America in 2021. Won the World Cup in 2022 — Argentina's first in 36 years. Won another Copa America in 2024. Runner-up in 2026 at 39, still the best player on the pitch most nights.

https://twitter.com/ClutchPoints/status/2094570833905942833

The cross-sport question: where does this sit next to MJ and the Dream Team, Gretzky wearing the maple leaf, LeBron in Beijing and London and Paris? None of them carried what Messi carried. Basketball and hockey have deep national talent pools. Argentina's World Cup drought was Messi's personal weight for 17 years.

https://twitter.com/NBAMemes/status/2094605025607901587

The Dolphins Went Bananas on the Waiver Wire

NFL teams claimed players off waivers on Monday. The Dolphins claimed seven of them. No other team claimed more than three.

https://twitter.com/FieldYates/status/2094483547008454988

https://twitter.com/AlbertBreer/status/2094485141095678146

Personnel departments around the league read waiver claim volume as a tell for roster depth. Miami just told the entire NFL exactly how thin the depth chart looks. Seven claims is a GM standing in the parking lot yelling "get in the car."

Meanwhile in Dallas, the Cowboys claimed a running back off Kansas City's scrap heap and cleared the spot by waiving their backup quarterback.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2094515390491533775

Dallas is either fully committed to the Sam Howell experience or they think RB depth matters more than a QB2. If Dak sneezes wrong in Week 3, we'll find out which.

The SEC Is Exploring EXPULSION of LSU

Day five of the NFL-players-return saga. The SEC has moved from "we don't love this" to "we are considering removing a flagship member from the conference." Thursday's court hearing could bring forfeited games, federal court action, or the nuclear option. Kicking LSU out of the SEC is on the table.

And the first crack in LSU's plan showed up Monday. Jack Pyburn, one of the players at the center of the eligibility fight, got claimed by the Chiefs off waivers — and immediately told LSU he's out.

https://twitter.com/PatMcAfeeShow/status/2094490334503874712

Turns out an NFL 53-man spot beats a college locker room and a federal lawsuit. Lane Kiffin, asked about all of this yesterday, said Ole Miss is "focused on Clemson."
