Aaron Judge Has Been Playing on a Cracked Rib Since April

Aaron Judge has a stress fracture in his right rib. It started with a dive in late April. He's been playing on a broken bone for over a month. He'll be reimaged in 4-6 weeks. Realistic return: August.

NBA Investigators Are Knocking on Kawhi's Door

The league interviewed Kawhi Leonard and Uncle Dennis as part of the probe into whether the Clippers funneled cap-circumventing payments through the Aspiration sponsorship deal. The investigation opened in September. Now it's reaching the actual figures involved.

When the Timberwolves did a secret side deal with Joe Smith in 2000, they lost five first-round picks and Glen Taylor was suspended. That's the precedent everyone is whispering about in LA.

The Clippers built a roster, a building, and a brand around Kawhi. The price of admission might be a decade of forfeited draft picks.
