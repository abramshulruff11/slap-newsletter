Rick Adelman, 1946-2026

Rick Adelman, a Basketball Hall of Fame inductee who played seven NBA seasons before becoming one of the game's all-time winningest coaches, has died at 79. He was the father of Denver Nuggets coach David Adelman.

Across 23 seasons as a head coach for the Trail Blazers (1988-94), Warriors (1995-97), Kings (1998-2006), Rockets (2007-11) and Timberwolves (2011-14), Adelman amassed a 1,042-749 record for a winning percentage of .582. Only four other coaches — Pat Riley, Gregg Popovich, Jerry Sloan and George Karl — coached more games and had a better winning percentage than Adelman.

His greatest successes came with the Portland Trail Blazers, where he spent part of his playing career in the 1970s before guiding them to Western Conference titles and NBA Finals appearances in 1990 and 1992. He also engineered a 22-game winning streak with Houston in 2008, one of the longest in NBA history. He was inducted into the Hall of Fame in 2021 and received the NBCA's Chuck Daly Lifetime Achievement Award in 2023.
