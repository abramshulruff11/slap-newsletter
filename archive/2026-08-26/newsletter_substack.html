Big Ten and SEC Draw the Line: No NFL Players Coming Back to College

The SEC and Big Ten just teamed up to close the loophole nobody knew existed until Tuesday.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2092482053916745785

Twenty-four hours ago, Dae'Quan Wright was a Cleveland Brown. Then he got waived, agreed to play tight end for LSU, and became the first player in history to try the NFL-to-college pipeline. The two most powerful conferences in college sports saw that and shut it down.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2092396740473147720

https://twitter.com/AlbertBreer/status/2092272244806340989

The ACC and Big 12 sitting silent while the SEC and Big Ten sprint to the podium is peak college football politics. Sankey now has "full authority to enforce with penalties," which is the kind of phrase that gets written into rulebooks specifically so nobody has to define what it actually means. Suspensions? Forfeits? Retroactive ineligibility? Yes.

Then Lane Kiffin went on McAfee and did what Lane Kiffin does — took both sides of the argument in the same segment.

https://twitter.com/PatMcAfeeShow/status/2092311790239564087

https://twitter.com/PatMcAfeeShow/status/2092309525621616880

Translation: pros shouldn't come back to college, but also, please note that the pros who came back to college chose me. Kiffin has been running the "chaos agent" playbook for so long he's forgotten he can only pick one side.

LSU currently has Will Wade rebuilding a basketball roster with players just cleared by courts — RJ Luis (two-day Jazz deal, never played a G-League minute), potentially Keyshawn Hall pending legal relief. The SEC just passed a rule that could theoretically kick them off the roster mid-season if Sankey decides "full authority" means retroactive. Kiffin's own conference might have just torched his own athletic department.

The other wrinkle: this is only enforceable if the courts let it be. Judges have already ruled that the Class of 2022 gets five years of eligibility, which is the entire reason Wright had a runway to LSU in the first place. The SEC and Big Ten are essentially saying "we know the courts said yes, but we're saying no."

Cal Raleigh Homers in Four Straight At-Bats

Cal Raleigh had gone the entire season without a multi-homer game. Then he hit three on Monday. Then he hit one more Tuesday in the ninth to tie the Phillies.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2092435018115080478

https://twitter.com/ClutchPoints/status/2092435682996400504

Four home runs in four consecutive at-bats. Lou Gehrig, Mike Schmidt, Rocky Colavito, Carlos Delgado, Mike Cameron, J.D. Martinez, Scooter Gennett, and Bobby Lowe. Every one of them did it in a single game. Raleigh did it across two.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2092469847825256583

He's a switch-hitting catcher who was quietly having a normal season, and now he's chasing a fifth straight homer on Wednesday. Ken Griffey Jr., Ichiro, Randy Johnson — nobody in Mariners history has ever had a 48-hour stretch like this.
