Three Swings, Three Homers, Infinite Hype

Joshua Báez got called up from Triple-A Memphis on Friday night. Saturday afternoon at Wrigley Field, he hit a home run on the first pitch he ever saw in the big leagues. Then he hit one in his second at-bat. Then he hit one in his third.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2088716762162282770

https://twitter.com/JomboyMedia/status/2088711395676135858

449 feet to left-center off a Matthew Boyd fastball. Second one down the left-field line. Third one the opposite way to right. Left. Center. Right. He hit for the cycle in ballpark directions before he had a career batting average.

Cubs fans watching Matthew Boyd throw to him for a THIRD time:

https://twitter.com/BarstoolBigCat/status/2088716559845884277

https://twitter.com/BarstoolBigCat/status/2088716805334331651

The Cardinals dugout. His Triple-A teammates back in Memphis. His mom and 30 members of his family who traveled to Wrigley. Everyone on the same emotional rollercoaster.

https://twitter.com/JomboyMedia/status/2088742483035357340

Before the call-up, Báez was leading all of Triple-A with 34 home runs. Cardinals' No. 3 prospect. His father passed away before ever seeing him play in the majors — Báez had spoken publicly before the game about making good on a promise to him.

Then he came up in the 8th inning with a chance at a fourth homer.

He lined out to left. Ended the day 3-for-4, five RBIs, and a permanent chapter in the Baseball-Reference Trivia section.
