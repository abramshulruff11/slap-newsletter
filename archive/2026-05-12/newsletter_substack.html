NFL Schedule Leaks Begin: Chiefs-Broncos Monday Night, Bills' New Palace Opens on TNF

The NFL announced Chiefs-Broncos as Week 1 Monday Night Football on September 14. Site to be determined. For Week 1. The league has made schedule release a multi-day Netflix trailer campaign but still hasn't figured out where Patrick Mahomes plays his first game after shoulder surgery.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2054165025007239343

Chiefs-Broncos as the season opener makes sense. Mahomes vs. Bo Nix round two. Defending champions against the division rival that swept them in the regular season. But the site being TBD three months before kickoff is absurd. International game? Stadium conflict? Nobody knows.

Buffalo opens its new Highmark Stadium on Thursday Night Football in Week 2, hosting the Lions.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2053981039358316968

The 60,108-seat stadium cost $850 million in taxpayer money — $600 million from New York State, $250 million from Erie County. Largest taxpayer contribution ever for an NFL facility. And they're christening it with a primetime game against Dan Campbell's Lions.

The stadium doesn't have a roof but about 65% of seats are protected from the elements. Which means 35% of Bills fans will be getting buried in snow come December. They knew what they signed up for.

Cowboys-Eagles on Thanksgiving. First time these two meet since Philly traded for George Pickens. Jerry Jones gets his ratings. We all get to watch Skip Bayless melt down on Friday morning.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2053946685886197979

Full schedule drops Thursday night on NFL Network. Two-hour prime-time special with dramatic reveals, celebrity cameos, and graphics packages that look like movie trailers. The NFL has turned a spreadsheet release into appointment television.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2054200659109396656

Strength of schedule rankings are already circulating. Casual fans will complain about their team getting screwed. Same as every year. And in four months, half these projections will be completely wrong because nobody saw the Commanders going 13-4.

LeBron's Future in Doubt After Thunder Complete Sweep

Thunder swept the Lakers 115-110 in Game 4. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander scored 35 points. Ajay Mitchell added 28.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2054070018833256826

LeBron missed a crucial floater in the final 20 seconds. After the game: "I don't know what the future holds for me, obviously."

https://twitter.com/ESPN/status/2054083538710880703

He's 41. He's a free agent. This was only the fourth series sweep James has endured in 302 postseason games. And now he's telling us he doesn't know what happens next. That's not "see you in October" talk.

https://twitter.com/ClutchPoints/status/2054070435646730705

The Thunder have won nine straight playoff games dating back to their championship last season. Their eight wins this spring tie the longest win streak to start a postseason by a defending champion in league history. They just sent LeBron home.

Donovan Mitchell's 39-Point Second Half Ties Playoff Record

Donovan Mitchell scored 39 points in the second half — tying the NBA playoff record set by Eric "Sleepy" Floyd in 1987 — to lead the Cavaliers to a 112-103 Game 4 victory over the Pistons.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2054028760123375947

https://twitter.com/StatMuse/status=2054028783506592229

Mitchell had four points on 1-of-8 shooting in the first half. Then he came out and put on one of the greatest scoring performances in playoff history. The Cavaliers put together a 24-0 run spanning halftime — the most consecutive points in a playoff game in franchise history.

Mitchell scored 15 of those 24 points himself.

https://twitter.com/BallsackSports/status=2054023235734302816

Series tied 2-2 heading back to Detroit. The Pistons have no answers for Mitchell when he's cooking like this. Jalen Duren struggled with eight points, four turnovers, and two rebounds in 26 minutes. Cade Cunningham had 19 points but five turnovers. Cleveland's defense locked in and Mitchell took over.

Wemby Avoids Suspension, Will Play Game 5 Tonight

Victor Wembanyama will not face any further discipline for elbowing Naz Reid and will play in Game 5 against the Timberwolves in San Antonio.

https://twitter.com/ShamsCharania/status/2053904174459293748

Wembanyama was ejected from Game 4 after striking Reid — who had swarmed him along with Jaden McDaniels — with his elbow. After video review, the call was upgraded to a flagrant 2 for excessive contact above the neck. Flagrant 2 means automatic ejection. But the NBA decided no further punishment was warranted.

Kendrick Perkins was not happy:

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status=2053908775124242882

"You prioritized superstarship and views over someone's health." Perkins went full scorched earth. Mike Conley said the Wolves expected no suspension and "we don't want guys to miss games." Two very different reactions.
