The Bills Had Josh Allen and Went 2-for-9 on Two-Point Conversions

The Buffalo Bills went 2-of-9 on two-point conversions in 2025. That's a 22% conversion rate. The worst in the NFL since at least 2000.

https://twitter.com/SharpFootball/status/2052090403935891853

Read that again. Zero rush attempts. The Bills had Josh Allen — consensus top-5 QB, 6'5" 240 pounds, routinely trucks linebackers for fun — and they never once handed him the ball or let him keep it on a read option from two yards out.

They made him throw. Nine times. He completed two passes. Threw one interception. The other six attempts? Incompletions into traffic.

For context: the Cowboys, Seahawks, and Patriots all went 100% on two-point conversions last season. The Chiefs and Broncos were over 65%. Even bad offenses like the Texans only attempted six. The Bills attempted nine and failed seven times.

https://twitter.com/SharpFootball/status/2052086574519595440

Josh Allen: elite QB, mobile threat, cannon arm, runs people over. 2-of-9 on two-point conversions. Zero rush attempts.

Why didn't they just let him run it in?

One of those failures came in the final seconds against the Eagles. Allen sailed a pass just out of reach of Khalil Shakir with five seconds left. Would have won the game. The Bills entered that play 1-of-6 on two-point attempts for the season. They went for it anyway. Lost 13-12.

That loss cost them home-field advantage in the playoffs.

Cubs Win 14 Straight at Wrigley, Walk Off Three Games in a Row

The Cubs have won 13 consecutive games at Wrigley Field, their longest home winning streak since a 14-game run in 2008. Tonight they made it 14.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2052222885628936456

And they just walked off for the THIRD STRAIGHT GAME. Tonight's walk-off? A walk. Bases loaded, ninth inning, walked in the winning run.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2052216398617702821

The game before? PCA tied it, then they walked it off. The game before that? Another walk-off. Five walk-offs during the streak. Five different players.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2052211060078149838

The Cubs have started this season 16-5 at home, marking the team's best start at Wrigley Field since posting the same record in 1985.

Cubs manager Craig Counsell: "You just make yourself hard to beat. You develop this resilience and this hard-to-beat mentality".

The Cubs have won slugfests and 2-1 pitchers' duels. They're tied for fourth in MLB with 44 home runs and lead the league with a .353 OBP. Wrigley Field is back.

The Spurs Punched Back

The Spurs demolished the Timberwolves in Game 2, 133-95. That's a 38-point win. San Antonio won the first half 59-35, holding Minnesota to its lowest scoring half of the entire season.

Pop sat in on the film session after Game 1 and figured the Wolves out in one sitting.

The Knicks Are Up 2-0 But Might Lose OG Anunoby

The Knicks pulled away late for a 108-102 win in Game 2 over the 76ers. The Knicks now have a 2-0 series lead and a five-game playoff win streak.

https://twitter.com/TimBontemps/status/2052205432098242997

But here's the problem: OG Anunoby sustained an apparent lower leg injury in the closing stages of Game 2. Anunoby appeared to suffer a right leg injury late in the fourth quarter, went to the New York locker room and did not return.

https://twitter.com/TimBontemps/status/2052204450605859280

Reporters coming back from the Knicks' locker room said Anunoby wasn't in there and players wouldn't really speak about his health.

Anunoby finished with 24 points, five rebounds and four steals in the Game 2 win. In the ongoing playoffs, he's averaged 21.4 points, 7.5 rebounds, 1.9 steals and 1.1 blocks on 62/54/81 shooting splits.

Jalen Brunson is unstoppable — 26 points, clutch buckets in the fourth — but the supporting cast is banged up. Joel Embiid was ruled out for Game 2 with a right ankle sprain and right hip soreness. The Sixers were already in a 1-0 series hole after a 137-98 drubbing in Game 1.

And Paul George? He played all 12 minutes of the fourth quarter and scored ZERO points.

George and VJ Edgecombe played the entire fourth quarter and combined to shoot 0-for-9, while Tyrese Maxey was 2-for-8. The Sixers' 12 points on 21 possessions in the fourth quarter was their third least efficient quarter of the entire season.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2052230929423249515

The series shifts to Philly for Game 3 on Friday.
