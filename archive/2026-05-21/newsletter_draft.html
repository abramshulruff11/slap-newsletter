VEGAS STEALS GAME 1 WITHOUT MAKAR

Cale Makar is LeBron-level important to the Avalanche. Coach Jared Bednar announced before Game 1 that Makar (day-to-day, undisclosed injury) would miss the opener. Colorado's best player, their engine, the guy who runs everything — sidelined for the Western Conference Finals.

@ESPN
GAME 1 GOES TO THE GOLDEN KNIGHTS ⚔️

Vegas goes into Colorado and snatches the WCF opener 🔥

Vegas took full advantage. 4-2 win in Denver. Dylan Coghlan scored his first career playoff goal. Carter Hart made 36 saves. Mitch Marner fed Pavel Dorofeyev for an insurance goal. The Golden Knights have home-ice advantage now — they stole Game 1 on the road, and Game 2 is still in Denver before the series shifts to Vegas.

Colorado had moments. Valeri Nichushkin scored a between-the-legs beauty. Nathan MacKinnon extended his goal streak. But without Makar running the blue line and quarterbacking the power play, they couldn't sustain offense.

COACH O IS BACK

Ed Orgeron is back at LSU. Seven years after leading the Tigers to the 2019 national championship as head coach, Coach O returns as Lane Kiffin's special assistant to recruiting and defense.

@AdamSchefter
RT by @AdamSchefter: NEWS: LSU has finalized a deal to hire Ed Orgeron on Lane Kiffin's staff, Orgeron's agent Tzvi Grossman tells ESPN. Orgeron returns to LSU, which he led to the 2019 national title as head coach. Orgeron has a decades-long relationship with Kiffin.
View tweet

Orgeron and Kiffin have worked together before — at USC, at Tennessee. They've got a decades-long friendship. Kiffin took over at LSU this offseason and immediately brought in his old friend. Orgeron was fired by LSU in 2021 despite winning a national title two years earlier. Now he's back in a different role.
