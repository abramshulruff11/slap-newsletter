Game 3 of the Stanley Cup Finals May Have Been the Greatest Hockey Game Ever Played

Vegas 5, Carolina 4. Double overtime. Knights lead the series 2-1.

That score does not begin to describe what happened in T-Mobile Arena last night. Hockey, played at the top of the sport, on the edge of chaos.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2063477815895372015

Mitch Marner. The guy Toronto ran out of town for not being a playoff player. Second period. Three goals in 6:10. Fastest Stanley Cup Final hat trick ever. The previous record was 6:21, set by Maurice Richard in 1957.

He broke Rocket Richard's record. The Rocket Richard Trophy is literally named after the guy whose record he just took.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2063444564963156051

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2063477563326873879

Read that last line again. He scored more goals in this one game than in any playoff series he ever played for the Maple Leafs. Toronto front office watching this in real time:

Four points in one period. First NHL player to do that in the Final since Frank Foyston of the Seattle Metropolitans in 1919. The year before sliced bread was invented.

Vegas led 4-0 entering the third period. Game over. Series tied 1-1 going to Game 4. Cool, fine, we all go home.

Carolina had other plans.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2063480002025247011

Three goals in 39 seconds. Fastest three goals by one team in a Stanley Cup Final game. The previous record was 56 seconds, set by the 1954 Canadiens. Then Svechnikov tied it with 1:42 left in regulation. 4-4. From a 4-0 deficit. In the third period. Of the Stanley Cup Final.

Jacob Misiorowski Threw the Fastest Pitch by a Starting Pitcher in the Modern Tracking Era

103.7 miles per hour. From a starter. In the third inning. As an opening act.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2063466634199674999

Aroldis Chapman's all-time fastest pitch was 105.8 mph. He was a closer throwing one inning. Misiorowski is a starter who's expected to do this for 90+ pitches. His ERA is 1.50, second in baseball, 0.04 runs behind first.

The USMNT Lost to Germany, But Jedi Robinson's Goal Was a Rocket

USA fell 2-1 to Germany at Soldier Field. Final tuneup before the World Cup opener against Paraguay on June 12. The result wasn't great. The vibes were mixed. For about three seconds in the first half, Antonee "Jedi" Robinson made every American forget about the scoreline.

Golden Tempo Wins the Belmont Five Weeks After Winning the Derby

Golden Tempo did it again. Ridden by jockey Jose Ortiz from 12 lengths off the lead, Golden Tempo surged from the back of the pack to win the 158th Belmont. Last-to-first charge. Same as the Kentucky Derby five weeks ago.

https://twitter.com/PatMcAfeeShow/status/2063398306118553781

He's the 13th horse to win the Kentucky Derby and the Belmont. He is also not a Triple Crown contender, because his team skipped the Preakness.

https://twitter.com/BarstoolBigCat/status/2063398518782333073

Trainer Cherie DeVaux made more history Saturday — first woman to win multiple Triple Crown races. When asked if Golden Tempo could have won the Triple Crown: "It's not something I want to think about. We made our decision, and he won today, and we're going to be happy about that."
