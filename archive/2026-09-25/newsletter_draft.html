The Falcons had scored one touchdown in two weeks. One. Their offense was a rumor. Then Michael Penix Jr. came back from injury on national television and immediately did this:

Brewers Clinch the No. 1 Seed and Their First 100-Win Season Ever

Jake Bauers with an 8th-inning go-ahead knock, Milwaukee beats the Phillies 5-1, and the Brewers lock up MLB's best record and home-field advantage throughout the playoffs.

@TalkinBaseball_

Fifty-seven seasons of Milwaukee/Seattle Pilots baseball. The franchise had never won 100 games. Not in 1982 when they went to the World Series. Not in any of the recent playoff runs. Tonight they got to 100 for the first time — and they still have games left.
