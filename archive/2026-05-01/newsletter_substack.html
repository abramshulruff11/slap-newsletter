The Knicks Set an NBA Playoff Record by Obliterating the Hawks

The Knicks led 83-36 at halftime. That's a 47-point lead. The largest halftime lead in NBA playoff history.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2050028150071894339

The Knicks went on a 43-6 run. After the first quarter, they were up 40-15. They led by as many as 51 at one point in the second quarter.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2050006286117618112

The final score: Knicks 140, Hawks 89. New York won the series 4-2, after losing Games 2 and 3 by exactly one point apiece — and then winning the next three games by 16, 29 and 51 points, respectively. The Hawks went from up 2-1 to getting demolished three straight times. Atlanta went 20-6 from the All-Star break to the close of the regular season. That was the best mark in the East and the third-best record in the NBA down the stretch. Then they ran into a buzzsaw and got beaten so badly the announcers stopped knowing what to say.

https://twitter.com/TheNBACentel/status/2050013249458839706

The 51-point margin tied for the sixth-largest in NBA playoff history. It was the biggest playoff margin in a win for the Knicks — and the second-worst margin in Hawks playoff history, the worst in the Atlanta era.

https://twitter.com/BallsackSports/status/2050007408965996907

Karl-Anthony Towns had a triple-double on only four shots. He was 1 for 4 from the field and still finished with 12 points, 11 rebounds and 10 assists. The only player in NBA playoff history with a triple-double on fewer shots? Magic Johnson, who took three shots in a 13-point, 13-rebound, 13-assist game for the Lakers in the 1982 NBA Finals. KAT didn't even realize he had a triple-double until someone told him. He was too busy running the offense like a point guard who accidentally wandered into the center position.

https://twitter.com/WorldWideWob/status/2050023231013404710

OG Anunoby had 29 points on 11-for-14 shooting. Mikal Bridges had 24 points on 10-for-12 shooting. The Knicks shot like they were in a layup line. The Hawks shot like they were in a nightmare.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2050010454626374105

And because Atlanta's night couldn't get any worse, the Hawks were down by 50 points when Mitchell Robinson and Dyson Daniels got ejected for fighting. Fighting. Down 50. In an elimination game.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2050010984471814416

The 47-point lead tied for the second-biggest halftime lead in NBA history, regular season or playoffs. Dallas led the Clippers by 50 points at the break on Dec. 27, 2020, and Golden State led Sacramento by 47 at the half on Nov. 2, 1991. The 2020 Clippers game? Jalen Brunson was on that Mavericks team. He's seen this movie before. He knows how it ends.

https://twitter.com/BackAftaThis/status/2050005621869510727

After suffering back-to-back one-point losses in Games 2 and 3 to go down 2-1 in the series, the Knicks never lost again. They closed out the series in Atlanta. On the Hawks' home floor. In front of their fans. By 51 points. This is revenge for 2021, served colder than a halftime lead in December.

The Ducks Eliminate Connor McDavid and the Oilers in Round 1

The Anaheim Ducks eliminated the Edmonton Oilers with a 5-2 win in Game 6. The Ducks won their first Stanley Cup Playoff series since eliminating the Oilers in seven games in the 2017 Western Conference Second Round. "We were an average team all year," Connor McDavid said. "When you're an average team with high expectations, you're going to be disappointed."

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2050078515295404436

McDavid, Leon Draisaitl and the Oilers have won nine playoff series, played 81 postseason games and reached two Stanley Cup Finals in the past four years. They finally ran out of gas.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2050076734381244915

McDavid, who led the NHL with 138 points during the regular season, was held without a point for the third time in the series. He had six points in six games. The Oilers are cursed. They only win when they play the Kings. 2021 — don't face Kings, eliminated. 2022-2025 — face Kings, advance every time. 2026 — don't face Kings, eliminated in Round 1.
