Jaden McDaniels and the Wolves Erase Denver—Without Ant, Donte, or Ayo

The Timberwolves beat the Nuggets 110-98 in Game 6 to eliminate the defending Western Conference champs 4-2, and they did it without Anthony Edwards, Donte DiVincenzo, Ayo Dosunmu, and Kyle Anderson. Jaden McDaniels scored a career-high 32 points. Terrence Shannon Jr.—in his THIRD CAREER START—dropped 24. Jamal Murray went 4-for-17 for 12 points and a team-worst minus-18.

The Knicks Delivered the Most Humiliating Beatdown in Playoff History

The Knicks beat the Hawks 140-89 in Game 6. New York led 83-36 at halftime—a 47-point halftime lead that broke the NBA playoff record.

https://twitter.com/BallsackSports/status/2050007408965996907

OG Anunoby scored 29 points in 27 minutes. Karl-Anthony Towns had his second triple-double of the series. Towns finished with 12 points, 11 rebounds, and 10 assists—on 1-4 shooting from the field and 10-10 from the free-throw line. KAT just became the first player in NBA playoff history to record a triple-double with only one made field goal.
At one point, OG Anunoby had outscored the Hawks on his own. New York led by as many as 61 points, which is the biggest lead in any NBA playoff game during the play-by-play era.

https://twitter.com/BallsackSports/status/2050004236629385445

Dyson Daniels and Mitchell Robinson were ejected after fighting following a pair of free throws that gave the Knicks a 50-point lead in the second quarter. Two guys fought. While down 50. In the second quarter.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2050227809834999916

The final score was 140-89. New York's 51-point win tied for the sixth-largest margin of victory in NBA postseason history.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2050011293994643565

The Knicks advance to face either the Celtics or Sixers. The Hawks advance to the offseason and a very long series of front-office meetings.

The Sixers Force Game 7—And Suddenly the Celtics Look Mortal

The Sixers defeated the Celtics 106-93 in Game 6, their second straight game shutting down Boston's high-powered offense. All five Sixers starters scored at least 14 points, with Tyrese Maxey leading the way with 30. With the win, the Sixers earned the right to play a Game 7. They'll visit Boston on Saturday and look for a third consecutive win.

https://twitter.com/PFTCommenter/status/2050026519133553086

Joel Embiid carried over his momentum from a stellar second-half performance in Game 5, when he scored 33 points to force Game 6. Paul George finished with 23 points in Game 6, scoring 10 in the third quarter.

https://twitter.com/AlbertBreer/status/2050030606231220298

The Celtics have lost their way at the worst possible time. Boston has been pushed to a Game 7 of what was trending toward a breezy first-round series by a revitalized Philadelphia 76ers squad. Jayson Tatum exited with 4:03 left in the third quarter and did not return. He told reporters his left leg was "a little stiff" and that he expects to play in Game 7. Celtics fans watching Tatum limp off the court while Embiid does pushups on the logo:

https://twitter.com/TimBontemps/status/2050040546912055327

Boston blew several chances to close out Philadelphia. Now they face a winner-take-all game at home against a team playing with house money.

https://twitter.com/TheNBACentel/status/2050041509961994247

Game 7. Boston. Saturday night. Sixers trying to snap decades of playoff heartbreak. Celtics trying to avoid becoming the biggest choke artists of the decade.

McDavid and the Oilers Get Bounced by the Ducks—Again

The Anaheim Ducks eliminated the Edmonton Oilers 5-2 in Game 6, winning their first playoff series since 2017—when they also beat Edmonton. Connor McDavid postgame: "We've been searching for consistency all year and we didn't find it here in the playoffs." McDavid said the Oilers "were an average team all year."

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2050077295298195590

The Oilers made the Stanley Cup Final the last two years. Both times they lost to Florida. This year they didn't make it out of the first round.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2050079990427881698

The 2026 Ducks included a dozen players under the age of 25, and 14 who made their NHL playoff debuts in the series. Cutter Gauthier scored his fourth goal of the series in Game 6. Ryan Poehling also scored four times against the Oilers. Greg Cote called it. LeBatard Show vindicated.

https://twitter.com/ESPN/status/2050078515295404436

McDavid sustained an ankle injury in Game 2 when he got tangled up with teammate Mattias Ekholm and Ducks forward Ian Moore. Leon Draisaitl returned for Game 1 after missing the final 14 games of the regular season with a lower-body injury. McDavid and Draisaitl on whether injuries are an excuse: "Too hurt too soon. The first round is always tough. It's always chaotic. It's tough to play through things so early on, as many guys did in here." McDavid's window isn't closed. But it's closing fast. And the Oilers just wasted another year of it.
