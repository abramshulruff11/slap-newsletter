Pitt Escapes Blacksburg After Virginia Tech Kicker Misses a 36-Yarder That Everyone Watching Thought Was Good

Pittsburgh 35, Virginia Tech 33. Final play. 36-yard field goal to win it. The ball goes up and sails directly over the top of the upright.

The official on the ground says no good. There is no review.

@BarstoolBigCat
College Kickers!
View on Twitter →

Per NCAA Rule 12-3-1-b, a kick that passes above the top of the upright is not reviewable. One man staring up into a cold Virginia sky decides a top-25 ACC game. Everyone with a decent angle thought it was good. The one guy with the whistle thought otherwise.

The internet's fix is already written: there is nothing in the NCAA rulebook preventing a 100-foot upright. Virginia Tech may have literally won this game if they'd built taller goalposts. In 2026. We're deciding playoff hopes on carpenter height.

Pat Narduzzi used the postgame to rip AP voters. Pitt now looks like the only real threat to Miami in the ACC, and they got there on a kick nobody can confirm either way.
