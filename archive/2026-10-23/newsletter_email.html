Kevin Cash looked at the matchup and decided his ace is better on extra rest. Martinez gets Game 1, Rasmussen gets an extra day, Freddy Peralta gets pushed to Game 3 at the Trop where he's posted a 2.03 ERA in five starts this year. Martinez is better on the road — 2.38 ERA in 16 road starts. Everything about the decision is a math problem Tampa Bay solved three different ways.

Milwaukee is countering with Jacob Misiorowski, who grew up a Royals fan and now gets to open the first World Series between two never-won-it franchises in decades. The Brewers just swept the Dodgers 4-0 and gave Freddy Peralta the ALCS MVP. The Rays are the same thing with a different logo.

One of these franchises wins its first title tonight-ish. Tonight, 8:08 ET at American Family Field, that math gets simpler for someone.

Cavs 124, Sixers 79: LeBron's Home Debut Was a 45-Point Massacre

The 76ers were booed by their own fans before halftime. Of their home opener. On LeBron James Night.

Allen Iverson was in the building. Zhuri James was courtside on her 12th birthday. The new LeBron-Embiid handshake got a public rollout. Cleveland went up 17 after one quarter, 27 at the half, and the Wells Fargo Center started raining boos while Donovan Mitchell was still buzzer-beating threes to close quarters.

Sixers fans watching the Big 3 era:

Philly is 0-2. They lost on ring night and then got housed by 45 at home. The 2010 Heat opened 9-8 and that was treated like a national emergency. The 2026 Sixers would kill to be 9-8. This group has played 96 minutes of basketball together and lost the second half by scores that look like typos.

Trump vs. LeBron, Day 2 of the LeBron-As-A-Sixer Era

Trump accused LeBron of disrespecting the country for continuing warmups during the national anthem — shooting jumpers and working on his golf swing. On his second day as a Philadelphia 76er. In the middle of a 0-2 start where the real controversy is getting outscored by 45.

LeBron has been through this before — the 2017 "U bum" chapter is a whole archived file — so the playbook exists. The timing is just extremely unhelpful. You either lead SportsCenter for the blowout or for the anthem. Right now it's a tossup, and neither one is a good look for Philadelphia.

Patriots at Bears, TNF: Ditka Tribute, Caleb Returns

Soldier Field held a moment of silence for Mike Ditka before kickoff. Only person to start for a Super Bowl champion and later coach a Super Bowl champion. Chicago icon in the most literal sense of the word.

Then they played some football. Caleb Williams is back after missing three games with a torn hamstring, Luther Burden III is already popping huge gains, and Christian Gonzalez is active for New England despite being sick all week. Two 4-2 teams in prime time, both trying to prove the start is real.

NBA Refs: Yeah, That Was a Travel. Four Steps, Actually.

The association that represents NBA referees publicly confirmed that LaMelo Ball's viral double stepback against the Heat was a travel. Four steps. Not two.

The clip spent 24 hours trending as a highlight-reel ankle-breaker before the officiating body stepped in to say, actually, that was two extra steps. The move counted. The bucket counted. LaMelo gets to keep the stats. The refs just want everyone to know, for the record, that they saw it and it was illegal.
