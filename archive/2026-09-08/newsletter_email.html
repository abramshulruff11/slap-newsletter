Christian Gonzalez Just Broke the Patriots' Piggy Bank

Christian Gonzalez is the highest-paid cornerback in NFL history. He is also, as of Monday night, the highest-paid Patriot ever. Any position. Any era.

Four years, $135 million, $102 million guaranteed, locked up through 2031. Tom Brady at his peak in New England made $25 million per season. Gonzalez is making $33.75M to cover receivers.

Robert Kraft signing off on a nine-figure guarantee for a cornerback:

The cornerback market caught fire and nobody called 911

Four different corners have reset the top of the market in a single offseason. Trent McDuffie at $31M. Denzel Ward at $31.1M. Devon Witherspoon at $33M. Now Gonzalez at $33.75M.

The Patriots kept a first-round pick. On purpose.

The Patriots signed a first-round pick to a second contract. Since 2017, every single one of New England's first-rounders has left before getting extended.

The list: Dominique Easley, Malcom Brown, Isaiah Wynn, Sony Michel, N'Keal Harry, Mac Jones, Cole Strange. A parade of guys who either didn't work out or worked out somewhere else.

Patriots fans finally seeing the front office keep a homegrown star:

He's playing Witherspoon in 48 hours

Gonzalez signs Monday night. Wednesday night in Seattle: a rematch against the Seahawks. The two highest-paid cornerbacks in football, on opposite sidelines, 48 hours after one of them leapfrogged the other by $750,000.

Witherspoon signed his $132 million deal on August 15. He held the crown for exactly 24 days.

Tua Is the Falcons' Starter (For Week 1)

Tua Tagovailoa is starting Week 1 against the Steelers. Michael Penix Jr. is inactive. Everyone read the headline and assumed a quarterback battle got settled.

It didn't.

@AlbertBreer
On the @AtlantaFalcons situation, Michael Penix has been fully cleared. So he'd be good to dress for Week 1. But he's still working his way back from the injury, so Atlanta's being careful here.
This isn't Tua Tagovailoa beating him out. Moreso, it's about Penix's readiness.
View on Twitter →

Penix is fully cleared medically. He wasn't cleared for 11-on-11 work until two weeks ago. Tua got the reps in camp because he was the only healthy option, and now he gets the ball because you can't roll out a QB whose last competitive live snap was in the spring.

The phrase "For Week 1" is doing more work than the entire Falcons offensive line.
