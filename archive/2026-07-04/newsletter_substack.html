Cape Verde. Population 525,000. Smaller than Wyoming. Smaller than the Lexington, Kentucky metro area. Ten volcanic islands off the west coast of Africa where soccer is played but nobody was supposed to notice.

They just took the reigning World Cup champions to extra time and lost 3-2.

Chapman Now Owns The Relievers

Aroldis Chapman struck out Denzer Guzman to lead off the ninth Friday night. That was his 1,364th career strikeout. Most in MLB history by a relief pitcher.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2073261527377891783

Wilhelm held that record for over 50 years. Knuckleballer who didn't make his debut until 29, pitched until nearly 50, first reliever in the Hall of Fame. Chapman defected from Cuba in 2009 and has been throwing 100 mph at hitters ever since.

The record-breaking pitch: 98.6 mph. Guzman couldn't get around in time.

May 18, Chapman passed Joe Nathan for 10th all-time in saves at 378. Friday's save was his 384th. Next: Dennis Eckersley at 390.
