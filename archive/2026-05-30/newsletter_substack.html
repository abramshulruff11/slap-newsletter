Canada Hasn't Won the Stanley Cup Since 1993 and the Internet Won't Let Them Forget It

No Canadian team has won the Stanley Cup since 1993. Thirty-three years.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2060565004311580989

Canada invented hockey. Has seven NHL franchises. Hasn't won its own trophy since the year Jurassic Park came out.

https://twitter.com/RedditCFB/status/2060555736355361238

The Knicks' NBA Finals drought lasted 27 years and ended this season. The Cubs went 108 years between World Series titles. Canada's 33-year Cup drought isn't the longest in sports history, but it might be the most embarrassing. They invented the sport. American teams have won it 33 straight times.
