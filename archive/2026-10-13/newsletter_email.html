The weight of that streak lifting in real time:

Stafford and Allen both over 300 total yards. A 4th quarter shootout. Allen was 5-for-5 passing for 121 yards and ran for 91 more between the 4th and OT.

James Cook fumbled in overtime. Aaron Donald recovered it. The Rams drove down the field with the game in their hands, lined up Harrison Mevis for a 47-yarder to end it, and Greg Rousseau came off the edge and blocked the kick. The OT curse was two feet of leg extension away from going 0-8.

Then after the whistle, Rams left tackle Alaric Jackson walked up and threw a punch at Bradley Chubb. On camera. Postgame.

Chubb's response was the most "I've been in the league 8 years" quote imaginable: emotions, they always catch the second guy, let him swing, teammates held me back, I just chilled. Bills to 4-1 and first in the AFC East. Rams to 2-3. Allen to 1-7 in OT and 26% to win MVP.

The OT curse, one more time for the people in the back

Josh Allen was 0-7 in overtime. The worst OT record ever by a QB. The league rewrote the overtime rules partly because of what happened to him in the 13-second game. He still couldn't win one.

Allen to Scott Van Pelt afterward: "I haven't been a fan of overtime games in my career, so we get the first one. We'll take it."
