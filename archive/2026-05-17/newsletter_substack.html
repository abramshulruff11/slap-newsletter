SGA back-to-back: The Thunder's 25-year-old just became the 14th player in NBA history to repeat as MVP

https://twitter.com/ShamsCharania/status/2056009401102700868

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander is the 14th player in NBA history to win back-to-back MVPs. He's the first player to do it since Nikola Jokić in 2021-22 and the first guard since Stephen Curry in 2014-16. At 27 years old, he now has more MVPs than Shaq, Kobe, Hakeem, KG, AI, Barkley, Wade, Durant, Dirk, Harden, and Russ.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2056011740253470871

The numbers are absurd:

https://twitter.com/ShamsCharania/status/2056011465950147051

The Thunder won 64 games despite Jalen Williams being limited to just 33 games due to injury. SGA carried them through massive roster attrition and never dipped below 20 points. Not once.

Big Cat's take on the MVP race:

https://twitter.com/BarstoolBigCat/status/2056012079530701283

Paul Reed dropped 28 points in the Pistons' Game 6 win over the Cavs last night. If playoffs counted, maybe he'd have a case. But regular season voters already locked in SGA months ago.

The list SGA now joins: Jordan, LeBron, Magic, Bird, Curry, Jokić, Giannis, Duncan, Russell, Chamberlain, Moses Malone, Larry Johnson, Kareem. That's the company.

The Thunder face the Spurs in the Western Conference Finals starting next week. SGA vs Wemby. The story of the next decade.

The man will not retire: 42-year-old Aaron Rodgers is coming back for Year 22

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2055797681415406017

Aaron Rodgers is 42 years old. He's coming back for a 22nd NFL season. One-year deal with the Steelers worth up to $25 million.

https://twitter.com/BarstoolBigCat/status/2055800714253734316

The deal pays Rodgers between $22 and $23 million in base salary, with incentives pushing it to $25 million — a raise from his $13.65 million deal last year. He reunites with Mike McCarthy, his old coach from Green Bay. They won 107 games together including playoffs — 9th-most by any head coach-QB duo in the Super Bowl Era.

Here's the rub:

https://twitter.com/billbarnwell/status/2055811133584327022

Steelers start OTAs Monday and Rodgers is expected to be there — a contrast to last year when he didn't commit until mandatory minicamp in June.

Game 7 in Montreal: Sabres erase series deficit with dominant blowout

Buffalo rallied from an early two-goal deficit to beat Montreal 8-3 in Game 6, forcing a winner-take-all Game 7.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2055845106381402470

Rasmus Dahlin had five points — a goal and four assists — becoming the first defenseman in NHL history to record five points in an elimination game. No Sabres defenseman had ever had more than three points in a playoff game.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2055847101087584560

Jack Quinn scored twice and Buffalo scored seven straight goals after trailing 3-1 in the first period. Starter Alex Lyon was pulled after allowing three goals on four shots, and Ukko-Pekka Luukkonen came in to make 18 saves. The series returns to Buffalo for Game 7 on Monday. Winner goes to the Eastern Conference Finals.

The Mac is back: Conor McGregor returns July 11 to fight Max Holloway

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2055836495378813191

After five years away from the Octagon — his last fight was in 2021 — McGregor returns at UFC 329 on July 11 to face Max Holloway. The fight is at 170 lbs. This is a rematch of their 2013 bout, which McGregor won by unanimous decision in his second UFC fight. Thirteen years later, they're fighting two weight classes heavier.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2055838055374672076

McGregor is 37 years old and hasn't fought in five years. Holloway is a legend in his own right. This is International Fight Week in Las Vegas — UFC's biggest annual event. The hype will be enormous.
