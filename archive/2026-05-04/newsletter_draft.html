BREAKING: The Dallas Mavericks are hiring Masai Ujiri as the franchise's new Team President and Alternate Governor, sources tell me and @BannedMacMahon. Ujiri ran the Toronto Raptors from 2013 to 2025, serving as the architect of the 2019 NBA championship team.
View tweet

Six months. The Mavs fired Nico Harrison in November and just now landed their guy.

Ujiri traded DeMar DeRozan for Kawhi Leonard on a one-year rental. One season. One title. Then Kawhi left and Ujiri rebuilt. Seven straight playoff appearances. He's not a project guy.

Dallas traded Luka for Anthony Davis. AD has played 14 of 44 games since arriving. Kyrie Irving tore his ACL in March. The Mavs started 3-8. Harrison got fired. Cooper Flagg is the only reason anyone in Dallas is still watching.

Six months means Dallas interviewed everyone. Got rejected by everyone. Ujiri had options. He chose Dallas because if he fixes this, he's a legend.

Magic Fire Mosley One Day After Blowing 3-1 Lead

@ShamsCharania
BREAKING: The Orlando Magic have dismissed head coach Jamahl Mosley, sources tell ESPN. Mosley's tenure in Orlando finishes after five seasons, including three consecutive playoff runs from 2023 to 2026. Orlando had a first-round elimination for the third straight time Sunday.
View tweet

One day. The Magic blew a 3-1 series lead to the Pistons on Sunday night. Mosley was fired Monday morning.

Orlando had a 24-point lead at home in Game 6. They scored 19 second-half points. They missed 23 consecutive shots. They got booed by their own fans. Then they lost Game 7 by 22. The eighth seed came back from down 3-1. Four of the previous five teams to take a 3-1 lead over a one seed won the series. Orlando didn't.

Three straight first-round exits. Two division titles. Zero series wins. Jeff Weltman signed an extension right before the NBA Cup final four, then fired the coach after the collapse.

@ShamsCharania
Magic president Jeff Weltman, who signed a contract extension just before Orlando's run to the NBA Cup final four, will lead the team's search for the next head coach. Mosley led the Magic to two division titles and three consecutive winning seasons while dealing with a litany of injuries to key starters, particularly over the last two years.
View tweet

Paolo Banchero and Mosley didn't get along. Worst-kept secret in the league. Paolo said after the loss that the team doesn't adjust to opponents' adjustments at halftime. When your star player is calling out your schemes in the postgame presser after a Game 7 loss, the coach is already gone.

The front office will point to injuries. Franz Wagner missed the final three games. Jalen Suggs played 35 games this year. Paolo missed half of last season. All true. At some point the front office has to own the roster construction. They traded four first-round picks for Desmond Bane and finished as the eighth seed. They expected 50 wins. They got 45 and had to survive the play-in.

Orlando's offense ranked 19th this season. First time in a decade they've been outside the bottom 10. 19th isn't good enough when you're trading four firsts for a scorer.

@HaterReport
The Orlando Magic front office scapegoating Jamahl Mosley after losing in the 1st round again
View tweet

Mosley's career in Orlando: 189-221. Five seasons. Three playoff appearances. Zero series wins. He took over a rebuild, developed Banchero and Franz, built a top-five defense two years in a row. Then this year the defense fell to 11th, the offense stayed mediocre, and they blew the biggest moment of his tenure.

Billy Donovan is already being linked to the job. Donovan resigned from the Bulls last month. Has history in Florida from his University of Florida days. If Orlando hires him, they're betting on offensive scheme and player relationships to fix what Mosley couldn't.
