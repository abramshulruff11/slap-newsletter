Rams fans doing the math on this offseason:

Brock Purdy just spent the offseason watching his team fly to Australia for a preseason electromagnetic-field-adjacent home game. Now he opens the season staring across the line at Aaron Donald AND Myles Garrett.

https://twitter.com/FieldYates/status/2094153697487966427

The last time a generational talent came back from an extended layoff and picked up where he left off, it was Jordan in 1995. Even MJ needed a rust year. Donald's plan is to show up Week 1.
