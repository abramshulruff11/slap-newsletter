Avs Rally from 3-0 Down, Advance to Western Conference Finals

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2054760583216378112

Down 3-0 midway through the second period, facing elimination on the road, Colorado rallied to beat Minnesota 4-3 in overtime. Brett Kulak scored the winner.

Nathan MacKinnon with the clutch gene:

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2054755262037193108

Wild fans watching a 3-0 lead evaporate in their own building:

Minnesota had it. Up three goals at home with the crowd going crazy. Then Colorado remembered they're the Presidents' Trophy winners and decided to actually play hockey. The comeback was complete when Kulak ended it in OT — his first goal since January.

The Avs are built for another run. They won the Stanley Cup in 2022. This core knows how to win when it matters.
