Algeria-Austria Delivers the Wildest Ending in World Cup History

Group J was done. Argentina won. Austria and Algeria knew a draw sent both through. Simple math.

89 minutes of normal soccer. Then stoppage time turned into a cardiac event.

https://twitter.com/ClutchPoints/status/2071081438540333380

Tied 2-2. Algeria running out the clock. Riyad Mahrez scored his second in the 93rd minute. Austria on the brink. Sasa Kalajdzic headed in the equalizer moments later. Three minutes. Two goals. One country eliminated on TV.

That country was watching from somewhere else entirely. Iran.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2071083529652298116

Iran needed Austria or Algeria to win. A draw eliminated them. When Mahrez scored in the 93rd, Iran was through to the Round of 32. For 180 seconds.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2071084910522098154

Iran fans watching the Kalajdzic header go in:

The full Iran experience is borderline cursed.

https://twitter.com/RedditCFB/status/2071081607944184184

Three goals taken away by VAR. A miracle restoration. A miracle robbery. All in two weeks. Iran finished third in Group G with three draws — Belgium, New Zealand, Egypt — and got eliminated by a stoppage-time header in a different stadium.

Austria finished second behind Argentina. They advance for the first time since 1982. Their reward? Spain on Thursday in Los Angeles.

Meanwhile, the 39-year-old GOAT came off the bench and did GOAT things.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2071078992573067599

https://twitter.com/StatMuse/status/2071077911268929656

Somewhere in Portugal-Colombia, Cristiano Ronaldo attempted a bicycle kick. Around the League has that disaster.

Round of 32 is set. Iran is out. Austria gets Spain. Algeria gets Switzerland. The 1982 ghosts of the "Disgrace of Gijón" got exorcised in Kansas City by two goals scored 180 seconds apart.
