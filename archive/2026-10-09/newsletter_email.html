Guardians Force Game 5, Stay Alive

Cleveland was three outs from going home. Down 2-0 in the series, up 3-2 late in Game 4, and then José Ramírez decided this wasn't the night.

A 100-mph fastball at the top of the zone. Gone. 3-2 Guardians.

Sixth inning. Six runs. A Ramírez three-run triple with a 1% expected batting average. A Patrick Bailey two-run single to put it away. The offense that managed three total runs in the first two games suddenly looked like it had been pretending the whole time.

Then Gavin Williams — 11 strikeouts in Game 2, starting Game 5 on Saturday — came in to close out the final three outs tonight. Stephen Vogt managing like a man who understands his season ends in 48 hours if he doesn't.

Cleveland's last World Series title was 1948. Saturday at 8 PM ET, they get to keep that shot alive or go home for the winter.

@JeffPassan
