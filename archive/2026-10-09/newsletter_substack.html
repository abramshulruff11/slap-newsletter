Winless Bucs Shock Cowboys in Dallas

Tampa Bay walked into AT&T Stadium at 0-4 with an undrafted rookie quarterback making his second career start. They left with 243 rushing yards, a 24-16 win, and the Cowboys' season in a trash can.

https://twitter.com/AlbertBreer/status/2108396786620445006

Jalon Daniels: UDFA. $25K signing bonus. Six years at Kansas. Beat out Jake Browning in camp. Now starting for Tampa Bay while Baker Mayfield's thumb heals. Bucky Irving did the rest.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2108398804496236723

Four weeks ago, Dak Prescott beat the Texans and told CJ Stroud to "get some wins." The Cowboys have not won since. Three straight losses, including this one to an 0-4 team starting a guy who went undrafted five months ago.

Then there's the hallway. Prescott walking to the locker room after the loss. A third-party contractor positioned outside the players' exit yelled "Embarrassing." Prescott looked at him, kept walking, told a Cowboys employee: "Tough guy. Get his ass out of here."

"Feel free to speak your mind. Speak it, just don't cower away from the reaction you're going to get. So, yeah, if you're saying it's embarrassing, say it's embarrassing. If you're saying it to me, say it to me. Don't go back on it."

The franchise quarterback spent his postgame press conference addressing a guy who drives a car for a third-party contractor.

Jake Ferguson dropped a 4th-down pass. Daron Bland dropped a pick-six that hit him in the hands.

https://twitter.com/NFLMemes/status/2108405192433549346

Jerry Jones left the box early. Schottenheimer is considering staff changes. Dallas is 2-3, third in the NFC East, 1.5 games back.

Guardians Force Game 5, Stay Alive

Cleveland was three outs from going home. Down 2-0 in the series, up 3-2 late in Game 4, and then José Ramírez decided this wasn't the night.

A 100-mph fastball at the top of the zone. Gone. 3-2 Guardians.

Sixth inning. Six runs. A Ramírez three-run triple with a 1% expected batting average. A Patrick Bailey two-run single to put it away. The offense that managed three total runs in the first two games suddenly looked like it had been pretending the whole time.

Then Gavin Williams — 11 strikeouts in Game 2, starting Game 5 on Saturday — came in to close out the final three outs tonight. Stephen Vogt managing like a man who understands his season ends in 48 hours if he doesn't.

Cleveland's last World Series title was 1948. Saturday at 8 PM ET, they get to keep that shot alive or go home for the winter.

LeBron's Sixers Debut, Lakers Suddenly Have a Defense

Boston beat Philadelphia 124-113 in preseason action. LeBron James made his Sixers debut.

Year 24. New jersey. Still working on handshakes with Jaylen Brown. Still being asked to be a point guard, a backup center, and the best player on a team that currently employs Joel Embiid — who, in the same game, flopped so hard he nearly injured the guy he was flopping into.

Meanwhile, in Los Angeles.

Lakers 127, Kings 103. The entire internet reached the same conclusion at the same time: maybe the 41-year-old wasn't helping on that end.
