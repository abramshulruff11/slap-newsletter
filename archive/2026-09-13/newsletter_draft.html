Texas Just Broke College Football's Most Unbreakable Record

AP No. 1 teams were 197-0 when holding a 20-point 4th quarter lead. Since 2004. That's 22 years, 197 games, zero losses. Until Saturday night in Columbus.

Texas was down 20 in the 4th quarter to No. 1 Ohio State. Then they scored 21 unanswered. Final: 24-23.

Arch Manning threw an interception earlier in the game. Then he came back and led the biggest comeback in Texas football in nearly two decades. Postgame he told reporters he couldn't lose to Ohio State again because he'd been having nightmares about last year's loss.

The kid took the nightmares quote and turned it into a haymaker.

Ohio State lined up for a field goal with 1:23 left. Wide left. Texas got the ball back down 6, and Hollywood Smothers punched in the go-ahead touchdown. Second week in a row a Big Ten heavyweight loses on a missed kick — Michigan did the same thing to Oklahoma the very same day.

Then Sarkisian gave us the postgame moment of the year. Sideline reporter tried to ask him a question. He was not interested.

"THAT'S CALLED TEXAS FIGHT! HOOK 'EM!" Mic dropped, walked off, done. No coach-speak. No "credit to our guys." Just pure, uncut Texas.

Ryan Day now owns the largest blown lead of his career. Week 3, at home, against a team he was favored against. The CFP path just got infinitely tighter for a coach who already lives under a heat lamp in Columbus.

Ryan Garcia Needed Two Rounds

Ryan Garcia defeated Conor Benn by second-round TKO. Garcia dropped Benn with a right hand in Round 2. Benn got back to his feet. Garcia kept throwing punches until the referee stopped it. 85 seconds of real work.

Then he grabbed the mic. "Teofimo [Lopez], get your ass up here. Where you at?" Garcia invited WBA welterweight champion Lopez into the ring to face off. Security refused. Knock a guy out and call out the next champ before your gloves are off. That's the job.

Rybakina Owns Sabalenka Now

Elena Rybakina beat Aryna Sabalenka in the US Open final for her third career Grand Slam and her first at Flushing Meadows. Second time this year she's beaten Sabalenka in a Major final.

At some point "rivalry" becomes "personal problem."

Maria Sharapova handed her the trophy. The last Russian-born woman to win a Slam handing it to the new one. Rybakina exits the tournament as the World No. 1.

Oklahoma State Was a 24.5-Point Underdog

Oklahoma State beat Oregon 39-31. The Cowboys came in as a 24.5-point underdog riding a 21-game losing streak to FBS opponents. Both of those numbers died in Stillwater.

Drew Mestemaker never started a varsity game in high school. At Vandegrift High in Austin, he played behind a Power Five quarterback recruit and spent his senior year on special teams and defense. No recruiting stars, no scholarship offers. He walked on at North Texas, led the FBS in passing yards last year, followed his coach to Stillwater, and on Saturday beat former five-star recruit Dante Moore and the Ducks. 317 yards, two touchdowns.

He also outrushed the entire state of Oregon by himself.
