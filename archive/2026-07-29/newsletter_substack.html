Jalen Carter Is Now the Highest-Paid DT in NFL History

Four years. $152 million. Up to $160M with incentives. $106 million guaranteed. Through 2031.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2082149239660884094

Carter's $38 million AAV resets the DT market one month after Jeffery Simmons reset it. Before Simmons, Chris Jones had been sitting at just under $32M for years. The interior D-line market went up by six million dollars a year in about 30 days.

Carter is 25, coming off a Super Bowl, signed through his age-31 season. Philly gave Jordan Davis $78 million in March. The Eagles' D-line is closed for business through the end of the decade.

Denzel Ward Just Became the Highest-Paid DB in NFL History. Again.

Two years. $62.2 million. $52.3 million guaranteed.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2082218593438069101

Ward signed the previous record in 2022 — five years, $100.5 million — and the market ate it whole. Now he's back on top with a new deal that pays $31.1M annually.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2082218828927283593
