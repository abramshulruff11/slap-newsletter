The Knicks Are Going to the Finals

The Knicks are headed to the NBA Finals for the first time since 1999. Twenty-seven years. That's how long New York waited.

They swept Cleveland 130-93 in Game 4, completing the series 4-0 and punching their ticket to the Finals. Through 14 playoff games, the Knicks own a +271 point differential (+19.4 ppg) — the highest ever by a team entering the NBA Finals, surpassing the 2016-17 Warriors' +196.

That includes 11 straight wins since falling behind 2-1 to Atlanta in the First Round. The Knicks are averaging 122.9 ppg during their 11-0 run — the highest-scoring 11-game stretch in a single postseason since the 1985 "Showtime" Lakers.

The Knicks won their three series-clinching games by an average of 39.3 points: a 51-point win over Atlanta, a 30-point win over Philly, and a 37-point victory over Cleveland. Jalen Brunson never played a single fourth quarter in any of those closeout games.

The city went absolutely insane. Streets flooded. Fans getting arrested. Timothée Chalamet was euphoric. Newly elected New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani tagged the official Department of Sanitation account and wrote that he would like to "report a sweep". NYC sanitation responded with: "CLEAN UP IN CLEVELAND!!"

The Cavs never had a chance. Kenny Atkinson said postgame that Cleveland "analytically" won two out of three games based on "expected score". "I know no one wants to hear that," Atkinson said. "The general public, everyone is outcome-based".

Yes, Kenny. The NBA Playoffs are outcome-based. They have this annoying habit of advancing teams that actually win the games.

Donovan Mitchell apologized to Cleveland fans after the sweep: "I'm sorry for the city of Cleveland for it to be like this in a sweep. That's ass".

The Cavs blew a 22-point lead in Game 1, lost in overtime, then got boat-raced in three straight. James Harden finished Game 4 with 12 points on 2-of-8 shooting, 0-for-6 from three, and five turnovers.

Through 17 playoff games in 2026, Harden averaged 19.6 points, 5.7 assists, and 5.2 rebounds. But the turnovers and defensive lapses defined the series. Jalen Brunson hunted him relentlessly.

This is the biggest Knicks story in a generation. The Nova Knicks — Brunson, Bridges, Hart — are one win away from a championship together after winning one at Villanova. The financial absurdity of trading for KAT and giving up assets for Mikal Bridges now looks like genius.

The Finals await. Thunder or Spurs. Either way, the Knicks are four wins from immortality.

First No-Hitter Since 2024: Astros Trio Makes History

Baseball finally got a no-hitter. The drought is over.

Tatsuya Imai, Steven Okert, and Alimber Santa combined to no-hit the Texas Rangers 9-0 on May 25, 2026. The most recent year without any no-hitters was 2025, and the last no-hitter before this came on September 4, 2024 when the Cubs' Shota Imanaga, Nate Pearson, and Porter Hodge combined to no-hit the Pirates.

Nearly two years. That's the longest drought since 2005.

The twist: Santa was making his MLB debut and became the second player in Major League history to pitch in a no-hitter in his debut, joining Bumpus Jones of the Reds, who threw one by himself on Oct. 15, 1892.

Imai threw six spotless innings after walking three of the first four batters he faced. Okert got three outs before Santa made his major league debut and fired two perfect innings to complete the Astros' 17th regular-season no-hitter.

Christmas came on Memorial Day. Santa threw 11 pitches in the bottom of the ninth — the final pitch a sweeper that Brandon Nimmo watched for strike three (the call was upheld following a brief ABS challenge).

This was the 18th no-hitter in Astros history and the first since Ronel Blanco blanked the Toronto Blue Jays on April 1, 2024. Houston's 18 no-hitters are the most in baseball since they joined the league as an expansion team in 1962. The Los Angeles Dodgers are a distant second with 13 no-hitters during that time.

Knights Erase 3-0 Deficit, One Win From Finals

Vegas trailed 3-0 after the first period in Game 3, then scored five unanswered goals to win 5-3 and take a 3-0 series lead. The Avalanche were 74-1 all-time when leading by at least three goals in franchise playoff history — they're now 74-2.

Nathan MacKinnon, the team's Hart Trophy finalist and leading playoff scorer, was injured when he blocked a slap shot in the second period. He didn't come out for the third period at first, then took only four shifts when he returned.

The Knights are one win from the Stanley Cup Finals. The Avalanche entered the playoffs as the heavy favorite to win the Stanley Cup, having captured the Presidents' Trophy for having the best record in the regular season. Now, they're only one loss from being another footnote in that trophy's infamous "curse".

Hurricanes Edge Habs in OT, Take 2-1 Lead

Carolina won Game 3 in Montreal 3-2 in overtime, taking a 2-1 series lead. Andrei Svechnikov scored 14:04 into OT to give the Hurricanes their second consecutive OT victory.

The stat that defines this game: Montreal had ZERO shots on goal in overtime. Not one. Carolina dominated the extra frame and now has momentum heading into Game 4.
