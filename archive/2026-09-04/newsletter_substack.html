College football is back, and it's already broken

Georgia Tech had the lead, the ball, a chip-shot 45-yarder. A win over Deion Sanders' team on Thursday night to open the season.

Then Boo Carter jumped.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2095721357795328073

Colorado freshman quarterback Julian Lewis hit tight end Charlie Williams for a 20-yard touchdown with 42 seconds left. Buffaloes up 14-13. Then Boo Carter blocked Aidan Birr's 45-yard attempt as time expired.

Georgia Tech: the only school on Earth capable of losing this specific way twice in five years.

https://twitter.com/ClutchPoints/status/2095720533039989027

Rahkeem Smith returned a kick 100 yards for a touchdown 14 seconds after Colorado went up 7-0 — Smith's first touch as a Yellow Jacket, the program's first kick return TD since Jahmyr Gibbs in 2021. A 100-yard opening statement. They had Colorado dead.

Then a 17-play drive died on a blocked field goal by a 6-foot-9 tight end. Then the game-winning field goal died on another block. Georgia Tech had every play they wanted.

https://twitter.com/RedditCFB/status/2095722028758130860

Deion at halftime, per his own retelling: get up there, be that man, we believe in you. Lewis walked into the final drive with 41 yards passing.

Deion survives Week 0. Georgia Tech returns to Atlanta in mourning.

Then, the same day, the SEC sued LSU in U.S. District Court in Alabama. The conference is taking its own member school to federal court over roster construction.

UMass hadn't won in 677 days. Rutgers set off fireworks anyway.

Rutgers paid UMass $1.55 million to travel to Piscataway and lose. UMass, a 29.5-point underdog, coming off an 0-12 season, hadn't beaten anyone in 677 days.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2095683377651167569

UMass won 45-29. FBS teams favored by 29-plus points were 213-0 since 2024. Rutgers' 16-point loss was the fifth-largest by a favorite of 25-plus points since 2000. The largest FBS upset since Kansas defeated Texas as a +31 underdog in 2021, and Rutgers' largest upset loss since the FBS/FCS split in 1978.

UMass head coach Joe Harasymiak was Rutgers' defensive coordinator from 2022 to 2024. His first win as UMass' coach came against his former team.

Rutgers had planned a postgame fireworks show. Then they lost. Then the fireworks went off anyway.
