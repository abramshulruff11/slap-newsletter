Case Keenum, 38, Turned Back the Clock on Monday Night

Case Keenum's last NFL start came on Christmas Eve 2023, when he threw for 62 yards and two picks for the Texans and got benched for Davis Mills. That was 1,009 days ago. Last night he walked into Soldier Field as the third-string emergency option — Caleb Williams (hamstring) out, Tyson Bagent (concussion) not cleared — and dismantled the Eagles 27-7.

Two thousand five hundred seventy-seven days is over seven years. The last time Case Keenum had a game like this, TikTok wasn't in America yet. He now has more passing TDs than Drake Maye this season and more rushing TDs than Saquon Barkley, who is on a long touchdown drought.

Somewhere Jalen Hurts is staring at a wall.

Hurts was out-schemed by his own defense. T.J. Edwards jumped a route because he heard the playcall at the line of scrimmage and picked Hurts off. When Hurts left for concussion protocol, Andy Dalton came in and threw one too. Hurts cleared, came back, and the Eagles still couldn't get out of their own way. The tush push scored as time expired in the first half only because the Bears thought Philly was spiking it.

Nick Sirianni's contribution to the evening was calling Bears linebacker D'Marco Jackson a "pussy" on the sideline after a late hit. Real Big Coach Energy from a team down three scores to Case Keenum.

Ben Johnson, meanwhile, dialed up a full clinic. Luther Burden III scored on the opening drive on an underhand flip from Keenum. Kalif Raymond ran a 41-yard touchdown untouched. And when it was time to put the game away in the fourth quarter, Keenum called his own number for the sneak.

Zero turnovers, zero sacks, two offensive penalties. The 38-year-old third-stringer played the cleanest game of quarterback anyone in the NFC East has seen in a month.

Giants Trade for J.J. McCarthy

With Jaxson Dart's knee ending his season three weeks in, the Giants went shopping. They came home with a 2024 first-round pick for the price of a 2027 fifth.

@TomPelissero
Trade! The Vikings are sending QB J.J. McCarthy to the Giants for a 2027 fifth-round pick, sources tell @ringer @netflixsports.

With Jaxson Dart out for the regular season, New York adds another young QB, while Minnesota gains flexibility and McCarthy gets a fresh start.
View tweet

McCarthy went 10th overall in 2024. Minnesota just moved him for a pick that will be made when he's 26 years old, saving $4.6M in the process. He was 6-4 as a starter with 11 passing TDs against 12 interceptions. The Giants QB room is now Jameis Winston, J.J. McCarthy, and one very expensive rehab stint from Jaxson Dart.

Giants fans processing this in real time:

In the past two decades, the rate of first-round quarterbacks failing to make it three seasons with their draft team has accelerated dramatically. From 2010 to 2024, seven — Tebow, Weeden, Manziel, Rosen, Trey Lance, Pickett, and now McCarthy. From 2000 to 2009, that number was zero.
