Chris Sale, 37, Threw 100 MPH for the First Time Since 2018 and Shut Out the Dodgers

Chris Sale hadn't finished a game in seven years. He hadn't thrown 100 mph in eight. Thursday night at Truist Park he did both. Against the two-time defending champs.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2093152618184892619

9 IP. 0 R. 11 K. 0 BB. 109 pitches. He struck out Shohei Ohtani twice. In the third inning, Sale reached back and fired a 100.1 mph fastball — the first time he'd touched triple digits since 2018.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2093161677294321764

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2093171351917842843

Sixteen years in the league and the man still answers postgame questions like a substitute teacher who wants to end the interview.

At 37 years and 150 days, Sale became just the sixth pitcher since 1900 at that age or older to record 10-plus strikeouts and issue no walks in a shutout. The most recent: Randy Johnson. 2004 and twice in 2001. Sale's childhood idol.

The last time Sale threw a complete game he was in a Red Sox uniform. Since then: Tommy John, a broken rib, a Cy Young at 35. He barely pitched from 2020 through 2023. He shouldn't be doing any of this.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2093152563319263739

The best moment of the whole night wasn't a pitch. It was Sale's son in the stands when he saw dad walking out for the ninth.

https://twitter.com/JomboyMedia/status/2093178430795772083

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2093178738519273723

Every kid in America wants to be Chris Sale's son right now. Every kid in Los Angeles wants to be somebody else's son.

The Braves won this game 1-0 on a leadoff home run and nothing else. First pitch, homer, close the laptop. The Dodgers — with Yamamoto on the mound the same week he threw a perfect game — got to watch their season slip in a nine-inning masterclass and never came close.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2093186153646112993

Cy Young Sale, part two, is not something anyone had on the 2026 bingo card. The guy who couldn't stay healthy for four straight years just hit triple digits on a Thursday in August like it's 2013 again.
