Harry Kane Has Two, England Has Four, Croatia Has Questions

England 4, Croatia 2. Harry Kane scored twice. Jude Bellingham gave England the lead in the second half. Marcus Rashford added a late one off the bench. Standard tournament-opener stuff — except for the part where Croatia went up 2-0 first.

Then England remembered they were England and ripped off four unanswered. Kane has now scored 10 World Cup goals, tying Gary Lineker for most by an English player. He is now 6 behind top-scorers of all time, Lionel Messi and Miroslav Klose.

This was also England's first World Cup win against a side ranked inside FIFA's top 15 since 2002 v Argentina, ending a nine-game winless run at the finals against such sides. A generation-long drought against good teams, ended in 90 minutes.

Ronaldo's Drought Is Now a Geological Event

Congo DR, playing at the World Cup for the first time in 52 years, held Portugal to a 1-1 draw and earned their first-ever World Cup point.

@HaterReport
Ronaldo getting out of bed this morning after doing 90 minutes of cardio against DR Congo
View tweet

The Ronaldo numbers from this match are something else. He played his 6th World Cup with 0 shots on target — his 23rd career World Cup game, tied fourth most in competition history. It was the 2nd time he recorded 0 shots on target in a World Cup match in which he played 90 minutes.

He was held scoreless for the fifth straight World Cup match and the 10th consecutive game in major competitions. He hasn't scored a non-penalty goal in a major competition since June 19, 2021.

That is nearly five years. The last time Cristiano Ronaldo scored from open play at a major tournament, the Tokyo Olympics hadn't happened yet.

He's also now the oldest outfield player to start a World Cup game at 41 years, 123 days. Roberto Martinez was asked if he'd consider pulling him. Martinez brushed aside concerns about Ronaldo's goal-scoring drought following Portugal's frustrating 1-1 draw with Congo DR. Bold strategy.

Congo DR walked away with the point. Ronaldo's last non-penalty major tournament goal continues to age like a fine, increasingly embarrassing wine.

Olivia Miles Drops 31 on 80%

Olivia Miles just put up a career-high 31 points on 12-of-15 shooting against the Sparks. Twenty-four of those came in the first half.

The only other rookie this century with a 30+ point game on 80%+ from the field is Paige Bueckers. The 2026 rookie class is just going to keep doing this to us. Lynx won by 16.

Sparks defenders trying to figure out what just happened:

Trae Young Says No to $48.97 Million

Washington Wizards star Trae Young plans to decline his $48.97 million player option for the 2026-27 season and will become a free agent Monday, sources told Andscape's Marc Spears. Washington remains the front-runner for the four-time All-Star, but multiple teams are expected to have interest.

He's not actually walking away from $49M. Early projections on the likely ballpark for Young's new deal: $120 million over three seasons. Young has until June 23 — Night 1 of the draft — to activate or bypass the player option. He's eligible for a three-year extension worth nearly $160 million until June 30.

Trading one year at $49M for three years at $120M is the kind of math that makes agents very happy.

Here's the absurd part of Washington's situation: Trae played only five games for the rebuilding Wizards last season. Five. And they're about to hand him nine figures.

They also have the No. 1 overall pick and are weighing whether to draft A.J. Dybantsa, Darryn Peterson or Cam Boozer. Add Anthony Davis. Six months ago, Washington was openly tanking. Now it's Trae, AD, the #1 pick, Bilal Coulibaly, Alex Sarr, Kyshawn George — one of the more chaotic franchise pivots in recent memory.

Passan's Trade Deadline Top 100 Drops

Jeff Passan and Kiley McDaniel published their top 100 MLB trade candidates with the August 3 deadline looming. The headliners: Tarik Skubal, Matt Chapman, David Peralta, Luis Arraez.

Skubal at the top of any trade board is the kind of thing that should be illegal. A reigning Cy Young winner under team control being shopped means the Tigers are either doing a full teardown or running the most aggressive leverage play in baseball.

UNC and Oklahoma Punch Tickets to Omaha

North Carolina eliminated West Virginia to reach the College World Series Finals for the first time since 2007. Oklahoma beat Georgia to advance. Best-of-three for the title starts Saturday in Omaha.

UNC at the CWS Finals after a 19-year wait. The roster that won that 2007 berth has aged into actual MLB veterans by now. Anyway — Omaha is set.
