Caleb goes down

Caleb Williams went down in the fourth quarter untouched. No hit. No pressure. Just a QB pulling up with his hamstring, then a cart, then tears. He gave a thumbs up to the crowd on the way out — the kind of thumbs up that tells you it's not fine.

Ben Johnson took the mic afterwards and did the coach thing.

@TomPelissero
Bears coach Ben Johnson says Caleb Williams: "He's in good spirits." Says they won't know the severity until Monday.
View tweet

"Good spirits" is what you say when you've just watched your QB get carted off the field crying and you don't have any actual information yet. Monday is a long way away when your season is a hamstring MRI.

One week ago the Bears hung 59 points on Carolina. Ben Johnson spent the postgame talking Super Bowls. Seven days later they scored three points in Minneapolis and lost their quarterback to his own hamstring.

@BackAftaThis
Maybe Chicago's 59 points last week won't be their worst game of the year after all.
View tweet

59 to 3. Bears fans get one week of joy per decade and they have to spend it worrying about what's coming next.

Jayden's elbow

Jayden Daniels tried to brace a fall on the final play of the first half against Dallas. The elbow went the wrong way. Not a little the wrong way — the wrong way as in bent backwards.

@TomPelissero
Commanders QB Jayden Daniels dislocated his left elbow again, coach Dan Quinn confirmed, adding that an X-ray didn't show a fracture.

More tests to come Monday, but Daniels will be out for some time: "We'll rally around Marcus (Mariota)."
View tweet

No fracture is the only good news in that paragraph. "Rally around Marcus" is the tell. When your head coach is already saying the backup's name at the podium, your starter isn't week-to-week. Washington lost 37-20. It got worse after halftime, and it's going to keep being worse for a while.

Chiefs survive

Chiefs 33, Colts 30. Overtime. Mahomes threw for 382 and three touchdowns and it still took a Butker field goal to get out of the building alive.

Laquon Treadwell went down. Fumble. Reviewed. Down by contact. Chiefs ball. Except it wasn't Chiefs ball because he was down.

@BillBarnwell
Chiefs get all the calls
View tweet

Mahomes admitted after the game he was relieved they didn't rule it a fumble because he didn't want to see what Twitter would do. The timeline had thoughts and he wanted no part of them.

Then the actual crime scene: the Colts got the ball down the field in overtime and kicked a field goal to tie it instead of going for the win.

@KevinOConnor
Cannot believe the Colts played for the tie. Totally predictable play calling after getting it down the field. Coaching scared!
View tweet

You do not play a Patrick Mahomes overtime for the tie. You do not hand the ball back to Patrick Mahomes and hope. Butker walked out and did the rest. Alec Pierce left the stadium in a walking boot.
