Chris Johnson Reveals ALS Diagnosis

Former NFL running back Chris Johnson revealed on Good Morning America that he has been diagnosed with ALS. He is 39 years old and was diagnosed in 2025.

@AdamSchefter
Former NFL running back Chris Johnson revealed to Good Morning America that, at age 39, he has been diagnosed with ALS.
View tweet

Johnson, known as CJ2K, was one of the most electrifying players of his era. In 2009, Johnson won the NFL rushing title with 2,006 yards, becoming the sixth of only nine players ever to rush for over 2,000 yards in a season, and breaking Marshall Faulk's record of total yards from scrimmage with 2,509. He was named the 2009 Offensive Player of the Year. He was selected by the Tennessee Titans in the first round of the 2008 NFL draft, after running a then-record breaking 4.24 seconds in the 40-yard dash at the NFL Combine.

Arnold faces four counts of armed robbery and four counts of kidnapping in connection with a February incident in Tampa. He has categorically denied involvement.
