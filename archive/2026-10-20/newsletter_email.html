Angel Reese Demands the Ball, Drops 30-16 — Dream Even Finals 1-1

Angel Reese told anyone who would listen after Game 1 that she needed the ball. Monday night at Chase Center, the Dream gave it to her.

30 points. 16 rebounds. 3 assists. 2 blocks. Third player to score 30 points and grab 15 rebounds in a Finals game, joining Breanna Stewart (2020) and Jonquel Jones (2019). Youngest player in WNBA history to put up those numbers in a Finals game.

The win gave Atlanta its first victory in WNBA Finals history after 10 straight losses across three previous appearances. Dream fans had never seen a Finals W. Reese handed them one.

She was snarling at the crowd and letting everyone in Ballhalla know a defender who could contain her wasn't in the building. Series tied 1-1. Game 3 Wednesday in Atlanta.
