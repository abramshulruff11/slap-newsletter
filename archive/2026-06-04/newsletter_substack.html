Knicks Steal Game 1 in San Antonio, Brunson Goes Nuclear in the Fourth

The Knicks were down 14 in the second half. In San Antonio. Against Victor Wembanyama. Playing in their first Finals since the Clinton administration.

Jalen Brunson said no.

https://twitter.com/StatMuse/status/2062372503553495281

Thirteen of his thirty in the fourth. The Spurs managed 40 in the second half. In the fourth alone, Knicks 29, Spurs 19. Brunson's dagger was a 15-footer with 38 seconds left. Knicks 105, Spurs 95.

Josh Hart had the strangest stat line of the night while scoring three points.

https://twitter.com/StatMuse/status/2062372659757826545

Game-high rebounds. Game-high assists. Game-high steals. On a court with four seven-footers. From a 6-foot-5 wing who went 1-for-5 from the field.

Hart is the first player with 15-plus rebounds, six-plus assists and four-plus steals in an NBA Finals game since Hall of Famer Larry Bird in Game 3 of 1986 NBA Finals for Boston against the Houston Rockets. Forty years. The last guy to do this had a mustache and was hitting jumpers in Reagan-era Boston Garden.

The streak is doing the unthinkable.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2062373738566320174

https://twitter.com/ESPN/status/2062380459883888742

Forty-one days without a loss. The only team in NBA history with a longer single-postseason streak is the 2017 Warriors, who had Steph, Klay, KD, and Draymond all in their prime. The Knicks have a 6'2 guard from Villanova and a guy who rebounds like he's being paid per board.

KAT had his moment too — 18 and 12, including four offensive boards while sharing the floor with Wembanyama.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2062392743830110627

Wemby finished with 26 and 12 on 21 shots. The switch-everything scheme worked — Mitchell Robinson and KAT in waves, live with contested twos. Wemby did get one moment of pure cinema when a fan rushed the floor.

https://twitter.com/TheNBACentel/status/2062367890066555337

Security in the AT&T Center treating that the way Knicks defenders treated the Spurs lead.

Game 2 is Friday. Still in San Antonio. The Knicks are three wins from their first title since 1973.

Myles Garrett Officially a Ram, and Bill Barnwell Has a Take

Myles Garrett got introduced at the Rams facility Wednesday. He put on the horns. He smiled. Sean McVay talked about the pursuit. And then Bill Barnwell dropped the line that's going to define this trade for the next five years: Garrett may be the best NFL player ever traded in the absolute prime of his career.

Two DPOY awards in three years. Still 30. Now lining up across from Jared Verse's old spot on a defense that already had teeth.
