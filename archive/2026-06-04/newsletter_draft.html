Game-high rebounds. Game-high assists. Game-high steals. On a court with four seven-footers. From a 6-foot-5 wing who went 1-for-5 from the field.

Hart is the first player with 15-plus rebounds, six-plus assists and four-plus steals in an NBA Finals game since Hall of Famer Larry Bird in Game 3 of 1986 NBA Finals for Boston against the Houston Rockets. Forty years. The last guy to do this had a mustache and was hitting jumpers in Reagan-era Boston Garden.
