Josh Allen Is Inevitable

20-for-31. 248 passing yards. 69 on the ground. Three passing touchdowns. Two rushing touchdowns. That's now four career games with three touchdown passes and two rushing touchdowns — no other player in NFL history has more than two, and that "no other player" is Kordell Stewart. Allen has ten total games with 2+ pass TDs and 2+ rush TDs. Nobody else in NFL history has more than four.

He leads the league in passing touchdowns. He also leads the league in rushing touchdowns. Pick a category, he's on top of it.

The setting was the new $2.2 billion Highmark Stadium's first regular season game. Allen scored the first touchdown in the building. Bills fans got a housewarming gift and a laminated MVP campaign flyer at the door.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2100790214402748584

On the other sideline, the Lions defense is officially in crisis. Detroit blew a 21-0 lead against the Saints in Week 1. Tonight they were staring at a 41-10 hole by the third quarter. That's 63 points allowed in six quarters, all without safeties Brian Branch and Kerby Joseph.

https://twitter.com/AlbertBreer/status/2100896171912773860

Ben Johnson took the offensive brain to Chicago in the offseason. Turns out he was also holding the defense together with duct tape and vibes.

Allen also wasn't doing it alone. Dalton Kincaid caught seven for a team-leading 95 yards and now leads all NFL tight ends in receiving through two weeks. James Cook went for 135 rushing yards and a score on 21 carries. Joshua Palmer is quietly averaging a touchdown a week. Joe Brady is dialing up hook-and-ladders and downfield laterals like he found a cheat code.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2100791180111868102

Which brings us to the only question that actually matters in Buffalo:

https://twitter.com/NFLMemes/status/2100785667437449400

Allen is playing the best football of his career. The Bills are 2-0. The offense looks like a video game. And somewhere in a fan's basement, there's a folding table already picked out for late January.

Pitt Is 3-0 and Tied for the Best Record in FBS. The Company Is… Interesting.

Pittsburgh beat Syracuse 27-13 to move to 3-0. That ties them for the best record in FBS.

USC makes sense. North Dakota State is an FCS team. Pitt fans get to brag about being tied with USC and also get tied with a program that isn't technically in their subdivision.

Syracuse spent the night beating themselves. At halftime they had 93 passing yards, 16 rushing yards, and 80 penalty yards. More yards in flags than on the ground.

Jared Young Carted Off After a Scary First Base Collision

Mets first baseman Jared Young collided with pitcher Nolan McLean on a play at first base and had to be carted off the field. He gave a thumbs up on the way out, which is the universal athlete signal for "I'm alive, please stop crying."

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2100750806018293836

CT scan came back negative. He had feeling in his extremities. He's dealing with real concussion symptoms but avoided the scariest outcomes. Real fear early, real relief late. The Phillies won 3-0, but nobody in either dugout was thinking about the scoreboard.

Dodgers Clinch the NL West. Again. Obviously.

The Dodgers beat the Reds 8-2 to lock up the NL West.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2100667131117351353

The only team to unseat them in that stretch was the 2021 Giants, who won 106 games and still finished second. Then the Dodgers knocked them out of the NLDS anyway.

Since divisional play began in 1969, the Dodgers lead all teams with 24 division titles, one more than the Braves and three more than the Yankees. This clinch got a Champagne shower and a passing mention on the ticker. Just another Thursday in September.
