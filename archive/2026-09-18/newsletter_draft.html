Josh Allen Is Inevitable

20-for-31. 248 passing yards. 69 on the ground. Three passing touchdowns. Two rushing touchdowns. That's now four career games with three touchdown passes and two rushing touchdowns — no other player in NFL history has more than two, and that "no other player" is Kordell Stewart. Allen has ten total games with 2+ pass TDs and 2+ rush TDs. Nobody else in NFL history has more than four.

He leads the league in passing touchdowns. He also leads the league in rushing touchdowns. Pick a category, he's on top of it.

The setting was the new $2.2 billion Highmark Stadium's first regular season game. Allen scored the first touchdown in the building. Bills fans got a housewarming gift and a laminated MVP campaign flyer at the door.
