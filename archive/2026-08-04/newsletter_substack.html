Kyle Tucker Came Home. Wrigley Sent Him a Card.

Kyle Tucker played one season on the North Side. Twenty-two homers, an All-Star nod, a playoff run that ended in the NLDS. Then he took Los Angeles's $240 million and left in January.

Tonight was his first trip back to Wrigley.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2084433055339463034

Cubs fans have a specific type of memory. It's the kind that boos a guy who hit 22 homers for them because he took a $60 million AAV somewhere warmer. Fair.

And then the Cubs went out and beat him 10-5 anyway.

Welcome to the Yankees, Luis. Watch This.

The Yankees acquired Luis García Jr. from Washington yesterday — a 25-year-old middle infielder who was slugging .560 with 23 homers for the Nationals. In his first at-bat as a Yankee that mattered, he did this:

https://twitter.com/ClutchPoints/status/2084458373349851496

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2084457247229579593

Then he sat in the dugout and watched the bullpen give up seven runs in the next inning.
