Falcons Announce Passing of Cristina Ulbrich

Cristina Ulbrich, wife of Falcons defensive coordinator Jeff Ulbrich, passed away Sunday after a long battle with cancer. She is survived by Jeff and their children Samantha, Jax, and Jace.

@AdamSchefter
The Atlanta Falcons announced the passing of Cristina Ulbrich, wife of defensive coordinator Jeff Ulbrich. She passed away Sunday after a long illness. The team's statement: "The Atlanta Falcons are heartbroken by the passing of Cristina Ulbrich, beloved wife of defensive coordinator Jeff Ulbrich, after her courageous battle with cancer. "Cristina was a devoted wife, mother, and friend whose strength, grace, and resilience touched all who knew her. She was a constant source of love and support to Jeff, their family, and the broader Falcons community. "On behalf of the entire organization, we extend our deepest condolences to Jeff, their children, and all who loved Cristina. During this incredibly difficult time, our thoughts and prayers are with the Ulbrich family. "We ask that their privacy be respected as they grieve this profound loss, and we stand with them in support and remembrance of Cristina's life."
