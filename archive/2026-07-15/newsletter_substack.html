Spain Rolls France 2-0, Yamal's Aura Gamble Pays Off

Spain is going back to the World Cup final. 37 matches unbeaten in regular time — 28 wins, nine draws since March 2024. France is going to the third-place game in Miami.

https://twitter.com/JomboyMedia/status/2077135948404445597

First final since 2010. Also when they won their only World Cup. One appearance, one trophy, then 16 years of nothing. Now they're 90 minutes away from a second star.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2077135855055982677

Hours before kickoff Lamine Yamal posted three photos on Instagram telling France to be scared. He is 18 years old. He then drew the penalty that opened the scoring.

AL Blanks NL 4-0, First All-Star Game Shutout in 13 Years

The American League shut out the National League in the All-Star Game. First time since 2013.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2077230209850364097

Dylan Cease set the tone in one inning:

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2077197246693572776

Then Cody Bellinger — in as an injury replacement for Aaron Judge (rib fracture) — smacked a two-run single up the middle off Cristopher Sánchez in the top of the first. Ben Rice singled home another run two pitches later. That was the ballgame. Top of the first inning of the All-Star Game.

https://twitter.com/ClutchPoints/status/2077235300221477226

Texans Co-Founder Janice McNair Dies at 89

Janice S. McNair, co-founder and Senior Chair of the Houston Texans, passed away on Tuesday in Houston with her family beside her. She was 89.

Janice and her late husband Bob McNair founded the Texans and returned the NFL to Houston after the Oilers left for Tennessee in 1997. After Bob's death in 2018, Janice assumed the role of Senior Chair and remained one of the most prominent women in NFL ownership.

https://twitter.com/HoustonTexans/status/2077121322996400267

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2077148173873668548

Our condolences to the McNair family and the Texans organization.

Silver: NBA Didn't Pause the Kawhi Trade, Expects Resolution This Summer

The Kawhi Leonard trade between the Clippers and Raptors was agreed to on June 30. It is July 15. The trade is still not official. Nobody knows when it will be. Adam Silver would like everyone to know none of that is the league's fault.

https://twitter.com/ClutchPoints/status/2077219358175363341

Translation: the Raptors were told that if they finalized the trade, they'd inherit any penalties from the Aspiration investigation. They said "we'll wait." Rational. Also functionally the same as pausing it.

https://twitter.com/TomerAzarly/status/2077216532254593376

Every player involved has reported to their "new" team. Scottie Barnes has been in Vegas hanging with the Clippers Summer League squad. Everyone is acting like the trade is done. Legally, it is not.

MLB Backs Players for 2028 Olympics, Now Fighting Over Hotel Rooms

MLB owners finally agreed to let big leaguers play in the Olympics for the first time in modern history. The last hurdle? Hotel room allocation.

https://twitter.com/JeffPassan/status/2077013795239567707

The league and the union are fighting over how many tickets, hotel rooms, and credentials players get for their families. Baseball was cut from the 2028 Olympics, then added back because MLB said it would send its stars. The sport is one credential squabble from blowing up its own moment.
