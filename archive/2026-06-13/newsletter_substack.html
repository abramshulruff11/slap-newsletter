Misiorowski's 15-K Maddux: The Most Efficient Dominance in Baseball History

Jacob Misiorowski threw 95 pitches. He struck out 15. He gave up one hit. He walked nobody. He faced the minimum. He was throwing 103 mph in the ninth inning. Never in MLB history has a pitcher struck out 15 in a shutout that took fewer than 100 pitches.

https://twitter.com/JeffPassan/status/2065617733597675867

A "Maddux" is a complete-game shutout in under 100 pitches, named after Greg Maddux, who treated efficiency like a personal challenge. Greg Maddux himself never struck out 15 in a game. Not in a Maddux, not in 130 pitches, not ever. Misiorowski did it on 95.
