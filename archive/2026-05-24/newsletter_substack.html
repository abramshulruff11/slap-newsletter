Knicks One Win from Finals After Crushing Cleveland in Game 3

Jalen Brunson scored 30 points, Mikal Bridges added 22, and OG Anunoby had 21 as the Knicks demolished Cleveland 121-108 in Game 3. New York leads the series 3-0 and is one win away from their first NBA Finals appearance since 1999. The Knicks have now won 10 straight postseason games.

New York is the seventh team in NBA history to win at least 10 straight during a postseason run. The last team to do it was the Boston Celtics, who went on a 10-game run on their way to the 2024 title. Cleveland, San Antonio and the Los Angeles Lakers have done it twice. All but one of the Knicks' wins have been by double digits, with an average margin of victory of 22.5 points.

https://twitter.com/ClutchPoints/status/2058408502894510357

Their point differential is now +225 across 10 games. The most one-sided 10-game winning streak in playoff history was turned in by the 2016-17 Golden State Warriors, who outscored opponents by 171 during that stretch on their way to that season's title. The Knicks' current differential crushes that record.
No NBA team has ever come back from a 3-0 deficit in the playoffs. Not even an appearance by the superstar couple of Taylor Swift and Cleveland Heights native Travis Kelce could spur the Cavs to beat the Knicks.

The Knicks look fresher than the Cavaliers, who have been playing every other day since Game 5 of their first-round series against the Toronto Raptors. Cleveland has played 13 games since April 29, while New York has played only eight in that span. Game 4 is Monday night in Cleveland. The Knicks can close it out and punch their ticket to face either the Thunder or Spurs.
