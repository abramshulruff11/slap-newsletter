Wild Card Wednesday: Yankees and White Sox Sweep to the ALDS, Phillies Survive

Three wild card series, three different kinds of carnage. The Yankees finished burying Boston. The White Sox finished a 21-year wait. And the Phillies finished the day with a pulse.

https://twitter.com/JeffPassan/status/2105496871494185419

The Chicago White Sox just won their first playoff series since the 2005 World Series. Twenty-one years. The day before Game 1, they got a letter from Pope Leo XIV offering "abundant blessings."

https://twitter.com/JeffPassan/status/2105451966671278115

Gavin Sheets pinch-hit two-run bomb to seal it. Cleveland next — first time these division rivals have ever met in the playoffs. Also: the Pope is now 1-0 as a baseball operations consultant.

Atlanta had a lead in the 8th and the Phillies' season on a string. Then Schwarber and Harper hit back-to-back homers to tie it. Then Alec Bohm hit a go-ahead shot in the 10th. Then JT Realmuto — the most accurate challenge catcher in baseball — iced it with an ABS challenge for the final out.

https://twitter.com/JeffPassan/status/2105407471938580788

Andrew Painter threw three scoreless. The only Wild Card series not ending in a sweep. Series tied 1-1. Game 3 Thursday.

The Red Sox have not scored a run in their last 26 postseason innings. They're hitting .136 with one extra-base hit in 88 at-bats across that span, dating back to last year's Wild Card. The Yankees pitching didn't just beat them — the staff exposed the whole operation.

Arike Ogunbowale Just Set the WNBA Playoff Scoring Record

45 points in a playoff game. The most anyone has ever scored in a WNBA playoff game, period.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2105508260514243039

Faded corner three to tie it late in regulation, then a wild one in OT to extend the lead. Dallas 108, Golden State 100. Series tied 1-1.

Paige Bueckers after the game said "Arike is definitely different." She's right. This is the woman who ended UConn's season in the Final Four with a buzzer-beater and then did it again in the title game. Clutch gene isn't a thing until you watch her and realize it absolutely is.

Mike Tomlin Has Been Building a Minecraft City For 12 Years

The Pittsburgh Steelers head coach has, in his spare time, constructed a Minecraft world that includes a CVS, a 30-story luxury high rise with a lap pool, an indoor/outdoor restaurant, and a wellness center.

Tomlin before Minecraft: one Super Bowl ring, two Super Bowl appearances. Tomlin with Minecraft: zero rings, zero Super Bowl trips. The CVS is cursed.

Steelers Trade Joey Porter Jr. to the Cowboys

Pittsburgh sent Porter to Dallas for a 2028 second-round pick and a 2027 sixth. The Steelers also publicly listed him as "conditioning" and "not injury-related" alongside the back issue he reported. That's a franchise calling its own starting corner a liar on the injury report.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2105602578578870423

https://twitter.com/AlbertBreer/status/2105602775283327050

Tomlin went home to Minecraft.

Dallas now has four defensive starters acquired via trade since August: Kenny Clark, Quinnen Williams, Rashan Gary, and Porter. Two of them (Porter and George Pickens) want big extensions and share an agent — David Mulugheta, who also reps Micah Parsons, the guy Dallas traded away. Jerry Jones is going to pay that man's clients one way or another.

Padres Sweep the Cubs

San Diego beat Chicago 4-1 to finish the sweep. Xander Bogaerts drove in the early run, and 19-year-old Ethan Salas — now the youngest player ever to catch in a postseason game — added a sac fly. Padres get Milwaukee in the NLDS.
