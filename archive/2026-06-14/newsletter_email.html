Aldon Smith Dies at 36

Former 49ers All-Pro defensive end Aldon Smith has died at age 36. The 49ers announced his passing Saturday night. No cause of death has been released.

Smith was the No. 7 overall pick of the 2011 NFL Draft out of Missouri. In his second season in 2012, he set a 49ers franchise record with 19.5 sacks, earned First-Team All-Pro honors, and helped lead the team to Super Bowl XLVII. He reached 30 career sacks in just 27 games, becoming the fastest player in NFL history to hit that milestone, breaking a record previously held by Hall of Famer Reggie White.

He totaled 33.5 sacks in 2011 and 2012, the highest total by any player through their first two seasons in the NFL. His career was later derailed by legal troubles and substance abuse issues. He finished with 52.5 career sacks across six seasons with the 49ers, Raiders and Cowboys.
