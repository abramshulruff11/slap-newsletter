A.J. Brown trade talk won't die

Jalen Hurts said his relationship with A.J. Brown is "really good" at OTAs Wednesday. Nothing has changed since the end of the season. "We're really good."

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2059945408416075875

June 1 is when trade talks ramp up. If traded before then, Brown would leave the Eagles with a massive cap hit. After June 1, Philadelphia can absorb it: $21.8 million in 2026 dead money, $1.55 million in cap savings.

New England is the expected destination. Worst kept secret in the NFL. Brown has been linked to the Patriots for months. Compensation centers around a 2028 first-rounder.

Sánchez breaks 115-year-old record

Cristopher Sánchez just passed Grover Alexander for the longest scoreless streak by any Phillies pitcher since 1893. 44 2/3 consecutive scoreless innings. Alexander's 41-inning streak as a rookie in 1911 is done.

https://twitter.com/SleeperHQ/status/2059802873802977303

44 2/3 innings is seventh-longest single-season streak in the Live Ball Era. Sánchez and Orel Hershiser are the only true starters in MLB history to record an entire scoreless month with at least four starts.

https://twitter.com/JeffPassan/status/2059851153475571846

Murakami first to 20 homers

Murakami crushed his third home run in as many games. 20 on the season. Third White Sox player ever to hit 20 before June. Most by any MLB rookie since 1901.
