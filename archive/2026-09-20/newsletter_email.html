Lane Kiffin Returns To Oxford And Gets The Belt

Lane Kiffin walked back into Vaught-Hemingway Stadium as the head coach of LSU and walked out with a 32-24 loss. Ole Miss led 24-7 at halftime before LSU stormed back to tie it, and then Trinidad Chambliss put a bow on the whole thing.

Chambliss is the quarterback Kiffin himself recruited from a Division II program to Ole Miss last year. Since Kiffin left for LSU, the kid he transformed has gotten better without him.

33 of 48, 363 passing yards, and three total touchdowns against the guy who used to call his plays.

Oxford was ready for him. Fans lined the stairwells. The boos started early and never stopped.

Chambliss faked a handoff and ran 14 yards against the grain, outracing LSU safety Ty Benefield to the left pylon for the go-ahead score with 6:14 left. LSU marched right back down. Sam Leavitt's fourth-down pass got deflected off the helmet of defensive end Kam Franklin, and Jaylon Braxton intercepted it in the end zone to seal it.

Go-ahead touchdown. Game-ending interception setup. In a revenge game. Against the coach who left.

Then Chambliss got the mic postgame and gave the sideline reporter a live-TV "HOTTY F*CKING TODDY." New king of Oxford, crowned in prime time, cursing on ESPN.

Gable Steveson's UFC Debut Ended In 12 Seconds

Gable Steveson — Olympic gold medalist, two-time NCAA champ, Jon Jones protégé, the guy who was supposed to be the next great heavyweight — walked into UFC 331 as a -2000 betting favorite.

He walked out unconscious 12 seconds later.

Sean Sharaf, who went into Saturday with a 0-2 UFC record, both losses coming by knockout, immediately went at Steveson and knocked him out with the second punch he threw — a right hand followed by a devastating left hook to Steveson's chin. Third fastest heavyweight knockout in UFC history.

MLB Had A Walkoff Problem Last Night

Three walkoffs. One night. All of them absurd.

In San Diego, 20-year-old Ethan Salas came off the bench cold and hit a pinch-hit walkoff to beat the Marlins 7-6. Griffin Conine had already tied it in the ninth off Mason Miller, one of the best closers in baseball, just to give Salas the stage.

In Phoenix, the Diamondbacks were celebrating the 25th anniversary of the 2001 World Series team. Randy Johnson and Curt Schilling hoisted the trophy together pregame. The scoreboards were altered to look like 2001. And then Pavin Smith hit a walkoff homer to beat the Yankees 5-3.

The same Yankees they beat for that trophy 25 years ago.

In Anaheim, the Angels were down to their final strike. Vaughn Grissom tied it in the ninth. Christian Moore walked it off in the 11th. 6-5, Halos.

NC State Tried An Intentional Safety And Lost The Game

Vanderbilt 35, NC State 31. Vandy never led all game. Until the final play.

NC State was up 31-28. They lined up in their own end zone to take an intentional safety — give up two points, get better field position on the free kick, force Vandy to drive 60+ yards with no timeouts.

They fumbled the snap. Vanderbilt scooped it up and walked into the end zone. 35-31, ballgame.
