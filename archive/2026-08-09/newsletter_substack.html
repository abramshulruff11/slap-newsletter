Payton Tolle Strikes Out 14, Red Sox Win Their 9th Straight

Boston is 27-3 in their last 30 games.

Payton Tolle — 23-year-old rookie lefty — went six innings, two hits, zero walks, fourteen strikeouts against the A's.

https://twitter.com/JomboyMedia/status/2086075957514023168

The 14 strikeouts were the most by a Boston pitcher 23 or younger since Roger Clemens punched out 20 on April 29, 1986.

Boston is just the 10th team to go 27-3 or better in a 30-game span since 1900. First since Cleveland in 2017.

Two months ago the Red Sox were 32-46. They now have a rookie doing Clemens things and a lineup that hasn't lost since Tuesday.

Jorge Messi, Father and Agent of Lionel Messi, Dies at 68

Jorge Messi, the father and longtime agent of Lionel Messi, died Saturday at the age of 68 at Sanatorio Centro, the medical center in Rosario, Argentina, where he was receiving care. The specific illness and medical cause of death have not been made public at the family's request.

He played a fundamental role in his third son's career, serving as his agent and managing his business affairs. He was the one who traveled with a young Messi to Barcelona in the early 2000s for the trial that would open the doors to La Masia, the club's youth academy. Every contract, every move — Barcelona, PSG, Inter Miami — passed through Jorge.

Concerns about his health became public during the 2026 World Cup in North America, when Lionel Messi became emotional after scoring during Argentina's opening match against Algeria and later said he had been dealing with difficult circumstances away from soccer.

Tributes came from Newell's Old Boys, Barcelona, Real Madrid, PSG, Inter Miami, CONMEBOL, and UNICEF. "My dad was always by my side," Lionel Messi said in a 2007 interview with Radio del Plata. "Sometimes I would lock myself in my room to cry when we arrived in Barcelona, or my dad would do the same without me seeing him."
