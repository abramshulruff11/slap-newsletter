The Canadiens Just Played a Period of NHL Jam

Montreal beat Buffalo 6-3 on Thursday night to take a 3-2 series lead. Five goals in the first 10:15, including two scored nine seconds apart.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2055088983521067351

Captain Nick Suzuki and Jake Evans scored 68 seconds apart late in the second period to give Montreal the lead. Cole Caufield, Josh Anderson, Ivan Demidov and Alexandre Texier also scored. Ivan Demidov scored his first career Stanley Cup playoff goal. 19-year-old Russian rookie moment.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2055095529957208227

Jakub Dobes allowed three goals on the first four shots he faced. Finished with 33 saves. Montreal's top line — Suzuki (goal, two assists), Slafkovsky (three assists) and Caufield — finally delivered. The trio had combined for four goals and five assists in the first four games.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2055095537729204653

Montreal is one win from the semifinal round of the playoffs for the first time since the Covid pandemic altered 2021 playoffs. Game 6 is Saturday night in Montreal.

Mitch Marner Finally Did the Damn Thing

Mitch Marner had a goal and an assist to help Vegas eliminate the Anaheim Ducks with a 5-1 victory in Game 6. After nine postseasons with Toronto labeled a playoff disappointment, Marner leads the Stanley Cup playoffs in scoring with 18 points in 12 games.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2055106125440139661

William Karlsson sent a long pass to Marner. Ducks defenseman Jackson LaCombe tried to check him. Marner stopped at the top of the crease, drawing goalie Lukas Dostal to his right as LaCombe skated by them. Marner brought the puck back through his legs and deposited it into the net for his seventh goal of the postseason. 62 seconds into the game. Between the legs. Elimination game.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2055109319079670061

Marner has scored more goals (seven) in this postseason than he scored in his past three postseasons with Toronto (six). Sometimes you need to get as far away from Toronto as geographically possible.
The Golden Knights advance to face the Colorado Avalanche in the Western Conference Finals. Vegas reaches the third round of the NHL postseason for the first time since they won their lone Stanley Cup championship in 2023.
