Cubs Sweep Dodgers, PCA-Ohtani Duel at Wrigley Becomes MVP Referendum

The Cubs just swept the Dodgers at Wrigley. Pete Crow-Armstrong and Shohei Ohtani both hit leadoff. Both went 3-for-5. Both hit two home runs. Both drove in multiple runs. The MVP race, in a single box score.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2085115517816168800

Ohtani led off the finale by tattooing the seventh pitch of the game 110.2 mph over the right-field wall. PCA answered in the bottom half by teeing off on the first pitch he saw from Eric Lauer — 90.2 mph fastball — and sent it 420 feet to center.

https://twitter.com/BarstoolBigCat/status/2085115584237166704

The PCA company he's keeping is not normal.

https://twitter.com/BetweenTheNums/status/2085121672349094008

PCA leads MLB in WAR at 7.0. He's the first player in Cubs history to string together consecutive 25-HR/25-SB seasons — a franchise that has employed Sammy Sosa, Andre Dawson, and Kris Bryant.

The Dodgers have dropped six straight. Swept in back-to-back series by the Cubs and Red Sox.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2085115418801258805

A division lead that was once 14 games could be down to eight. They traded for Tarik Skubal — the reigning two-time AL Cy Young — and immediately lost his debut.

Barstool's diagnosis was more direct.

https://twitter.com/BarstoolBigCat/status/2085109939089858712

PCA, asked directly about the MVP race:

https://twitter.com/JesseRogersESPN/status/2085127486317187531

Humble, self-aware, "I really should go get in the lab and start pitching." He's outplaying Ohtani AND casually joking about becoming Ohtani.

Dillon Brooks Cashes In: 3 Years, $73M to Stay in Phoenix

Phoenix locked down Dillon Brooks on a three-year, $73 million extension through 2029-30.

https://twitter.com/ShamsCharania/status/2085175910064374119

Two years ago the entire NBA internet was calling this guy the most delusional player alive. He got ejected in a playoff series, told LeBron he owned him, then LeBron dropped 40 on him. Now he's a "key part of a two-way, hard-nosed culture."

The Suns are running a strategy here. Continuity.

https://twitter.com/ShamsCharania/status/2085176433102524668

After the Durant-Beal-CP3 experiment ended in a smoking crater, Phoenix has quietly assembled the most annoying-to-play-against roster in the West. Brooks, Bridges, Goodwin — every opponent is going to leave the arena needing a shower.

Aaron Judge Cleared for Light Activity, Vows to Return This Season

Aaron Judge — three-time AL MVP, current human load-bearing wall of the Yankees offense — has been cleared for light physical activity and says he'll be back this year. He hasn't played since May 31.

The Yankees lost three straight to the White Sox last week. Every day without Judge, the AL East feels a little further away.
