Chiefs OC Eric Bieniemy's Wife Shot at Home, Son Arrested

Mia Bieniemy, wife of Kansas City Chiefs offensive coordinator Eric Bieniemy, was shot Sunday night at the couple's home in Ashburn, Virginia. She is hospitalized in stable condition.

@espn
The wife of Chiefs offensive coordinator Eric Bieniemy was shot at the couple's home in Virginia, sources told @ByNateTaylor. The Chiefs said in a statement they are "aware of the incident involving Eric Bieniemy's family," but did not provide further details. More: http://spr.ly/6015BEaTQb
View tweet

Loudoun County Sheriff's Office spokesperson Leah Paul said police responded to a report of a shooting at a home on the 20000 block of Northpark Drive in Ashburn, Virginia, at 7:32 p.m. ET Sunday. Mia Bieniemy was shot twice in the upper body and underwent surgery, but a portion of a projectile remains lodged inside her body, according to the criminal complaint.

@AdamSchefter
Elijah Zion Bieniemy, 27, was arrested and charged with malicious wounding, use of a firearm in commission of a felony, and discharge of a firearm inside of a dwelling, according to the Loudoun County Sheriff's Office and @ByNateTaylor. Elijah Bieniemy is being held without bond at a detention center in Loudoun County, according to police. Eric Bieniemy left the Chiefs' training camp and was not in attendance for Monday's practice. He was with the Chiefs on Sunday in St. Joseph, Missouri, at the campus of Missouri Western State for their second practice of training camp.
