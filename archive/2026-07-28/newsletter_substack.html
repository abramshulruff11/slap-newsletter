Chiefs OC Eric Bieniemy's Wife Shot at Home, Son Arrested

Mia Bieniemy, wife of Kansas City Chiefs offensive coordinator Eric Bieniemy, was shot Sunday night at the couple's home in Ashburn, Virginia. She is hospitalized in stable condition.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2081747288347566184

Loudoun County Sheriff's Office spokesperson Leah Paul said police responded to a report of a shooting at a home on the 20000 block of Northpark Drive in Ashburn, Virginia, at 7:32 p.m. ET Sunday. Mia Bieniemy was shot twice in the upper body and underwent surgery, but a portion of a projectile remains lodged inside her body, according to the criminal complaint.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2081759948912955772

Andy Reid addressed reporters after Monday's practice.

https://twitter.com/ByNateTaylor/status/2081764990697046396

Reid did not know how long Bieniemy would be away from the team. Pass game coordinator Joe Bleymaier and offensive line coach Andy Heck will take over his duties as the Chiefs continue with offensive installations during training camp. The investigation is ongoing. Our thoughts are with the Bieniemy family.

Marlins End Franchise-Worst 12-Game Skid With Walk-Off

Miami had not won a baseball game since July 9. Twelve straight losses. Longest skid in franchise history. Down 7-5 heading into the bottom of the ninth against the Phillies.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2081916555219120280

Jhoan Duran entered with a two-run lead, no outs. He gave up three ground-ball singles, plunked a batter to load the bases, and walked out with a blown save on a walk-off.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2081917830056870066

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2081917504419463285

The Marlins had done plenty of winning before the All-Star break and were at 52-42 and three games behind NL East-leading Atlanta. They also had the third NL wild-card spot. Then the wheels came off. For two weeks.

Clayton McCullough before the game, describing the vibe:

https://twitter.com/ClutchPoints/status/2081918256315785679

"I feel a bit like Odysseus. Like I'm away from home for 10 years. At some point, I'm going to return home. I don't know what baseball god I infuriated but it's where we're at, so today is an opportunity."

McCullough quoting Homer in a postgame press conference to describe a two-week drought.
