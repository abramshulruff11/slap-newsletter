Brett Howden put Vegas back up 81 seconds into the third. Shayne Gostisbehere tied it 4-4 at 11:19. Then with 3:24 left, Tomas Hertl scored the go-ahead goal off a give-and-go play with Colton Sissons. Snap shot, top corner, past Andersen.

Vegas became the first road team in NHL history to stage a multigoal comeback win in Game 1 of the Stanley Cup Final. Fifty-five tries. Zero successes. Until a team nine years old walked into Raleigh and flipped the script.

Carolina's last Cup Final was 2006. They won it. Twenty years later, two goals in thirteen minutes, then the building went silent.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2062007839065776445

Game 2 is Thursday. Same building. Vegas has won seven straight, swept the Presidents' Trophy Avalanche, and stole home-ice from a series they're not even hosting.
