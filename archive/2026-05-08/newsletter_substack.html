The Bears Tax: Chicago's Opponents Drew 400 Fewer Penalty Yards Than Anyone Else

Chicago's opponents were called for 678 penalty yards in 2025. Next closest: Detroit at 701. The Cowboys and Giants: 1,142 and 1,143.

464 yards. The gap between Chicago and Dallas.

Kansas City — the team that leads the league in its own penalties — drew 969 yards from opponents. 291 more than the Bears.

Caleb Williams spent his rookie season behind an offensive line that got zero help. Every third-and-long that should've been bailed out by defensive holding? Didn't happen. Every chop block? Happened constantly.

If the Bears got 140 more opponent penalty yards — still bottom-10 — that's two touchdown drives. Two games flip. Maybe they make the playoffs.

Instead: 678 yards. 23 fewer than Detroit. 464 fewer than Dallas.
