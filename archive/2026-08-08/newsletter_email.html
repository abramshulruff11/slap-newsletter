Red Sox Win 9th Straight Behind Payton Tolle's 14-K Gem, Now 27-3 in Last 30

Payton Tolle just carved his name into a list that only has one other Red Sox rookie on it. And that rookie's name was Roger Clemens.

@TalkinBaseball_
Here are all 14 Payton Tolle strikeouts!
View on Twitter →

14 strikeouts. Six innings. No walks. 13-1 final over the Athletics. Tolle's 14 strikeouts are tied for the second-most in a game by an American League rookie over the last 50 years, trailing only Roger Clemens, who fanned 15 in August 1984. Half a century of AL rookies. One name in front of him.

The line gets more absurd the longer you look at it. Only MLB pitcher since pitch tracking started in 1988 to record 14+ strikeouts in a game on fewer than 85 pitches. Most by a Red Sox pitcher, ever, in a six-inning start. 24 swings and misses — most by a Red Sox pitcher in more than three years.

Payton Tolle deciding whether to strike out the side or work on his cutter between pitches:

The Clemens shadow gets darker. Tolle became the second pitcher in Red Sox history, age 23 or younger, to record 12+ strikeouts without walking a batter. The other? Roger Clemens in the first of his two 20-strikeout games in 1986 against Seattle.

27-3 in a 30-game stretch. Seventh team since 1920 to win 27 or more in a 30-game span. Only one to begin the run under .500.

Every other team that did this was already good and then got hotter. Boston was mediocre and then decided to stop losing baseball games entirely.

Even the guy catching Tolle is telling reporters this feels like catching a Cy Young winner. Jake Rogers, acquired from the Orioles earlier this week in the Adley Rutschman blockbuster, enjoyed being on Tolle's side this time in his first start for his new team. Rogers said it reminded him of a different power lefty he used to catch in Detroit named Tarik Skubal. "That's how it felt tonight."
