Football Is Back and the Hall of Fame Game Actually Ripped

The Hall of Fame Game is usually the football equivalent of a soft launch. Backups, camp bodies, a Thursday night ESPN slot, and a stadium half-full of people who mostly want the fireworks after. This year it was a 33-30 thriller that ended with a rookie scrambling into the end zone as the clock hit zero.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2085564546639347919

Haynes King, an undrafted rookie out of Georgia Tech, capped off a 13-play, 65-yard game-winning touchdown drive with a five-yard scramble as time expired. Cardinals up 30-27 with 1:55 left. King took charge. Two minutes, one drive, walk-off touchdown. In August.

https://twitter.com/BarstoolBigCat/status/2085562677804335320

The other quarterback in this game was also doing quarterback things. Cardinals rookie Carson Beck made his NFL debut and looked like he'd been doing it for years.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2085549602950775003

https://twitter.com/SharpFootball/status/2085530819674705920

11.2 yards per attempt. In your first 20 minutes of pro football.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2085533194011459845

Schefter has been watching football since the Ford administration. When he says he doesn't recall it, he doesn't recall it.

Game tied 17-17 at halftime. Both offenses forgot they were supposed to be running vanilla install-1 concepts and just started cooking.

https://twitter.com/SharpFootball/status/2085540660388503597

The people who skipped this now have to live with the fact that a Georgia Tech rookie with no draft capital just outdueled a first-round pick and won it on the last snap of an exhibition game. They can never get that back.

https://twitter.com/PFTCommenter/status/2085562925431873760

King finished 21-of-34 for 180 yards and two touchdowns, plus 39 rushing yards and a score on three carries. NFL.com noted that if you're looking for a closer stylistic match to starter Bryce Young, it might be King. Panthers fans who came in expecting Kenny Pickett to settle the backup job:

Football is back. It's stupid and it's August and none of the starters played and it doesn't matter. Football is back.

Chelsea Gray Buries the Fever Twice in the Same Game

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2085539276339524025

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2085540541870002380

Gray is the first player in WNBA history to make a game-tying buzzer-beater at the end of regulation and a game-winning buzzer-beater at the end of overtime in the same game.

Aces won 86-84. Gray scored five points through the first 39 minutes and 59 seconds. The last twenty seconds were hers.

Las Vegas erased a huge deficit by closing the first half on a 15-0 run to cut a 42-22 hole to 42-37. Jackie Young scored the final 13 points of the half. This was in Indianapolis, in front of a home crowd that watched Gray erase them twice.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2085530737025933495

Clark had 20 points and eight assists. She also missed two free throws with seven seconds left in regulation up three, then lost the ball with seven seconds to go in overtime with Indiana ahead by one. Eight turnovers total. Every one of those mistakes gave Gray the runway to do what Gray did.

Jahmyr Gibbs Is Now the Highest-Paid Running Back in Football

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2085479269803651204

$22.5M per year for a running back. Three years ago that number would have sounded like a prank.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2085477707249320397

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2085478203150225468

One $20M-a-year back on the planet in May. Three by August. The Analytics Twitter guys who spent a decade tweeting "never pay a running back" are watching their entire ideology get run over in a single week.

This is also the last piece of a Lions offseason that looked like they raided their own vault. Gibbs 3/$67.5M. Hutchinson 4/$180M. Goff 4/$212M. St. Brown 4/$120M. Sewell 4/$112M. Jameson Williams 3/$80M. Campbell 4/$81M. Alim McNeill 4/$97M. Kerby Joseph 4/$85M. Detroit didn't extend a core. They extended a whole roster.

The Super Bowl window is either wide open or about to slam on their salary cap. Probably both.

Red Sox Win Eight Straight, Now 31-5 Since June 25

Boston beat the White Sox in 13 innings to complete a sweep. They erased five separate deficits to do it. Since June 25 they are 31-5. Every other team in baseball is at least seven games behind that pace.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2085497747944222736

Kurkjian doesn't do hyperbole. When he says dangerous in October, he means dangerous in October.

Red Sox fans, who spent most of the last four years staring at the standings like a hostage tape, are now allowed to look up.

Mike Krukow Announces Retirement After 36 Years in the Giants Booth

https://twitter.com/JeffPassan/status/2085412448765428212

Kruk and Kuip have been calling Giants games together longer than most Giants fans have been alive. Three championships, Bonds chasing 756, Lincecum's no-hitters, Buster Posey's entire career — all of it in the same two voices. If you grew up in the Bay Area, summer sounds like Kruk.

He'll finish out the 2026 season, so there's still time for the standing ovations at every road stadium. But this is the end of one of the last true institutional voices in the sport.
