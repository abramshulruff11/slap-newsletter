One Out Away

Dylan Cease got 26 outs. He needed 27.

The Blue Jays ace carried a no-hitter into the ninth inning against the Giants on Wednesday. Heliot Ramos led off with a clean single to center. Standing ovation, ball to the manager, dugout. That was it.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2074981700845600792

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2074980128895963622

This would have been the first Blue Jays no-hitter since Dave Stieb in 1990. Thirty-six years. Cease's second — he threw one for the Padres in 2024. First solo no-hitter since Blake Snell, eight days after Cease's.

Instead:

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2074981481647100317

Second no-hitter lost in the ninth. The 2022 one was broken up by Luis Arraez with two outs — and Arraez, now San Francisco's second baseman, was the hitter after Ramos on Wednesday.

- "I was really commanding the ball well, mixing the ball well. I'm just happy it wasn't Luis who broke it up again."

Wait, that one ran last week. Swap it.

Manager John Schneider let him throw a career-high 118 pitches. His reasoning:

- "I think if a guy has a chance to throw a no-hitter, I think you let him do it. If I can let a player have that opportunity, I'm going to do it every single time."

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2074980964158058641

Leads the AL in strikeouts by 17. Missed two starts. Leads the majors in K/9. 15 innings, zero runs, 20 strikeouts in July. 26 outs before a line-drive single ended it.

https://twitter.com/SleeperHQ/status/2074981272460648570

That tweet felt like a joke on Tuesday. On Wednesday it feels like a weather forecast.

Meanwhile, in Pittsburgh, a Manager Was Chasing Down His Own Pitcher

Same day, different pitcher, opposite decision.

Six perfect innings. Pulled for pitch count. Second time in four days a manager yanked a pitcher during a perfect game bid — the Marlins pulled Eury Perez after seven perfect on Sunday and their bullpen immediately gave up a grand slam.

The Blue Jays let Cease throw 118 pitches chasing history. The Pirates pulled Jones at 6 innings. Nobody knows where the middle is.

Pete Crow-Armstrong Is Doing This On Purpose Now

PCA hit two homers off the Orioles Wednesday night. Both off splitters.

https://twitter.com/JesseRogersESPN/status/2075011341140492603

Splitters dive at the plate. Nobody hits them for power, let alone twice in a night. The last guy to do it was Manny Machado five years ago.

- .297/.385/.544, 21 HR, 23 SB, 5.8 fWAR — leads all big-league hitters
- On pace for another 30/30 season

Justin Verlander Will Retire After the 2026 Season

https://twitter.com/JeffPassan/status/2074903765946171490

Three Cy Young Awards. Two World Series championships. Ten All-Star selections. A no-hitter in 2007, another in 2011, a third in 2019.

Verlander is 43. He made one start this year. He's finishing his career back in Detroit, where it began 21 years ago. Rob Manfred named him to this year's AL All-Star team as a Legend Pick — the honor MLB reserves for careers that deserve one more curtain call.

He'll be a first-ballot Hall of Famer in 2032. There's a real argument he was the best pitcher of a generation that also produced Kershaw and Scherzer.
