USMNT Beats Bosnia 2-0, Advances to Round of 16 Down a Man

The United States men's national team won a World Cup knockout game for the second time ever.

https://twitter.com/ESPNFC/status/2072502182385963339

The USMNT have made six World Cup knockout rounds in the modern era and have won just two matches at this stage — 2002 and 2026.

And they had to do this one down a man for the last 26 minutes.

Celtics Trade Jaylen Brown to the 76ers

https://twitter.com/ShamsCharania/status/2072443555113468324

Boston sent out: Jaylen Brown, who three months ago was finishing his best season as a pro, averaging 28.7 points per game and taking a leap from All-Star to All-NBA player in Tatum's absence while leading the Celtics to the East's No. 2 seed. He finished sixth in MVP voting this past season. He won Finals MVP two years ago.

Boston received: Paul George, 36 years old, coming off two of the worst seasons of his career, plus two firsts and two seconds.

Yankees Lose Seventh Straight, Get Swept by Tigers

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2072419321238729149
