Wemby Declares War in Oklahoma City

Victor Wembanyama just put up 41 points, 24 rebounds, and 3 blocks in double overtime. In Game 1 of the Western Conference Finals. On the road. Against the defending champions.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2056581513521639594

At 22 years old, Wembanyama is the youngest player in NBA history with a 40-20 playoff game. He's the seventh player ever to post a 40-20 game in the Conference Finals or later, joining Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Shaquille O'Neal, Charles Barkley, Moses Malone, Elgin Baylor, and Wilt Chamberlain.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2056580397178196161

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander received his second MVP trophy in a pregame ceremony. Wemby watched. Then delivered a statement.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2056606997542297745

26.3 seconds left in the first overtime. Thunder up by three after a 7-0 run. Wembanyama stopped around 27 feet from the basket — near the center logo — and hit a three to tie the game at 108.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2056577531239637251

The shot came from nearly the exact same spot Stephen Curry hit his legendary 2016 dagger on that very court.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2056609567551668597

Thunder fans watching that ball leave his hands:

First Western Conference Finals game to go to double overtime since 1976. Oklahoma City entered the Conference Finals undefeated in the playoffs, having swept the first two rounds. The Spurs had already beaten them four times in five regular-season games.

Now OKC is down 1-0 in the series. At home.

https://twitter.com/WorldWideWob/status/2056576060054217027

Dylan Harper — starting in place of injured De'Aaron Fox — put up 24 points, 11 rebounds, 6 assists, and 7 steals. First player since Larry Bird with 20+ points, 10+ rebounds, 5+ assists, and 5+ steals in a Conference Finals game. Harper is a rookie.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2056581570643787961

Alex Caruso came off the bench with 31 points and hit 8 threes. Most points off the bench in a Conference Finals game since Tyler Herro in 2020. Wemby had 41-24. Harper had 24-11-6-7. Caruso had 31. It took double overtime to put the Thunder away.

https://twitter.com/coleadamss/status/2056581321388953941

https://twitter.com/WorldWideWob/status/2056577175802028466

The Spurs' starting five: Wemby (22), Harper (20), Stephon Castle (21), Keldon Johnson (25), Devin Vassell (25). Youngest starting lineup in Conference Finals history. They just stole homecourt from the defending champs.

OKC thought they were building the next dynasty:

https://twitter.com/BarstoolBigCat/status/2056580519513501979

Game 2 is Wednesday in Oklahoma City. Good luck.

Canadiens Survive Epic Series, Advance to ECF After Game 7 OT Win

Montreal beat Buffalo 3-2 in overtime in Game 7. Alex Newhook scored the winner 11:22 into the extra period.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2056568978193998191

Second straight series-clinching Game 7 goal for Newhook. He also scored the winner in Game 7 against Tampa Bay in the first round.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2056576472018530738

Montreal reaches the Eastern Conference Finals for the first time since 2021. They'll face Carolina, who swept the first two series and have been off since May 9.

Buffalo fought back from 2-0 down in the first period. Rasmus Dahlin tied the game 6:27 into the third to force overtime. The Sabres erased a 3-1 series deficit just to get to Game 7. Then lost it at home in sudden death.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2056569392909918370

Buffalo fans watching Newhook score his second Game 7 OT winner of the postseason:

https://twitter.com/PatMcAfeeShow/status/2056568412587860241

Buffalo won their first Atlantic Division title and ended a 14-season playoff drought. They're now 1-7 in Game 7s. 1-3 in overtime.

A's Pitcher Loses No-Hitter and Game in Span of Four Pitches

J.T. Ginn took a no-hitter into the 9th inning against the Angels. The game was 0-0. Adam Frazier broke it up with a single with zero outs. Four pitches later, Zach Neto hit a walk-off homer. Angels won 2-1.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2056585905209508157

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2056586581012553909

About 90 seconds. Ginn went from pitching history to devastating loss faster than you can microwave popcorn.

MLB still hasn't seen a no-hitter since September 4, 2024, when three Cubs pitchers combined to no-hit the Pirates. The 2025 season was the first full season without a no-hitter since 2005.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2056587758018187625

Aaron Rodgers Officially Back: Signs 1-Year Deal, Shows Up at 7am for OTAs

Pittsburgh signed Aaron Rodgers to a one-year contract worth up to $25 million. He showed up at the Steelers facility at 7am before the first OTA practice.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2056393866723405876

Rodgers has a reputation for… not doing that. 22nd NFL season. 42 years old. Showing up early to voluntary workouts.

Part of the deal: Rodgers stayed away until now to give Mike McCarthy extended time to evaluate Will Howard. McCarthy apparently sees something in Howard.

https://twitter.com/AlbertBreer/status/2056441150509310121

Rodgers is betting $25M that he has something left. The Steelers are betting the same. This is either a redemption arc or a victory lap.
