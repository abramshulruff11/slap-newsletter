America's Soccer Team Just Won Group D

The United States Men's National Team beat Australia 2-0 in Seattle. Paraguay beat Turkey 1-0 a few hours later. The USMNT won Group D.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2068198929804501260

The United States have won their group three times in World Cup history. 1930. 2010. 2026. That's the list.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2068077355608719695

Babe Ruth was still playing baseball the last time the United States won consecutive World Cup games.

Australia put one in their own net. Alex Freeman headed in a second that needed a VAR review longer than a Marvel post-credits sequence. When it stood, Seattle erupted.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2068058774795874721

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2068109696083329428

Earthquake sensors picked up the crowd reaction. Pulisic watched from the bench with a calf injury. Alex Freeman — son of former NFL WR Antonio Freeman — became the youngest USMNT scorer since Julian Green in 2014. 21 years, 314 days old. NFL bloodlines came through.
