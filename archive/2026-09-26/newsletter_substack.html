Steph Stays Home

Steph Curry signed a 2-year, $116M extension with the Warriors. He took $20M less than his max to give Golden State cap space next summer.

https://twitter.com/ShamsCharania/status/2103673135115517986

Twenty million dollars is not a hometown discount. Twenty million dollars is a house. Twenty million dollars is a small country's defense budget. Steph looked at the max number, subtracted a Bugatti dealership, and slid the paper back across the table.

https://twitter.com/ClutchPoints/status/2103686957994012717

Nobody finishes where they started anymore. Player empowerment, super-teams, the annual trade demand cycle. Steph is going for year 19 and possibly 20 in the same jersey.

https://twitter.com/KevinOConnor/status/2103673204606705830

Duncan. Dirk. Kobe. One franchise, one legacy, walk out the door on your own terms. Steph just RSVP'd.

The cap space isn't just a nice gesture — it's a runway. Golden State now has real flexibility next summer to add another piece around Steph, Butler, and Kuminga.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2103674621593632810

Cleveland Claws Back

Guardians 12, Royals 9. Cleveland pulled within a half game of the White Sox in the AL Central after nine innings of complete chaos. David Fry hit a two-run go-ahead shot in the fifth. Carter Jensen answered with two homers of his own for Kansas City, including one to tie it in the eighth. Then Cleveland walked it off in the ninth with the bases loaded.

Carter Jensen having a two-homer night in a division race game is the kind of breakout that shows up on baseball reference pages for decades. He picked the wrong game to have it in.

Cleveland has not won a World Series in decades. Half a game back with a weekend to play.

Race to .500

The AL West is going to hand out a playoff spot to a team with a losing record.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2103705210547790250

Christian Walker hit a go-ahead homer for the Astros. Then the Athletics answered with a five-run sixth inning — including a two-run triple — and beat Houston 6-5. The team that wins this division gets home-field advantage in October at 80-82.

Every Astros fan planning their playoff watch party:

Houston spent more money than most small nations to fight Oakland for the right to lose in the Wild Card round at 80 wins.

Snakes Alive

Arizona 11, San Diego 4. Jordan Lawlar homered. Nolan Arenado hit his 30th of the year. And while the Diamondbacks were dismantling the Padres, the Phillies were getting shut out by the Rays 2-0.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2103653487301234981

Arizona is the team that ended the Phillies' NLCS run in 2023. The same franchise is now stalking them for a playoff spot.

Phillies fans watching the standings this week:

Arenado's 30th is the story inside the story. Written off for years, hitting 30 again in September while carrying a playoff push. The Cardinals traded him and this is what he does in Arizona.
