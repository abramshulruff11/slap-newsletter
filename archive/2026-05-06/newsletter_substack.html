https://twitter.com/espn/status/2051804962816921808

Jaylen Brown Fined $50K for Crying About Refs on Twitch

The NBA fined Jaylen Brown $50,000 on Tuesday for comments he made on a Twitch livestream on May 3 after the Celtics lost Game 7 to the 76ers.

https://twitter.com/ShamsCharania/status/2051819552716013857

Brown went on Twitch after being eliminated and unloaded on the refs. Said they "clearly had an agenda." Said they told him before the series they'd call offensive fouls on him every time. The Celtics blew a 3-1 series lead. Brown got called for 10 offensive fouls in the series — more than double anyone else. So he went on Twitch and accused the refs of targeting him. Fifty thousand dollars. Jaylen Brown after getting the fine notification:
Brown's second fine this season. He was fined $35,000 in January for criticizing officials after a loss to the Spurs. The NBA bumped it to $50K because he's a repeat offender. You can say the refs were bad. You cannot say they had a premeditated agenda to target you specifically. That's the line. Brown crossed it. The Celtics led 3-1 and lost three straight to get eliminated at home. Instead of taking accountability, Brown went online and blamed the refs.
