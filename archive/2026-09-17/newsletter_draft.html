The White Sox Just Blew a 4.5-Game Lead in 17 Days

On August 30, the Chicago White Sox were nine games over .500 with a 4.5-game lead in the AL Central. Seventeen days later, they are out of first place after going 3-14.

Since August 30, 167 players in Major League Baseball have 50+ plate appearances. The three worst hitters by batting average all play for the same team. Colson Montgomery, Braden Montgomery, Munetaka Murakami — 165, 166, 167. A clean sweep of the bottom three.

Tommy Pham had one plate appearance for the White Sox all season before Wednesday. He was on the IL with a calf strain. Manager Will Venable said Monday he'd "be shocked" if Pham came back for the Cleveland series. Then, late Tuesday night, shortly after a 7-6 walk-off loss, Pham found himself sharing an elevator with general manager Chris Getz. Pham told him he was ready. "If I didn't see him on the elevator, I probably would have had to wait until this weekend."

Twelve hours later he was batting cleanup in the biggest game of the season.

Pham, activated off the IL hours after the elevator conversation, launched a go-ahead three-run homer in the fifth inning. A 412-foot bomb to left-center on an 80-mph curveball. First hit of the entire 2026 campaign.

The White Sox lost 6-3 anyway. Cleveland moved into first place.

Messi's 100th Inter Miami Goal Was a Header

Lionel Messi has scored 100 goals for Inter Miami. The 100th one was a header. If you've watched Messi for two decades, you know how strange that sentence is. He's built a career on his left foot and his brain. Headers are the appetizer he never eats.

Miami won the Campeones Cup final against Cruz Azul 2-0. Another trophy for the cabinet, another category of goal added to a résumé that already had everything.
