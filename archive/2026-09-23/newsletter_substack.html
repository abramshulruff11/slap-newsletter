The Giants' season just ended in an MRI machine

Jaxson Dart's knee was supposed to be a sprained MCL. Then the MRI happened.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2102467323315560526

Rewind eleven days. Week 1 of the 2026 season, Dart completed 23 of 29 pass attempts for 230 yards with three touchdowns and no turnovers. Beat the Cowboys. Giants 1-0. Franchise QB unlocked. The rebuild was working.

Monday night in LA, on the opening drive, Dart dropped back to pass and jumped to make a throw downfield while under pressure. He was sandwiched between Rams linebackers Josaiah Stewart and Byron Young before falling to the ground holding his left knee.

Giants fans getting exactly one week of hope:

Jameis Winston in relief Monday night: 11 of 27 for 111 yards and an interception. That's 4.1 yards per attempt.

And now he's the starter. The Giants have no other quarterbacks on the 53-man roster. Jake Haener is on the practice squad.

Two eras of the Giants QB room:

Eli started over 200 consecutive games across more than a decade. Never missed a game because of injury. Since he walked away: Daniel Jones, Tyrod Taylor, Tommy DeVito, Drew Lock, Dart, and now Jameis. In a calendar year.

Kawhi left $18M on the table to finish his career in Toronto

Kawhi Leonard just did something Kawhi Leonard never does: he made a loud move.

He signed a 2-year, $115M extension with the Raptors. He took $11M less than the max. Then he waived a $7.45M trade kicker on top of that. Add it up: $18.45M in real, spendable, Ferrari-buying money he chose not to take, specifically so Toronto's front office could add pieces around him.

And the reason is not subtle.

https://twitter.com/ShamsCharania/status/2102476667625324965

Full circle. Won a title in Toronto in 2019. Left for the Clippers. Five injury-plagued years in LA, one federal investigation into cap circumvention later, back in Toronto at 35 on a deal that runs through age 38. He's not chasing a ring on a min contract in Miami. He's cementing where he wants the plaque to say he played.

The Rays are AL East champs and Aaron Judge still isn't back

Drew Rasmussen took a no-hitter into the seventh against the Yankees. In the Bronx. To clinch the division.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2102570874264326619

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2102581758117974108

One of the smallest payrolls in the sport. A 21-year-old Junior Caminero leading the league in home runs. The Rays do this every few years and everyone acts surprised every time.

Meanwhile, in the other dugout: Aaron Judge is not walking through that door next week. The Yankees confirmed he won't be available for the Wild Card series. Highest payroll in baseball, home-field lost, MVP shelved, and the division belongs to Tampa Bay.
