Cale Makar Just Became the NHL's First $20 Million Man

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2093453624248590455

Eight years. $163.2 million. The NHL has its first $20 million man. Total contract is the largest in league history — just below the $20.8M cap maximum. Two-time Norris winner, Conn Smythe winner, Stanley Cup champion. Also a defenseman.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2093452220993843516

The gap between Makar and Kaprizov is $27.2 million. Kaprizov signed his record deal last September and just watched it get vaporized.

Makar just became the highest-paid athlete in the entire NHL. He would be the 10th-highest-paid QB in the NFL, slotting right between Jalen Hurts ($51M) and Lamar Jackson ($52M). He'd make 29% of what Shohei Ohtani averages. In the NBA, $20.4M gets you rotation guard money.

This is the fourth time in 11 months the NHL AAV record has been broken. Kaprizov set it at $17M last September. Leo Carlsson pushed the Ducks to $18M in July. Celebrini hit $18.8M weeks later. Now Makar at $20.4M.

Connor McDavid and Auston Matthews are both eligible for extensions next summer.

Makar's résumé:

- Calder Trophy, two Norris Trophies, Conn Smythe, and a Stanley Cup
- 4th-fastest defenseman to 500 points, behind only Orr, Coffey, and Potvin
- 99th percentile skating speed at 23.68 mph
- 507 points in 470 games with the Avs
- Career-high 30 goals and 92 points in 2024-25

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2093450459331584237

A defenseman scoring at more than a point per game. The last time that was normal, players still weren't wearing helmets.

Tarik Skubal Comes Home

Tarik Skubal took the mound at Comerica Park for the first time as a Los Angeles Dodger. The Tigers played a tribute video before he threw a pitch. He didn't make it through dry.

https://twitter.com/JomboyMedia/status/2093469025128902788

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2093479608272200160

Then he went out and pitched like the reigning Cy Young winner.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2093500691570430120

Six innings, one run, seven punchouts, zero walks. In the ballpark he'd called home his entire career. Against the fans who watched him grow into an ace. He still got a no-decision — Max Clark tripled in a run to tie it, and Freddie Freeman put LA ahead in the 8th.

Skubal walking off the mound at Comerica for the last time as a visitor is going to age like a wine cellar. Detroit gave him a hero's welcome for a guy currently wearing the wrong colors.
