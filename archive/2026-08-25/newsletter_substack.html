Jed York Arrested in an Ohio Trailer Park

The 49ers own the NFC West. They also apparently own a Wheat Hill Mobile Home Community timeshare.

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2091928952042307817

Two days in jail. $1,150 in fines. And a permanent place in NFL owner history that he cannot buy his way out of, and he can definitely afford to try.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2091938938881462434

York was arrested Sunday in East Palestine, Ohio, carrying his cellphone and $160 in cash, after allegedly responding to an online prostitution ad and agreeing to pay $140 for sex. A billionaire, whose family fortune is $8.5 billion, drove out to a trailer park in East Palestine with $160 in his pocket. He was going to get $20 change.

Now the charge that broke Twitter — "possession of criminal tools."

https://twitter.com/PFTCommenter/status/2091944516404088890

His "criminal tool" was his cellphone. His phone. The device he used to schedule a trailer park rendezvous is now a court-recognized weapon.

https://twitter.com/ghetto_gronk/status/2091969924579094562

And then — because the universe is a comedy writer — Twitter located the scene of the crime.

https://twitter.com/ghetto_gronk/status/2092042596122017869

The 49ers cannot escape the electrical substation. It took out Levi's Stadium last year. Now it's within walking distance of their owner's arrest. If Christian McCaffrey pulls a hamstring in Week 1, we know why.

https://twitter.com/NFLMemes/status/2091989693986378130

Roger Goodell now gets to review this under the personal conduct policy. The same policy that suspended players for tweeting the wrong thing is going to "review" a billionaire owner's no-contest plea to soliciting prostitution. Take a wild guess how that ends.

Angel Reese Breaks Two Rebounding Records in One Night

Angel Reese grabbed 26 rebounds against the Sparks. That broke the WNBA single-game record. It also pushed her past the WNBA single-season record. Both records. Same night. Same game.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2092103057131589957

Reese surpassed Chamique Holdsclaw's record of 24 rebounds, set on May 23, 2003, for the Washington Mystics. That record stood for 23 years. Reese broke it on a night she shot 4-of-14 from the floor.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2092103090778300513

She now owns the #1 and #3 spots on the all-time single-season list. She's setting the record and chasing herself for second at the same time.

The best part was Allisha Gray watching this happen next to her.

https://twitter.com/ClutchPoints/status/2092114656168947996

Gray had 20 points in the same game and had to sit at her locker doing rebounding math on her own career. That's the Reese effect. She makes teammates who just went for 20 feel like they've never seen a basketball.
