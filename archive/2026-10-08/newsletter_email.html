Thirty years ago almost to the day, a 12-year-old kid named Jeffrey Maier reached over the right field wall at Yankee Stadium, pulled a Derek Jeter fly ball into the stands, and the umpires called it a home run because replay didn't exist yet. The Yankees won that game and eventually the World Series.

Last night, a grown man in left field reached over the wall on an Anthony Volpe drive and the universe finally squared the ledger.

Brewers to NLCS After Garrett Mitchell Robs Machado

Manny Machado hit a ball to deep center field in the 8th inning with the Padres down one. Machado knew. Machado started his trot. Machado was already thinking about where he'd point when he rounded first.

Garrett Mitchell was waiting at the wall with his glove up.

Milwaukee is going to the NLCS. They'll face the Dodgers in a rematch of last year's NLCS, this time with home-field advantage. Two best teams in baseball by record, round two, Brewers hosting.

The Padres head home with another postseason that ended one swing short. Fernando Tatis Jr. homered. Machado almost tied it. Almost doesn't send you to October next week.

Both Top WNBA Seeds Down 0-2 at Home

Breanna Stewart just did something nobody has ever done in a WNBA playoff game. 35 points. 11 rebounds. 6 assists. 2 blocks. First 35/10/5 game in the history of the WNBA postseason.

The Liberty lost by three in overtime.

Jordin Canada went for 23 and 8 on 9-of-15 from the field in Game 2 after dropping 25 in Game 1. She is currently leading every player left in the playoffs in total assists.

Then there was the timeout. Stewart said after the game that two different referees told her the Liberty were out of timeouts late in regulation. The scoreboard had the Liberty with a timeout left. The crew chief's explanation afterward was that Stewart was standing behind the referee when he confirmed the timeout situation with his partner, so she must have misheard.

Atlanta is one win from the Finals. Angel Reese — the reigning Defensive Player of the Year — went for 23 and 7 with six points in overtime on the road against the best player in the league. Rookie year was fun. Year two she's turning the Dream into finalists.

Golden State — a second-year expansion team — is up 2-0 on the Aces. In Las Vegas. A'ja Wilson. Becky Hammon. Four straight Finals appearances. Down 0-2 to a team that didn't exist 24 months ago.
