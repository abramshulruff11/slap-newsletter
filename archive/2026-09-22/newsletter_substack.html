The Giants' Season Just Ended in One Quarter

Jaxson Dart sprained his MCL on Monday Night Football. Two Rams defenders crunched him, he grabbed his knee, got carted off. Rookie franchise QB, drafted to be the future, Week 1 upset of the Cowboys — gone by the second quarter of Week 2.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2102334152762904640

Then Malik Nabers went down with a shoulder. Then Andrew Thomas. Same quarter. The three players the Giants' entire offensive plan was built around — the ones who barely got 23 snaps together all of last year — all hit the turf on the same primetime broadcast.

https://twitter.com/billbarnwell/status/2102234778804297967

Jameis Winston relieved him. Single worst passing performance of his career in any game he's attempted 25 or more throws.

https://twitter.com/Ihartitz/status/2102241206847545774

He also threw a red-zone interception. Jameis Winston is going to Jameis, and the Giants signed up for exactly this outcome the moment they put him on the roster as QB2.

Giants fans beat the Cowboys in Week 1. Tonight they lost 28-6, watched their rookie QB get carted off, watched their WR1 grab his shoulder, watched their LT go down, and got to enjoy the Jameis Winston Experience in prime time.

Davante Adams, 33, Looks 32

Puka Nacua inactive with a hip. Davante Adams was going to get more looks than usual. 8 catches, 195 yards, 2 TDs.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2102234040409465234

Six yards short of his career high, which he set with the Packers in overtime against the Bengals. He got that on 11 catches. He did this on 8.

https://twitter.com/Ihartitz/status/2102220227073228801

The multi-touchdown-receiving-game leaderboard, all-time, has exactly three names above his:

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2102220998762487951

Rice, Moss, T.O., Davante. That's the top four wide receivers in history at doing the thing that ends drives. Not "of this era." All-time.

Matthew Stafford went 22-of-31 for 327 and 4 TDs. Stafford has now thrown 4+ TDs in more games than all but five quarterbacks in history. The Stafford-Adams connection has existed for exactly two games and is already the most productive pairing either of them has had since the primes of their previous quarterback-receiver marriages. Puka gets his hip right, the Rams have three problems for every defense on their schedule.

Bregman Dodged It

Alex Bregman was standing in the on-deck circle Sunday when a foul ball hit him in the face. Multiple fractures. No surgery required. He's hopeful to be back for the postseason.

https://twitter.com/JeffPassan/status/2102135553932083678

"Multiple facial fractures but hopeful to return in the postseason" is one of those sentences that reads like great news and terrible news depending on which half you emphasize. The Cubs will take it.

The on-deck circle has zero protection. A hitter standing there is watching the pitcher, taking practice cuts, doing anything except tracking a 100 mph line drive off the bat behind him. MLB added protective netting for fans after enough of them got hurt. The players are still out there in the open.

Pete Alonso Hits 40 for the Team That Wasn't the Mets

Pete Alonso homered twice against the Blue Jays Monday. The second one was his 40th of the season.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2102213262850789403

He's the only player in MLB history to hit 40+ homers in his first season in each league — 53 with the Mets as a rookie, 40 now with Baltimore. He's leading the American League in RBIs. The Orioles gave him five years and $155 million last December. He's paid it back with the fourth 40-HR season of his career.
