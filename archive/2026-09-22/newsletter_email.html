Bregman Dodged It

Alex Bregman was standing in the on-deck circle Sunday when a foul ball hit him in the face. Multiple fractures. No surgery required. He's hopeful to be back for the postseason.

@JeffPassan
Chicago Cubs star Alex Bregman suffered multiple facial fractures after being struck by a foul ball while standing in the on-deck circle Sunday, sources tell ESPN. Bregman will not undergo surgery and is hopeful to return in the postseason.
View on Twitter →

"Multiple facial fractures but hopeful to return in the postseason" is one of those sentences that reads like great news and terrible news depending on which half you emphasize. The Cubs will take it.

The on-deck circle has zero protection. A hitter standing there is watching the pitcher, taking practice cuts, doing anything except tracking a 100 mph line drive off the bat behind him. MLB added protective netting for fans after enough of them got hurt. The players are still out there in the open.

Pete Alonso Hits 40 for the Team That Wasn't the Mets

Pete Alonso homered twice against the Blue Jays Monday. The second one was his 40th of the season.

@TalkinBaseball_
Pete Alonso reaches 40 home runs in his first season with the Orioles!
View on Twitter →

He's the only player in MLB history to hit 40+ homers in his first season in each league — 53 with the Mets as a rookie, 40 now with Baltimore. He's leading the American League in RBIs. The Orioles gave him five years and $155 million last December. He's paid it back with the fourth 40-HR season of his career.
