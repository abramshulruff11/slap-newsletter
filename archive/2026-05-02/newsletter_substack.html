RJ Barrett's Miracle Bounce Saves Toronto

RJ Barrett hit a 3-pointer with 1.2 seconds left in overtime. The shot hit the rim twice, but the first bounce off the heel went 10 times higher than anything you've ever seen.

https://twitter.com/TheNBACentel/status/2050402979736363472

Toronto hasn't seen a fortunate bounce like this since Kawhi Leonard's game-winner in 2019. Same franchise. Same arena. Same type of elimination-avoiding moment. Kawhi's shot bounced four times on the rim before settling through the net after the buzzer. The 2019 Raptors were tied while the 2026 team was down a point, needing a basket just to survive the game.

https://twitter.com/ClutchPoints/status/2050445497044017334

Barrett explained why the moment meant more to him since it came with the Raptors, not the New York Knicks, his former franchise. "Just happy to be able to be in this moment, playing for my hometown team." Barrett was struggling the whole game, shooting just 8-21, but his late-game dagger shocked everyone inside Scotiabank Arena. Buffalo's 19-Year Drought Ends

The Sabres secured their first playoff series victory in 19 years Friday night with a 4-1 victory in Boston. Alex Tuch and Mattias Samuelsson scored in the first period, Zach Benson added another early in the third and the Buffalo Sabres beat the Boston Bruins 4-1 on Friday night in Game 6 to advance to the second round of the NHL playoffs for the first time since 2007.

https://twitter.com/ESPN/status/2050398814234640695
