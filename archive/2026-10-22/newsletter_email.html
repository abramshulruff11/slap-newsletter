Ty Jerome's previous career high was 33 points. He got that in a Cavaliers jersey in January 2025. He dropped 45 and broke the Memphis franchise record for points in a season opener — a record Ja Morant used to own with 44.

Ja got traded to Portland over the summer. Memphis handed Jerome the keys. First game as the starting point guard he goes 17-of-25 and runs the previous record holder's number off the board.

Journeyman guards who dropped a nuclear number out of nowhere live forever in League Pass folklore — Corey Brewer 51, Terrence Ross 51, Tony Delk 53. Welcome to the club, Ty.
