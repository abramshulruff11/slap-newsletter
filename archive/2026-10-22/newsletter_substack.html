Giannis Antetokounmpo was supposed to drag Miami into contention. Night one, at home, against Minnesota: 10 points, 8 rebounds, 5 assists, 3-of-12 from the field. Final score 131-106.

Anthony Edwards spent the entire game cussing out the Heat bench, then explained himself postgame like it was a civics lesson:

- "A player on they bench was talkin trash... talkin pretty crazy."
- "When I started talkin to him he didn't really have too much to say."
- "If you gonna talk sh*t keep talkin sh*t. Don't get quiet."

Giannis walked into Miami and got dropped by 25 at home while a veteran guard cussed out his bench for the entire game.

Ty Jerome Just Dropped 45 on Opening Night

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2113097327200104552

Ty Jerome's previous career high was 33 points. He got that in a Cavaliers jersey in January 2025. He dropped 45 and broke the Memphis franchise record for points in a season opener — a record Ja Morant used to own with 44.

Ja got traded to Portland over the summer. Memphis handed Jerome the keys. First game as the starting point guard he goes 17-of-25 and runs the previous record holder's number off the board.

Journeyman guards who dropped a nuclear number out of nowhere live forever in League Pass folklore — Corey Brewer 51, Terrence Ross 51, Tony Delk 53. Welcome to the club, Ty.

Pacers 123, Pelicans 120 — Haliburton's Back

Indiana's first win in New Orleans since January 2021.

Tyrese Haliburton returned with 23 points and 12 assists. Pascal Siakam went for 30-8 on 13-of-23. Zion Williamson bricked the shot that would have forced overtime, then did a little practice wrist-flick as he walked off the court.

Haliburton last played a meaningful game in the Finals. First game back: 23 points, 12 assists.

Angel Reese's Dream Are One Win From the Title

Atlanta beat Golden State 97-71 to take a 2-1 series lead in the WNBA Finals. Reese has been the best player on the floor since Game 2 and the Valkyries — who won Game 1 — are now one loss from the offseason.

Gabby Williams was screaming in the Valkyries huddle after the loss. Pretty understandable when your opponent is averaging a 20-point double-double and your season is on the ropes.
