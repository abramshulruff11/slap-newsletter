France Survives Paraguay Street Fight

France beat Paraguay 1-0 in the Round of 16. Mbappé penalty. France moves on. In reality it was 90 minutes of Paraguay trying to physically dismantle every player in a blue jersey, followed by a postgame scrum that had nothing to do with soccer.

The lone goal was also a history marker.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2073536775385915470

Mbappé is 27 years old and one goal behind Messi's all-time World Cup record of 20. Messi got there at 39. If France advances past Morocco, Mbappé ties it. If they win the tournament, he probably owns the record outright.

Now, about that Paraguay defense.

https://twitter.com/billbarnwell/status/2073527482821566970

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2073546299769504207

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2073544294917431452

Paraguay played this game like a Game 7 in the 1990s Eastern Conference. Every French touch met a shoulder, an elbow, or a shin.

Meanwhile, the actual best player on the field wasn't wearing the number 10.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2073430787551023598

Five assists in this tournament. Only Pelé in 1970 has ever had more in a single World Cup.

Then the final whistle blew.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2073549090307154089

Mbappé walks past the handshake line. Paraguay's keeper chucks the ball at the back of his head. Benches clear. Diplomatic incident on July 4 in Philadelphia.

A World Cup Round of 16 game played on July 4, in the city where America was born, between the first country to fight alongside us and a team that showed up ready to fight everyone. France advances. Morocco awaits.

Morocco Ends Canada's Dream, Sets Up France Quarterfinal

Morocco 3, Canada 0. Canada's first-ever knockout round appearance ends with the Atlas Lions doing what they always do: quietly getting better every minute until the other team collapses.

https://twitter.com/coleadamss/status/2073482979729371639

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2073485350232584492

Jesse Marsch reportedly described preparing for Morocco as a "gory, horrible nightmare." He then lost 3-0.

Per ESPN, Morocco won with five total shots — the fewest by any team to win a World Cup knockout match in the last 60 years.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2073487000196321658

Morocco is now the first African nation to reach the World Cup quarterfinals in multiple tournaments. Their four all-time knockout-round wins equal the total of every other African country combined. Brahim Díaz added two assists to become the first African player with four in a single World Cup.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2073491148887199946

France in the quarterfinals on Tuesday. Les Bleus just survived a cage match. Now they face a team that plays actual soccer and doesn't miss.
