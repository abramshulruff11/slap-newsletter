Dante Moore Carted Off With Serious Injury After Late Hit — USC Player Celebrates While He Lies Motionless

Late in the third quarter of Oregon-USC, Dante Moore slid at the end of a scramble. USC linebacker Desman Stephens got there anyway — helmet-first, well after the slide — and Moore didn't get up.

@AlbertBreer
Oh no ... Oregon QB Dante Moore is down. Looks bad. Whole Oregon team is out on the field now. USC players look shaken too.

Being loaded on to the cart now.
View tweet

Breer, who was in the stadium, noted seeing a fencing posture — the involuntary arm position that's a known neurological red flag after a serious head impact. Moore was carted off and taken by ambulance from the Coliseum. Stephens was ejected for targeting. Both teams had to be separated on the field.

@ESPN
Dante Moore has movement in all of his extremities and is being evaluated thoroughly at Los Angeles General County Hospital, according to a team spokesperson.

Moore was carted off the field and taken off by an ambulance after a late hit during Saturday night's game. http://spr.ly/6019BGmsfv
View tweet

Movement in the extremities is the first piece of genuinely encouraging news out of the hospital. Beyond that, nothing has been confirmed, and speculating past the team spokesperson serves nobody.

The second half of this story is what happened while Moore was still on the ground.

Stephens, after making the hit that knocked Moore unconscious, celebrated. He laughed. He dapped up teammates. He taunted the Oregon sideline. All of this while a 20-year-old quarterback lay motionless behind him waiting for the training staff to reach him. That is the sequence. That is what the broadcast captured and what set the Oregon bench off, and what turned the postgame conversation into something bigger than a targeting call.

Oregon won 41-27. Nobody involved is going to remember the score.

Iowa Stuns Michigan With Walkoff TD in Final Second

Iowa 20, Michigan 19. Final second. In Ann Arbor.

Hank Brown drove the Hawkeyes 91 yards in the final two minutes and hit Reece Vander Zee on a 16-yard touchdown with two seconds left. Vander Zee's second score of the fourth quarter. Both were 16-yarders from Brown. Big House goes silent, then empties.

Iowa is 4-0 for the first time since 2021. Ferentz is 4-0 in September for the seventh time. The offense went for 285 yards on 16 first downs. Michigan had 429 and 21. Iowa won anyway because Iowa always wins that way — punt it inside the 10, force a turnover, take one deep shot in the fourth quarter, hug your kicker.
