Julián Quiñones in the 22nd. Raúl Jiménez in the 31st. Ecuador never had a pulse. Mexico 2, Ecuador 0 at Azteca, and El Tri wins a World Cup knockout game for the first time since June 15, 1986, against Bulgaria.

Mexico had entered the knockout rounds eight straight tournaments and gone home eight straight times. That's the longest such streak in World Cup history — a curse with a name in Spanish, the quinto partido, and an entire generation of Mexican fans who had never seen this happen in their adult lives.

The last time it did happen, the ball was leather, the anthem was different, and Diego Maradona was about to score the Hand of God two rounds later.
