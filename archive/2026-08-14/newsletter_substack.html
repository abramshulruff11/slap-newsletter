Kyle Schwarber Hits Baseballs Into Iowa Corn Twice

Field of Dreams Game came back after four years. 8,000 fans in Dyersville, Iowa. Cornstalks in the outfield. Netflix broadcast. The whole Kevin Costner of it all.

Kyle Schwarber decided to turn it into a personal Americana tour.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2088046855468834874

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2088047470148296949

First pitch of the game. Straight into the corn.

Then he did it again.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2088064810650903035

The second one clanked off the glove of Twins right fielder Luke Keaschall before disappearing into the stalks. The corn was going to have it either way.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2088089625411588133

Three games have ever been played at this site. Two players have ever gone multi-homer. One of them is Aaron Judge. The other is a leadoff-hitting DH who looks like he'd fistfight a combine harvester.

Twins outfielder Luke Keaschall — the same guy who let Schwarber's second one bounce off his glove — hit one of his own into the stalks.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2088056225074159858

And Bryce Harper showed up in full costume.

https://twitter.com/CespedesBBQ/status/2088090553510482328

Phillies won 8-3. Schwarber cleared 335 down the lines, 380 to the alleys, 400 to dead center. Twice. On a Netflix broadcast. In a cornfield.
