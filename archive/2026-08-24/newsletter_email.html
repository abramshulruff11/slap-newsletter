Enes Kanter Freedom Gets Ejected From a WNBA Game He Wasn't Even Playing In

Enes Kanter Freedom was ejected from his courtside seat during the Indiana Fever's 113-90 win over the Chicago Sky on Sunday after getting into a verbal altercation with Sky guard Natasha Cloud. He wasn't playing. He was sitting in a chair.

Late in the third quarter, Cloud drove to the basket, converted a tough finish, and started pointing and yelling at Kanter Freedom. He stood up and walked toward the court.

Kanter Freedom's outfit: a black shirt that read "WOMAN noun. adult human female." This was not accidental attendance.

Context: Kanter Freedom has spent the past few weeks agitating to be eligible for the 2027 WNBA Draft, along with fellow former NBA player Royce White. Both claim they now identify as women. A 34-year-old, 6'10", 250-pound former NBA center is trying to declare for the WNBA Draft. And going to games. In a shirt. Alone.

The WNBA has never had a transgender player and does not have a public policy about transgender participation. The new CBA states simply that "only players who are women are eligible to play in the WNBA."

Kanter Freedom was escorted from the court area. He displayed his shirt as he walked toward the lobby. If you're going to get thrown out of a WNBA game, milk the walk.

Trista Smith of Boise, Idaho, was sitting in Kanter Freedom's row Sunday. She flew in to watch Caitlin Clark and ended up following him out to the lobby after he was ejected. Trista from Boise flew across the country to watch Caitlin Clark and ended up as a background extra in a culture-war segment.

Cloud spoke with team and league security after Chicago's 113-90 loss but did not address the media. The Sky just lost by 23, and somehow that's the third biggest story from the game.

Saints Trade for Zamir White as the RB Room Turns Into a Waiting Room

Alvin Kamara is out for at least several weeks with an MCL injury. Audric Estime (ankle) did not travel to California for Saturday's preseason game. Devin Neal returned from a hamstring issue last week, then reinjured the hamstring Saturday.

Three RBs down. One preseason. Regular season three weeks away.

The New Orleans Saints acquired Zamir White in a trade that sent veteran linebacker Deion Jones to the San Francisco 49ers, sources told ESPN's Adam Schefter on Sunday night.

White, who signed with the 49ers in early August, had faced long odds to make the team after running backs Christian McCaffrey, Kaelon Black, and Jordan James returned from injury in the past week. White was about to get cut in San Francisco. Now he's a starter in New Orleans. Same guy, same tape, two-week difference.

New Orleans had just signed Jones earlier this month. He was considered a long shot to make the team. Two players who were about to be cut got traded for each other. The NFL is a garage sale in August.

Ashton Jeanty Sprains Ankle, Raiders Take a Knee

During Sunday's practice, Jeanty ran a route and dove for a low ball from Kirk Cousins. He landed awkwardly and stayed on the ground before being helped off the field. He couldn't put pressure on his right leg.

His teammates took a knee and said a prayer after Jeanty exited the field. Rookie running back Mike Washington Jr. took the first-team reps for the remainder of practice. Football is the only sport where the team holds a candlelight vigil AND names your replacement in the same ninety seconds.

Raiders RB Ashton Jeanty is believed to have sprained his ankle, sources tell @rapsheet and me. The Raiders still are unsure how long he will be out, but it is not considered a long-term injury. A sprained ankle from a guy who couldn't put weight on his leg is somehow a best-case scenario.

Kirk Cousins threw the low ball that made Jeanty dive in the first place. The Cousins Effect is real.

Fernando Mendoza's Rude Welcome to the NFL

Week 1: Mendoza looks the part. Week 2: Houston's defense sends its regards.

The No. 1 overall pick threw a pick-six that went 80 yards the other way on the Raiders' opening possession. He finished his first start 8-for-15 for 86 yards. He also took a sack for a loss of 11 yards. Third pass of his first start. 80 yards the other way.

Meanwhile, the star of the rookie QB class this week wasn't a first-round pick. Joey Aguilar and Jalon Daniels both scored touchdowns in consecutive outings, and Daniels oversaw a comeback win for the Buccaneers in preseason Week 2. They could go from undrafted free agents to important depth pieces.

Jalon Daniels went undrafted out of Kansas and might crack an NFL roster. Fernando Mendoza went first overall and threw a pick-six on his third pass. Preseason will humble you and then hand you the depth chart upside down.

Adjusting from college football to the NFL is hard. Do not let one rough preseason game against a stellar defense get in the way of Fernando Mendoza's potential to find ample success as a pro, potentially as soon as the latter stages of his rookie year. It really was a tough one, though.

Kirk Cousins keeps his job for another week. Which, given Kirk Cousins's month, is not the resounding endorsement it sounds like.
