England Survives the Azteca

England beat Mexico 3-2 in the Round of 16. At the Azteca. In the altitude. Down to ten men for the final half hour. This is the kind of game that gets its own 30 for 30 in ten years.

Jude Bellingham decided to end the vibes early. Two headers in 99 seconds. Mexico's fortress was down 2-0 before the crowd finished tuning their whistles.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2073958112877302187

Then it got weird. Jarell Quansah studs up, straight red, England down to ten with 30+ minutes to go against Mexico at 7,200 feet.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2073954699208798455

Raul Jimenez pulled one back from the spot. Azteca started shaking. Then Harry Kane iced it from twelve yards.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2073956262560100355

Julian Quinones made it 3-2. Ten Englishmen defending the box for what felt like a full presidential term. Jordan Pickford turning into a brick wall. Pure siege football.

https://twitter.com/coleadamss/status/2073966704372113851

https://twitter.com/BarstoolBigCat/status/2073966413929136417

Mexico at the Azteca: W69, D17, L2 in 88 competitive matches. Ten consecutive World Cup matches unbeaten at home.

Mexico fans watching their 40-year-old fortress get demolished by ten Englishmen:

Then England did the most English thing possible and started singing Wonderwall at full volume in the middle of Mexico City.

https://twitter.com/coleadamss/status/2073967931235422253

Harry Kane's post-match interview sounded like he'd been gargling gravel for six hours. Altitude does not care that you're the England captain.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2073975964086816957

And then, because this game refused to end normally, Jordan Henderson broke his arm celebrating. Not on a tackle. Not in the box. Jumping over advertisement signage in the 97th minute.

https://twitter.com/HaterReport/status/2073980950636016070

England now faces Norway in the quarterfinals. Which means Harry Kane's voice has five days to recover before Erling Haaland tries to head the ball through Jordan Pickford's chest cavity.

https://twitter.com/coleadamss/status/2073967547490161019

Norway Sends Brazil Home, Neymar Walks Away

Speaking of Norway. They just beat Brazil.

Not "held Brazil to a draw." Not "gave Brazil a scare." Beat them. 2-1. Both goals from Erling Haaland. Vikings are in the quarterfinals for the first time ever.

https://twitter.com/LeBatardShow/status/2073890770956726582

Haaland was quiet for 79 minutes. Then he wasn't. Header in the 80th. Another before regulation ended. Seven goals in the tournament, tied with Messi and Mbappé for the most. Fourteen consecutive competitive matches with a goal.

https://twitter.com/PatMcAfeeShow/status/2073887986509402438

https://twitter.com/SharpFootball/status/2073888528929280216

Brazil had been in the quarterfinals of every World Cup from 1994 through 2022. Earliest exit since 1990.

And then Neymar. He scored a consolation penalty, jawed at Norwegian keeper Ørjan Nyland, and walked off the pitch for the last time in a Brazil shirt.

https://twitter.com/ClutchPoints/status/2073918063032885481

The most devastating fact about Neymar's international career:

https://twitter.com/PFTCommenter/status/2073888774572888466

Zero World Cup semifinals. Career over.

Marlins Pull a Perfect Game and Immediately Regret It

Eury Perez threw seven perfect innings against the Athletics. 92 pitches. Twenty-one up, twenty-one down. The Marlins pulled him.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2073904580241264737

The A's fans, watching their own team get no-hit, started booing the Marlins for taking Perez out. That's how bad the decision was. The people being no-hit were mad on his behalf.

https://twitter.com/JomboyMedia/status/2073907690946859344

The Miami bullpen received the ball with a perfect game intact and an 8-0 lead. Here's what happened next, in order: walk (perfect game gone), single (no-hitter gone), grand slam (dignity gone).

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2073909194453873079

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2073919147352752133

Miami won 9-8. That is not a typo. First team in MLB history to be perfect through seven and win by a single run.

Padres Snap Their Skid, Machado Sends the Message

Eight straight losses. Worst stretch since 2013. The Padres walked into Dodger Stadium looking for anything.

They found it three pitches in — by getting their manager AND their bench coach both ejected.

https://twitter.com/JomboyMedia/status/2073913090664497460

Then Manny Machado hit the reset button, dead center field, three-run bomb.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2073944966196236542

https://twitter.com/StatMuse/status/2073961012533821621

5-2 final. Skid over. There was also a moment before the game that needs to be said flatly. Dodgers catcher Eliezer Alfonzo Jr. made his MLB debut hours after learning his sister and stepmother had been killed in the Venezuelan earthquakes. He and Miguel Rojas wore messages on their hats. The Dodger Stadium crowd gave him a standing ovation.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2073920981249241407
