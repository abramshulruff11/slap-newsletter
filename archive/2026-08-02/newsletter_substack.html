White Sox Buy at the Deadline. No, Really.

The Chicago White Sox — a team that lost 121 games two seasons ago — are trading for a frontline starter.

https://twitter.com/JeffPassan/status/2083733316222534069

And here's where it gets weird:

https://twitter.com/Alden_Gonzalez/status/2083735714517360856

The White Sox are taking on $45M+ guaranteed, plus a $25M vesting option, for a 33-year-old starter. Either Chicago is entering its buyer arc or the front office fell asleep on the "reply" button during a Seattle salary dump negotiation.

The pitcher himself is legit — one of only three starters with 900+ strikeouts and a sub-3.75 ERA since 2022. But the vibes here are Sox walking off the Yankees back-to-back last week and deciding they're the 2026 Marlins.
