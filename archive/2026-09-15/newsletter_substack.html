Yankees Clinch, and Aaron Judge Picks Up Where He Left Off

Aaron Judge had not homered in pinstripes since May 31. A stress fracture in his right first rib. He missed over three months. He came back on September 9. Five games later, he did what he does.

Ben Rice put the Yankees ahead with a go-ahead homer in the 8th, they beat the Twins 8-3, and the math officially clicked over. Ninth playoff berth in ten years.

Dodgers Clinch — Tied for the Longest Streak in MLB History

Los Angeles beat the Reds 4-1 and locked up a playoff spot. Tied with the Braves for the longest streak in MLB history.

Mets Eliminated — Pete Alonso, Inches from 300

Pete Alonso stepped up needing one swing to reach 300 career home runs. He got it. It hooked foul by about a foot. Two innings later, Gunnar Henderson took the Orioles ahead for good, Baltimore won 2-1, and the Mets' season was over.
