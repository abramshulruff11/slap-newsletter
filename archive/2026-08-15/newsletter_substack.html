Falcons Lose James Pearce Jr. for Half the Season

The NFL suspended Falcons edge rusher James Pearce Jr. eight games for violating the personal conduct policy. Pearce is out for the first eight games of the 2026 season and will be eligible to return in Week 9 against the Bengals in Madrid. The suspension follows a February arrest near Miami stemming from a domestic dispute involving his ex-girlfriend, WNBA player Rickea Jackson.

Pearce led the Falcons with 10.5 sacks as a rookie and finished third in Defensive Rookie of the Year voting.

https://twitter.com/SharpFootball/status/2088225859312226558

Combined with the Jalon Walker ACL scare from earlier this month, Atlanta enters the season without both of its 2025 first-round pass rushers for the toughest stretch of its schedule.

Steelers Extend Keeanu Benton

Pittsburgh signed defensive lineman Keeanu Benton to a four-year, $72 million extension. $18 million per year makes Benton the highest-paid defensive lineman in Steelers history.

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2088289039602626628

Pittsburgh locking up a 24-year-old interior lineman before he hits free agency is exactly the kind of quiet, competent move the Steelers have made for four decades and it never stops working.

Dennis Schroder Is Now on His 12th Team

Cleveland traded Dennis Schroder and cash to Charlotte for Tre Mann. The deal marks the ninth time Schroder has been traded in his NBA career, making him the second-most traded player in NBA history behind Trevor Ariza, who was dealt 11 times. This will be Schroder's 12th NBA team, trailing only Ish Smith, who played for 13.
