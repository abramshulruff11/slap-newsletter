MLB Just Proposed a Salary Cap. The Players Are Furious.

The league dropped the bomb on the union: a salary cap and floor, with strict contract limits. Free agents switching teams capped at 5 years and $202M. Teams retaining their own can offer 6 years. No more deferred money. Qualifying offer eliminated. Players 30+ hit free agency after 5 years of service. Plus a complete ban on MLB-related prop bets.

Juan Soto's 15-year, $765M contract with the Mets would have been illegal three times over under this proposal. The years are triple the cap. The dollars are nearly four times the limit.

The union called it a "litany of additional restrictions on player rights." Jeff Passan said the two sides are speaking different languages. The current CBA expires after this season.
