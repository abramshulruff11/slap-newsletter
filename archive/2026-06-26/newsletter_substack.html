Marina Mabrey Just Scored 53 Points and Tied the WNBA All-Time Record in 31 Minutes

Marina Mabrey scored 53 points last night for the Toronto Tempo.

53 points. Tied the WNBA all-time record. Toronto beat the Sparks 125-97.

https://twitter.com/StatMuse/status/2070331865681289391

The line: 17-of-28 from the field. 9-of-18 from three. 10-of-12 from the line. Six rebounds. The four-player club at the top of WNBA history is now Liz Cambage, A'ja Wilson, Riquna Williams, and Marina Mabrey.

https://twitter.com/StatMuse/status/2070318213640880369

Mabrey didn't even know the record was within reach. She said her sister Michaela, sitting on the sideline, told her she was three points away. "There's no stat board in our gym."

First quarter: 19 points. Career-high for a quarter. Halftime: 27. End of third: 39. The fourth quarter was just a coronation.

Here's the part that breaks your brain:

https://twitter.com/ClutchPoints/status/2070342097761161511

Marina Mabrey's career-high is higher than Magic Johnson's.

She's not even a household name. Mabrey is a 29-year-old guard from New Jersey who played at Notre Dame, was the No. 6 overall pick by the Tempo in the expansion draft this year, and signed a two-year deal worth $2.4 million. The Tempo are paying her $1.2M a year and she just dropped one of the four greatest individual scoring games in league history.

She's also on a tear that has never happened in this league. 113 points over the past three games. Most in a three-game span in WNBA history.

MLB Just Proposed a Salary Cap. The Players Are Furious.

The league dropped the bomb on the union: a salary cap and floor, with strict contract limits. Free agents switching teams capped at 5 years and $202M. Teams retaining their own can offer 6 years. No more deferred money. Qualifying offer eliminated. Players 30+ hit free agency after 5 years of service. Plus a complete ban on MLB-related prop bets.

Juan Soto's 15-year, $765M contract with the Mets would have been illegal three times over under this proposal. The years are triple the cap. The dollars are nearly four times the limit.

The union called it a "litany of additional restrictions on player rights." Jeff Passan said the two sides are speaking different languages. The current CBA expires after this season.
