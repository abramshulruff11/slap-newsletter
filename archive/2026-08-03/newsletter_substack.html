Cubs Finally Show Up to the Deadline

The Cubs sat on their hands while the Dodgers got Skubal, the Yankees got García, and the Rays got Peralta. With the deadline hours away, Jed Hoyer finally moved. Kevin Gausman is a Cub.

Chicago sent outfielder Brett Bateman and infielder Ty Southisene to Toronto.

Gausman came out of the bullpen in Game 7 of the ALCS last October. Scoreless seventh. Sent the Blue Jays to their first World Series since 1993. Finished last postseason with a 2.00 ERA over 18 innings.

The Cubs won the Wild Card round over San Diego last year. Lost to Milwaukee in the NLDS. Getting bounced by the Brewers requires a specific kind of solution.

Jed Hoyer watching the deadline board light up for a week while Cubs Twitter refreshed MLB Trade Rumors:

Gausman: two-time All-Star, two-time All-MLB Second Team, 2023 AL strikeout leader. Cubs get him with postseason miles already on the odometer heading into a Central race where Milwaukee still lives rent-free in Chicago's head.

Yankees Get Their Bat: Luis García Jr. to the Bronx

The Nationals finally cashed in their best trade chip.

https://twitter.com/JeffPassan/status/2084082635291144444

García is leading the NL in slugging at .283/.313/.560 with 23 homers. Under club control through 2027. Washington got four pitching prospects — Ben Grable, Yovanny Cruz, Jake Bird, and Jack Cebert. Yankees added the left-handed power their offense has been begging for since April.

Rays Pivot from Skubal to Peralta

Tampa went hard after Tarik Skubal, got outbid by the Dodgers, and pivoted immediately to Freddy Peralta. They lose a bidding war on Sunday and land a frontline arm by Monday.

Peralta's line as a Met this year: 5-9, 4.99 ERA, 113.2 innings, 113 strikeouts.

The bet is that the Rays pitching lab does what the Rays pitching lab always does. Peralta joins Rasmussen, McClanahan, and Martinez in what's suddenly one of the AL's best rotations. On paper.

Misiorowski Reviews the Angel Stadium Mound: One Star

Jacob Misiorowski just took a 3-0 loss in Anaheim and had some feedback for the grounds crew.

His quote after the game: "It was awful. Probably one of the worst mounds I've had. It was dry. It was rock hard… You can't blame it on that. I gotta do my job… There's always an injury risk any time that there's something astray… I land so much further than most guys. They don't really fix that part of the mound."

The classic "I'm not blaming the mound but let me tell you about the mound for 90 seconds" quote.

When a reporter asked about the trade deadline and whether Milwaukee or the Dodgers might be interested, Misiorowski stopped them with "You can just stop the question." He leads MLB with 10 starts of 10+ strikeouts this year. He's earned the attitude.

Skubal Meets His New Teammates

Tarik Skubal is officially a Dodger. He was at the game tonight. He's keeping No. 29.

The Red Sox came in and swept the Dodgers over the weekend. 8-4 win on Sunday night. Not exactly the welcome LA was hoping for.

Red Sox Are Doing Something Historic

Boston went into Los Angeles and swept the reigning NL power. Three games. Three losses for the Dodgers. On their own field.

Sunday finale: the Red Sox hit three homers in a single inning. Ceddanne Rafaela, Wilyer Abreu, and Masataka Yoshida all went deep. Rafaela homered for the third straight game.

Boston went 21-4 in July. .840 win percentage. That's the best in any month in the franchise's 126-year history. Ted Williams never had a month like this. Big Papi never had a month like this. Pedro never had a month like this.

Benches Clear in Cleveland Because of a Bunt

Gavin Williams had a perfect game going. Arizona's Tim Tawa attempted a bunt. Cleveland took exception. Then it rained.

Benches cleared as the grounds crew tried to get the tarp on the field in a downpour. Baseball's unwritten rulebook, sponsored by weather.com.

Cleveland won 5-0. Perfect game was gone by then anyway. Everyone got wet for nothing.
