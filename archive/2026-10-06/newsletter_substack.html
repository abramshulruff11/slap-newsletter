Yankees Four-Error Meltdown Gifts Rays 2-0 ALDS Lead

Four errors. Three in the first inning. One of them a catcher's interference call on what should have been strike three.

Ryan McMahon kicked it off at third. Jazz Chisholm airmailed a throw. Then Austin Wells reached out with his glove on strike three and gave the Rays an at-bat they'd already given back. Tampa scored an unearned run before anyone in the Bronx had warmed up a hand.

https://twitter.com/JeffPassan/status/2107266132227100870

https://twitter.com/JeffPassan/status/2107290002090504399

The Rays don't walk up there trying to hit it 450 feet. They walk up there trying to make you catch it. And right now the Yankees can't.

Ben Rice did everything a single human being can do. Two solo homers — one in the third, one in the eighth — and he's now the first Yankees player with two multi-homer games in a single postseason. Three home runs in the series. Zero of them with anyone on base, because nobody else can get on base.

That's the whole offense. One guy. A historic one, but still one guy, hitting into a vacuum while the defense sets money on fire behind him.

And now they go home needing to win three straight to survive.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2107309822647402765

The only way out is to win one in the Bronx and then two more on the road at the Trop.

White Sox On The Brink Of The ALCS

Chicago is up 2-0 on Cleveland and has not lost a game this postseason. Rookie Braden Montgomery dropped a two-run double in the sixth to give the White Sox the lead for good. That was the final hit of the series.

The Guardians pitched well enough to win.

Cleveland hasn't won a World Series in decades. They can close that gap to "lost the ALDS at home again" by Wednesday, when the White Sox come back to the South Side with the broom.

Falcons Turn The "Domecoming" Into A Bloodbat

The Saints held a ceremony Monday night honoring the 20th anniversary of their return to the Superdome after Katrina. The Falcons showed up and hung 45 on them.

Bijan Robinson: 19 carries, 145 yards, 2 touchdowns. Took his first carry 59 yards to the house. Brian Robinson Jr.: 14 carries, 3 touchdowns. The two B. Robinsons combined for five rushing scores and tied the Falcons' franchise record for a game. Michael Penix hasn't lost yet. Alvin Kamara scored his first touchdown of the year, which was the one bright moment the home crowd got.

Then the halftime show happened.

Ovechkin Says This Is The Last One

https://twitter.com/JomboyMedia/status/2107126985143038250

Ovechkin is 41. He has 929 regular-season goals and 77 more in the playoffs, and in March he became the second player in NHL history to reach 1,000 combined goals, joining Gretzky. He already owns the all-time record for regular-season goals. The only thing left on the board is the round number, and he needs 21 more to hit 950 and keep stretching the gap on everyone chasing him.

One more year of The Great Eight. Every goal from here is the new ceiling of the sport.

Messi's Last Argentina Match Is Tonight

Lionel Messi plays his final match for Argentina on Tuesday night at the Monumental in Buenos Aires. Opponent: Benin, a friendly. He will finish his international career with 207 appearances and 125 goals, both Argentine records.

He already has the Copa América. He has the Finalissima. He has the 2022 World Cup. The one asterisk on the farewell: Argentina reached the 2026 World Cup final in June and lost 1-0 to Spain, which is the closest a Messi era has come to ending on a sour note. Tonight is the correction. One shirt, one stadium, one more time.
