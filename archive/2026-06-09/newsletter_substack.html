The Knicks' 46-Day Winning Streak Ends in Their Own Building

The Knicks hadn't lost a playoff game since April 23. Forty-six days. Thirteen straight wins. Only the 2017 Warriors had a longer single-postseason streak. They were two quarters from going up 3-0 in the Finals at home in front of Ben Stiller, Chalamet, Chappelle, Jeter, and a President.

And then Wemby happened.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2064188230468067345

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2064188604130197726

11-of-18 from the floor. Three blocks. Two steals. A line that puts him in a club with three Hall of Famers, and he's 22. The Knicks built a 12-point second-half lead and Wemby ate it whole.

The other guy who saved San Antonio's season isn't a Hall of Famer yet. He's a rookie.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2064188267344363814

Late three to stretch the lead. Two free throws to ice it. Castle, 21 years old, in the loudest building in basketball, did not blink.

Knicks fans watching their bulletproof postseason finally take a bullet:

Brunson hit a late three to make it a two-point game. KAT was a no-show. Mikal Bridges: 2 points on 1-of-5 shooting and four fouls. Fourth quarter: 16 points on 6-of-24 shooting and 1-of-11 from three.

https://twitter.com/TimBontemps/status/2064188384658989231

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2064188416732876995

Of the 37 teams that ever fell into a 2-0 Finals hole, only five have come back to win the championship. If the Spurs pull this off, they'd be the first team in NBA history to lose two home games to open the Finals and still win it all.

Game 4 is Wednesday in New York. The Knicks can still slam this door.

But Wemby is the thing nobody in that 37-team history book had to deal with. And the most secretly delicious subplot? Mike Brown was an assistant on Golden State's 2017 staff and went 12-0 as acting head coach during that playoff run while Steve Kerr was sidelined. He has now orchestrated two of the longest postseason winning streaks in NBA history from the bench.

The man who helped break the Knicks' streak is the same guy who helped set the record they were chasing.
