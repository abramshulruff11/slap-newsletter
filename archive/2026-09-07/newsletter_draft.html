Ole Miss Walks Off Louisville in an Instant Classic

College football is back. Week 1 already delivered the Game of the Year.

@RedditCFB
OLE MISS WITH THE WALK-OFF, COLLEGE FOOTBALL IS BACK!!
View tweet

Ole Miss 41, Louisville 38. Walk-off field goal from Lucas Carneiro. Zero on the clock. Lincoln Kienholz had just tied it 38-38 with a TD run and a two-point conversion with 1:29 left. Ole Miss got the ball back with a stadium full of people convinced this was going to overtime, then calmly marched down the field like they'd already seen the ending.

The hero was Trinidad Chambliss. Division II transfer from Ferris State a year ago. Now one of the best players in the country in a game where every possession mattered.

Redshirted in 2021. Missed 2022 medical. Made one start in 2023. Then in 2024: 2,925 passing yards, 1,019 rushing yards, Division II national championship at Ferris State. Now he's dropping 4 total TDs on a ranked ACC team in front of a Sunday night audience.

Louisville spent the whole night handing the game back. Fifteen penalties. Including a roughing-the-kicker call on a 57-yard field goal attempt that gave Ole Miss a fresh set of downs when the game was on the line.

@RedditCFB
Roughing the kicker in a 57-yd attempt with the game on the line is insane energy lmao
View tweet

Fifty-seven yards is a long field goal. The correct move on a 57-yard field goal attempt is to stand there and hope. Not to launch yourself into the kicker's plant leg and gift the opponent 15 yards and a first down.

Credit to Kienholz though. First real drive as a Cardinal, tie game, 1:29 on the clock, everyone in Nashville watching — the kid strolled in like it was a preseason walkthrough and punched in the tying score with the two-point conversion to boot. He picked the wrong opponent to have a coming-out party against, but he had one.

Notre Dame lost to Ohio State in the national title game. They've spent an entire offseason getting told they choke on the big stage. Marcus Freeman is not going to kneel on the ball up 28 in Week 1 to make Barstool feel better.

Wisconsin's one shining moment came from a punter. Sean West faced a 4th-and-6 from his own 29 in the second quarter and hit the five, bounced to the two, and side-spun to the sideline before rolling out at the one-foot line. Sixty-nine yards. Coffin corner from a coffin.

Ari Meirov called it the greatest punt he'd ever seen in his life. West responded by hitting a golf chip-shot celebration at midfield.
