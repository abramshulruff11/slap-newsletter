Cleveland, This Is For You: Cavs Dominate Game 7, Punch Ticket to Eastern Conference Finals

Donovan Mitchell reached the Conference Finals for the first time in his career. Ninth season. First Conference Finals.

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2056203247472279828

Mitchell became the first guard since Kobe in 2002 to have at least 25 points, five rebounds and five assists with zero turnovers in a Game 7. Zero turnovers in an elimination game on the road.

The Cavaliers beat the Pistons 125-94 on Sunday night in Game 7. On the road. Detroit was the 1-seed. Cleveland won by 31.

Aaron '2 Gloves' Rai Wins PGA Championship, Ends 107-Year England Drought

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2056144629469167812

First Englishman to win the PGA Championship since 1919. 107 years. England has been waiting since World War I.

The shot of the tournament:

https://twitter.com/AdamSchefter/status/2056138236158755288

Rai eagled nine, sunk a 50-foot putt at 17, held off Rahm, Thomas, Smith, McIlroy and Schauffele. Won the PGA Championship.

Jim Nantz with the call:

https://twitter.com/PFTCommenter/status/2056146283165880780

Rai wears two black gloves for every full swing. Started as a kid in cold English weather. Stuck. His iron covers tell a deeper story — his father cleaned every groove with a pin and baby oil because the clubs meant everything to their family. The iron covers are a reminder to appreciate what you have.

Caitlin Clark Sets WNBA Record with Third Career 20-10 Game, Fever Start 2-0

https://twitter.com/statmuse/status/2056167528741659074

Clark is now the WNBA's all-time leader in 20-point, 10-assist games. Twelve such games in 57 career appearances.

Courtney Vandersloot held the record with 10 across 436 games. Clark has 12 in 57.

Year 3. Record holder. The Fever are 2-0.
