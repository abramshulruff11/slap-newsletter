Kawhi in Limbo: The Trade That Won't Trade

The Kawhi Leonard trade to Toronto was agreed on June 30. It is July 10. Every player involved has physically reported to their new team. The trade is not official.

https://twitter.com/ShamsCharania/status/2075293917118136739

Quick refresher: the NBA launched its investigation in September 2025 after Pablo Torre reported that Leonard's $28 million sponsorship deal with Aspiration was to circumvent the salary cap. This came seven months after Ballmer personally invested in Aspiration and the Clippers announced a $300 million, 23-year sponsorship with the company. Potential penalties include forfeiture of Clippers draft picks, a suspension of Ballmer, various fines and even voiding Leonard's contract with the Clippers.

That last one is why Toronto slammed the brakes.
