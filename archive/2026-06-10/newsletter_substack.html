Now here's the part that turns this from a fun playoff series into a sports movie.

Rod Brind'Amour captained the Hurricanes to the Cup in 2006. Retired. Became an assistant. Head coach in 2018. If Carolina wins three of the next four, he becomes the seventh person in NHL history to win the Cup as a head coach with the same franchise he captained to one.

The last time that happened was 1956. Toe Blake. Montreal.

Seventy years. That's the company Brind'Amour is one Cup away from joining.

Braden Montgomery Walks It Off in His MLB Debut, Two Years After Breaking His Ankle

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2064545150869570008

Montgomery is the fifth player ever to hit a walk-off home run in his MLB debut. Bob Costas was on the call. Of course Bob Costas was on the call.

https://twitter.com/TalkinBaseball_/status/2064541225894478102

The full list:

- Braden Montgomery (2026)
- Carlos Perez (2015)
- Miguel Cabrera (2003)
- Josh Bard (2002)
- Billy Parker (1971)

Cabrera went on to win two MVPs, hit 511 homers, and win a World Series. No pressure, kid.

Here's the part that makes this story unhinged. Exactly two years and one day before this debut, Montgomery was a Texas A&M outfielder who broke his ankle in a Super Regional game. His teammates carried him onto the field on crutches so he could celebrate making the College World Series.

https://twitter.com/JomboyMedia/status/2064388283497345066

https://twitter.com/espn/status/2064559862021824554

Two years and one day. From crutches in a dogpile to a walk-off bomb in The Show. And he did it against the Braves, who entered the game with an MLB-best 45-21 record.
