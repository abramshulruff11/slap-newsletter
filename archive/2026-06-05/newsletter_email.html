HURRICANES STORM BACK

Halfway through the third period of Game 2, Vegas was up 2-0, had won seven straight, and was about to fly home with a stranglehold on the Stanley Cup. Then Carolina scored three goals in five minutes, watched Vegas tie it with a minute left, and won it in overtime on a power play.

Carolina became the fourth team in NHL history, and first since 1944, to win a Stanley Cup Final game after trailing by multiple goals in the final 10 minutes of regulation. The others: Montreal (Game 4 in 1944), Toronto (Game 3 in 1936) and Chicago (Game 3 in 1931).

The last team to do this was using leather helmets. World War II was still going on.

A stress fracture in the first rib is one of the rarest injuries in baseball. It usually shows up in pitchers, not in 6-foot-7 outfielders who hit the ball 450 feet for a living. Judge had been dealing with shoulder soreness all week. Turns out the shoulder wasn't the problem — the rib underneath it was.

Texas became the fifth team in WCWS history to lose their opener in Oklahoma City and go on to lift the trophy. Down to their last out of the tournament more than once. Six straight wins to close it out.

And then there's Teagan Kavan:

"I'm so proud to be a Longhorn." 🥹

Kavan was named the WCWS Most Outstanding Player, becoming the first player to win the award twice in a row. She also became the first pitcher with two shutouts and two saves in one WCWS. She finished the tournament 4-1 with a 1.47 ERA across 33.1 innings. She is a junior. She has one more year of eligibility.

NiJaree Canady deserves her flowers too. Two trips to the WCWS Finals at Texas Tech, the first $1 million NIL deal in college softball, and a four-year career that changed the sport. She just ran into someone writing an all-time legend story on the other side of the diamond.
