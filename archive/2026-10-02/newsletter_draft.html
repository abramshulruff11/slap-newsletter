Braves Take 2-1 Lead Over Phillies

Michael Harris II took a first-inning swing and the series was effectively over. 3-run bomb. Braves up 3-0 before Philadelphia had batted.

Then Ozzie Albies made it 5-0. Then Matt Olson made it 6-1. Joey Votto, on the broadcast, said the Braves would not score again. One batter later they scored again.

Ray Kerr is 32 years old and had never pitched in the postseason before Thursday. He threw the biggest outing of his life in relief, saluted the crowd on his way off, and later dropped F-bombs on the celebration interview.

Chris Sale closed it. The Braves took a 2-1 series lead and will face the defending-champion Dodgers in the NLDS if they close it out. For Philadelphia the window keeps closing on the same aging core.
