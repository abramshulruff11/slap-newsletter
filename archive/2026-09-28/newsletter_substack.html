Broncos Rally From 16 Down, Refs Help

Rams up 26-10 at halftime. Then the officials gave Denver a touchdown and Matthew Stafford gave them two more.

Josh Wallace jumped a Bo Nix route in the fourth quarter and came down with what should have been a game-sealing interception. The refs saw pass interference. Rams win, gone. Broncos life, granted.

Matthew Stafford threw two fourth-quarter interceptions to Denver's Talanoa Hufanga. One went for a touchdown. That pick-six was his 33rd career, breaking a tie with Brett Favre for most in NFL history.

https://x.com/PFF/status/2104410698076041248

Stafford just wrote his name at the top of the wrong leaderboard on national television. Four minutes later he threw another pick to the same guy to seal the loss.

Bo Nix spent the first half looking like a guy who should not be starting NFL football games. Then somebody flipped a switch. He captained the 20-unanswered rally and finished it himself by leaping over the pile on 4th down for the go-ahead touchdown.

Denver is 2-1. The Rams are 1-2 with Aaron Donald retired, Matthew Stafford collecting historically bad records, and Myles Garrett looking at his new locker room the way you look at a rental car you just realized has a check engine light on.

Loop Redeems Himself In Rio

Nine months ago Tyler Loop lined up for a 44-yard field goal in Week 18 that would have won the AFC North and sent Baltimore to the playoffs. He hooked it wide right, gave the division to Pittsburgh, and abruptly ended Baltimore's season. The Ravens missed the playoffs for the first time in years.

Sunday night in Rio, 80,000 Brazilians watching, seven seconds on the clock, 56 yards away. He drilled it. Ravens 34, Cowboys 31.

The setup was Dallas doing Dallas things. Baltimore drove the field in the final minute, and the Cowboys — with timeouts in their pocket — just... didn't use them. Dak knew. Dak knew before anyone else knew.

The controversy came earlier: a phantom PI call on Nate Wiggins that extended the Cowboys' go-ahead TD drive. Minimal contact, maximum consequence.

https://twitter.com/SportsCenter/status/2104355920352342340

Didn't matter. Lamar drove 60+ yards in under a minute, Loop turned the biggest miss of his career into the biggest make, and Dallas flew home from Brazil to think about what they've done.
