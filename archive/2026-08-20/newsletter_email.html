Peyton Watson is a Cleveland Cavalier. He is also $88 million richer. Both of those things happened at roughly the same moment on Wednesday.

Cleveland gets a 23-year-old two-way wing averaging 14.8 points, 4.9 rebounds, 2.1 assists, and 2.0 stocks. The Clippers get Max Strus. Denver gets an unprotected first in 2031 and a second in 2032.

2031. Nikola Jokić will be 36 years old in 2031.

Since winning the championship, Denver has lost Bruce Brown, Jeff Green, Kentavious Caldwell-Pope, and Michael Porter Jr. They've been eliminated in the Conference Semifinals two years running. Now Watson.

Watson's breakout was not subtle. As a starter this season: 17.3 points, 5.3 rebounds, 2.2 assists, 1.0 steals, 1.2 blocks. 49.9% from the field, 43.0% from three. He's the wing everyone in the league has been trying to find. Denver found him, developed him, and shipped him east for a pick that will be handed to someone who hasn't graduated middle school yet.

A.J. Brown Torches His Old Team, Says He Hasn't Talked to His Old QB, Everything Is Fine

The Patriots and Eagles held a joint practice Wednesday. A.J. Brown was there. Jalen Hurts was there. They did not speak.

Brown jogged right past his former Eagles teammates at the beginning of practice and didn't look back. He hasn't spoken to Hurts since the Patriots traded for him in June.

Great teammates. Nothing but love. Definitely not weird that these two set the two highest single-season receiving yard totals in Eagles history together — 1,496 yards in 2022 and 1,456 in 2023 — and now can't work up a "what's up" across the practice field.

When asked about Hurts, Brown scoffed and told Philly reporters to keep it positive. When asked about Drake Maye, his tone shifted immediately. He's thrilled to be catching passes from last season's MVP runner-up.

Both were at DeVonta Smith's wedding in the days following the deal. Same wedding. Still didn't talk. That's an active choice.

Vita Vea Went From "Trade Me" to "$54 Million" in About a Month

The Buccaneers reached an agreement with Vita Vea on a one-year, $30 million extension, ending a standoff that featured a trade request and a "hold-in" at training camp. Vea will also get an additional $6 million for 2026. Two years, $54 million total.

"I just don't see it ending in anything but a trade at this point" — Vea's agent, Collin Roberts, in early August. Days later: $54 million extension.

The 49ers, Cowboys, and half the league had called about Vea. Tampa never blinked. Sometimes the "hold-in" is just the negotiation tactic and the trade request is the polite ultimatum. Both sides knew how this ended.

Azzi Fudd Out for the Season After Knee Surgery

The No. 1 overall pick in the 2026 WNBA Draft is done for the year. Azzi Fudd will undergo right knee surgery and miss the rest of the season — the same right knee that gave her problems throughout her UConn career. The Dallas Wings' playoff hopes were already thin. This makes them significantly thinner.

You watch a player like Fudd shoot the ball and you understand immediately why she went No. 1. You also spend the entire time hoping the body cooperates long enough for the world to see it. Right now, it isn't.
