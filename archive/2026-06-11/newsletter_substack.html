The Largest Comeback in NBA Finals History Just Happened

The New York Knicks were down 29 points. In the NBA Finals. On their home floor. They won by one.

The Knicks came back from 29 down and beat the Spurs 107-106. San Antonio led 81-52 in the third quarter. They scored 30 points in the entire second half.
