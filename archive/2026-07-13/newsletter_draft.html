Sinner Defends Wimbledon

Jannik Sinner just won his second straight Wimbledon.

Sinner beat Alexander Zverev in four sets. Lost the first set in a tiebreak, won the second in a tiebreak, then took sets three and four. Fifth Grand Slam of his career at age 24. Only Nadal and Djokovic among active men have more.

@SportsCenter
Two consecutive years of elation for Jannik Sinner at Wimbledon 🙌

Six weeks ago, Sinner lost in the second round of the French Open to world No. 56 Juan Manuel Cerúndolo. He was up two sets and 5-1 in the third, then won two of the final twenty games. First time an incumbent world No. 1 failed to reach the third round at Roland Garros since Andre Agassi in 2000.

Six weeks later, he's holding the Wimbledon trophy again.

Since his 39th career major win in early 2024, Sinner has gone 62-6 at Grand Slam tournaments. That's a 91.2% win rate. He loses one match at a major about every two months.

Zverev won the French Open five weeks ago — his first Grand Slam at age 29. Now he's back in a final and the trophy slips away again.

Sinner's 100th career major win came in the same match. He's the eighth active player to reach the milestone. Djokovic sits at 409. Wawrinka at 160. Zverev at 131. Sinner got there faster than any of them.

Fever Beat Aces Again

Indiana went to Las Vegas and beat the two-time defending champions 109-75.

Kelsey Mitchell put up 27 with a plus-19. Caitlin Clark, playing through back issues, added 12-7-6 in 24 minutes. Second time in eight days Indiana has beaten Vegas.

The Fever are undefeated against the Aces this season. A'ja Wilson watched Mitchell pull up from anywhere she wanted and there wasn't a defender who could bother her.

The team that ran the WNBA for two straight titles just gave up 109 points at home.

Red Sox Go 9-0 On A Road Trip

Boston finished a nine-game road trip 9-0. Swept the Angels, the White Sox, and the Mets.

The last Red Sox team to reel off nine straight road wins came 49 years ago. That 1977 club featured Jim Rice, Carl Yastrzemski, and Carlton Fisk. All three are in the Hall of Fame now.

Eighteen days ago, Boston had the worst record in the American League. Now they're a half-game out of a wild card spot.

@TalkinBaseball_
Blue Jays have sole possession of last place in the AL East for the first time since April 18th
View tweet

Boston went from last place to a half-game out. The Blue Jays went from a playoff spot to sole possession of last.

Sunday's clincher was the peak. Boston trailed 2-0 in the ninth with one out and a runner on first. Romy Gonzalez hit what should have been a game-ending double play grounder to Francisco Lindor. Lindor muffed it. Devin Williams then walked in the tying runs, Jarren Duran singled home the go-ahead, and Boston won 3-2 in ten.

The Red Sox had been 0-43 when trailing after eight innings this year.

Bonds Nephew Goes To The Giants

The Giants used the 90th overall pick on Peyton Bonds. Yes, that Bonds.

Peyton is the nephew of Barry Bonds, the grandson of Bobby Bonds, and the son of Bobby Bonds Jr. He's a 6-foot-5, 230-pound outfielder out of Rutgers who hit .352 with six homers and 13 steals last season.

Peyton is bigger than his uncle. 6-foot-5, 230 pounds. He also hits right-handed, so the left-handed home run trot Giants fans remember will not be making a comeback.

All-Star Starting Pitchers Named

The Phillies' Cristopher Sánchez gets the ball for the NL. The Blue Jays' Dylan Cease gets it for the AL. Sánchez starts on his home mound at Citizens Bank Park.

Neither name is what casual fans expected. No Skenes. No Wheeler. Just two guys having career years who earned it.

Cease took a no-hitter into the ninth inning last week and fell one out short. Sánchez has 11 wins for a Phillies team that will host the Home Run Derby Monday with Harper, Schwarber, and Walker headlining.
