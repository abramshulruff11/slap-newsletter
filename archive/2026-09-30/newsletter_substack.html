Wild Card Game 1: Four Favorites, Four Openers, Four Statements

Ben Rice went 4-for-5 with two home runs and six RBIs. Cam Schlittler struck out 10. The Yankees hung a 9-spot on Boston and didn't allow a run.

https://twitter.com/StatMuse/status/2105139485944291457

Pete Crow-Armstrong is the presumptive NL MVP and he went 0-for-3 with three strikeouts. The Yankees front office spent all summer figuring out where to bat Rice. Turns out the answer was "in front of Boston's dugout."

Cam Schlittler is from Walpole, Massachusetts. His hometown team keeps showing up and he keeps doing this:

https://twitter.com/StatMuse/status/2105121855652958380

https://twitter.com/CespedesBBQ/status/2105103907315671166

6.1 dominant innings against his childhood tormentors in October. Twice.

Fernando Tatis Jr. hit the first leadoff homer in Padres playoff history on the Cubs' first pitch. Machado followed him back-to-back. Michael King carried a no-hitter into the seventh against the highest-scoring offense in baseball before Nico Hoerner broke it up with an infield single. The Cubs went 5-1 against the Padres this year with 9.2 runs per game. Tuesday night: one infield single, zero runs, three PCA strikeouts.

Don Mattingly went to Jhoan Duran in the seventh. Austin Riley hit a three-run go-ahead homer off him in the eighth.

https://twitter.com/JeffPassan/status/2105036088867995721

Duran went 75 1/3 innings without allowing a home run before Shohei Ohtani broke the streak with a 100 mph splinker.

The White Sox beat Houston 6-3 in a bullpen game behind three scoreless from rookie Hagen Smith. Chicago is looking to win their first playoff series in two decades.
