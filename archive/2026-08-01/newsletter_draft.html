Harrison Bader Rode a Scooter into a Fire Truck at 2 AM

This is not a bit. This is the actual sequence of events.

Harrison Bader — already on the IL for plantar fasciitis — spent Saturday night bar-hopping across San Francisco. Around 1:50 AM in Cow Hollow, he rode a scooter directly into the back of a parked San Francisco Fire Department engine. The wheel of the fire truck then rolled over his already-injured foot.

@BarstoolBigCat
This is taking freak baseball injuries to a whole different level
View tweet

He's out for the season. The Giants are suspending his pay. Buster Posey, in his president of baseball operations voice, said they will not pay Bader until he can "resume performing services for the club." Bader is on a two-year, $20.5 million deal. He signed it in December. He has played in 30 games.

The best part: this is not Bader's first scooter-into-truck incident. In 2014, he was suspended 19 games at the University of Florida for drunkenly crashing a scooter into a parked Ford F-150.

Twelve years later. Different truck. Same guy.
