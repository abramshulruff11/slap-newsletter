GUARDS BALL LIVES

Cleveland was down 2-3. Then they weren't.

Gavin Williams in Game 5:

- 5.2 IP
- 1 hit
- 0 earned runs
- 12 strikeouts

First Guardians pitcher with 12+ SO in a postseason game since Charles Nagy in 1996. His 24 strikeouts in the series are the most in an ALDS since Gerrit Cole put up 25 in 2019. Williams showed up to a winner-take-all game and threw a bullpen session with the White Sox hitters standing in as courtesy.

Then José Ramírez decided the game was over.

Fifth inning, two on, 100-mph fastball from Sean Burke. Ramírez turned it into a go-ahead two-run bomb. Travis Kelce was fired up watching it. The Spanish broadcast call sounded like a man seeing God.

@JomboyMedia
White Sox starting pitcher Sean Burke was on the mound in black Air Force 1s tonight
View tweet

Sean Burke pitched Game 5 of an elimination series in black Air Force 1s. He gave up the Ramírez bomb.

And then the White Sox just... stopped making contact.

Chicago struck out 21 times in Game 5 — a playoff record. They finished the series with 75 strikeouts across five games, the most in a five-game series in MLB history. According to JayCuda, the White Sox struck out 21+ times and lost.

Kyrie's Back, Jaylen's Salty, Ben's... Shooting?

Kyrie Irving played in an NBA game for the first time in 586 days. 17 points, 6-of-13 from the field, in the China Games against Houston. Cooper Flagg started Game 2 next to him. The Mavericks look like a basketball team again.

Meanwhile in Boston, Jaylen Brown returned to TD Garden for the first time since the Sixers trade. He decided to watch the game from the locker room instead of sitting on the Philly bench. Tatum and Brown ignored each other during warmups. The Celtics then put 144 on the Sixers.

Philadelphia 106, Boston 144. Jaylen watched that from a chair in the visitors' tunnel.

And Ben Simmons made his Kings preseason debut after missing all of last season. He hit stepback jumpers. He hit fadeaways. He also went under a screen on Steph Curry, which is one of the four or five worst decisions available to a defender in the NBA.
