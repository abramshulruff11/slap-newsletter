Gerrit Cole: 6 Shutout Innings, 569 Days Later

Gerrit Cole returned from Tommy John surgery Friday night — 569 days after his last MLB start — and threw 6 shutout innings against the Rays. He allowed 2 hits, 3 walks, struck out 2, and hit 98.6 mph. Cole's last MLB game was Game 5 of the 2024 World Series. He retired 12 of 13 batters including a seven-pitch third inning and a four-pitch fourth inning. The Yankees bullpen blew a 1-0 lead in the eighth. Four runs allowed. New York is 0-4 against the Rays this season. Cole looked sharp. The bullpen did not.

Kyle Manzardo Pinch-Hit Homer Breaks Scoreless Tie in 9th

Gavin Williams threw 8 scoreless innings with 11 strikeouts against the Phillies. Cristopher Sanchez matched him with 8 scoreless. Kyle Manzardo hit a pinch-hit homer off closer Jhoan Duran in the ninth. Cleveland won 1-0. First time in franchise history the Guardians won 1-0 on a pinch-hit homer. Williams and Sanchez were the first opposing starters to each pitch at least eight scoreless innings since Miami's Ryan Weathers and Detroit's Reese Olson on May 14, 2024. Manzardo was the first Guardians player to break a tie in the ninth with a pinch-hit homer since Jason Giambi on Sept. 24, 2013. Manzardo said he was "just trying to hit a double." Facing a closer throwing 100 mph with no previous at-bats to test timing. Cade Smith worked a scoreless ninth for his 19th save. Game time: 2 hours, 5 minutes.
